Generative Deep Learning

Authors: David Foster

Generative Deep Learning, Second Edition is a practical guide focused on teaching machine learning engineers and data scientists how to utilize TensorFlow and Keras for creating generative deep learning models. The book provides an accessible introduction to techniques like variational autoencoders (VAEs), generative adversarial networks (GANs), Transformers, normalizing flows, energy-based models, and denoising diffusion models. It begins with foundational deep learning concepts and progresses to advanced architectures, emphasizing efficiency and creativity in model design. Readers will learn to implement models capable of tasks such as altering facial expressions in images and training GANs. The book’s approach is geared towards hands-on experimentation and understanding the underlying principles of these powerful generative models. It’s designed for individuals who want to apply deep learning to creative projects and gain a deeper understanding of the rapidly evolving field of generative AI. The excerpt highlights the book's relevance to the current excitement surrounding generative AI and its potential applications.
