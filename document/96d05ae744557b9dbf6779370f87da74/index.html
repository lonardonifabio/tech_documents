Data Act Amendments

Authors: European Parliament

This document excerpt details the amendments adopted by the European Parliament on 14 March 2023 concerning the Data Act. The Data Act is a proposed regulation aimed at establishing harmonized rules for fair access to and use of data. The amendments were triggered by a referral back to the committee responsible, following Rule 59(4) of the ordinary legislative procedure. The European Parliament's changes are reflected within the document, with new or amended text highlighted in bold italics and deletions indicated by the symbol ▌.
