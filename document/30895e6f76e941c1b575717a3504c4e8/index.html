AI Mitigations Repository - Slide Deck

Authors: Schuett, Campos, U

This slide deck provides an overview of the AI Mitigations Repository, a database and taxonomy of AI risk mitigations. The repository was created through a systematic search of existing frameworks and classifications of AI risks, encompassing both academic and industry perspectives. The core purpose of the repository and this slide deck is to synthesize knowledge on AI risks, identify common themes and gaps in understanding, and offer a holistic view of how AI risk mitigations are currently conceptualized. Specifically, it presents frameworks derived from 13 documents, including a survey of expert opinion on AGI safety and governance (Document 1), a framework bridging AI practices and risk management (Document 6), and emerging processes for frontier AI safety (Document 11). The selection criteria for these documents prioritized their focus on structured taxonomies of AI risk mitigations, their coverage across industry sectors, and the proposition of original frameworks. The slide deck is intended to be a resource for understanding the diverse ways in which AI risk mitigations have been categorized, allowing users to bookmark relevant frameworks for future reference. The document highlights the need for a comprehensive understanding of AI risk mitigations to address the challenges posed by increasingly sophisticated AI systems. The repository and this slide deck represent a valuable contribution to the growing body of research and practice in the field of AI risk management, aiming to facilitate collaboration and knowledge sharing among researchers, practitioners, and policymakers. The slide deck serves as an introductory tool, directing users to the full research report and the interactive repository for deeper exploration.
