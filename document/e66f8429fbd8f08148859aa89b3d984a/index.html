2025 Responsible AI Impact Report

Authors: All Tech Is Human (ATIH)

The 2025 Responsible AI Impact Report, produced by All Tech Is Human (ATIH), addresses the urgent risks, emerging safeguards, and public interest solutions surrounding the rapid development of Artificial Intelligence. This inaugural report provides a critical assessment of the state of Responsible AI (RAI) throughout 2025, focusing on the contributions of civil society organizations to this evolving field. The report defines RAI as AI systems that are well-regulated, guard-railed, governed, assured (through documentation, standardization, and benchmarking), and rigorously assessed and evaluated, including red-teaming exercises. ATIH’s approach is rooted in a human-centered future, prioritizing AI models that minimize harm and are ethically deployed after careful risk consideration and mitigation. The document emphasizes the importance of agency in desired outcomes and rejects a purely deterministic view of technology. Specifically, the report highlights the need for robust oversight and continuous evaluation of AI systems to ensure they align with public interest and ethical considerations. It underscores the role of civil society in shaping the Responsible AI landscape, advocating for a collaborative approach that balances technological advancement with societal well-being. The report’s roadmap outlines ATIH’s strategy for influencing the future of AI’s impact on society, reflecting a commitment to proactive solutions and a deep understanding of the complex interplay between technology and human values. The document references a previous publication, the ‘Responsible Tech Guide (2025),’ further solidifying ATIH’s established position within the Responsible AI movement. The core message is a call for a more conscientious and accountable development and deployment of AI, prioritizing ethical considerations and minimizing potential harms.
