AI-Generated Algorithmic Virality

Authors: Natalia Stanusch, Martin Degeling, Salvatore Romano, Raziye Buse Çetin, Miazia Schüler, Silvia Semenzin

This report, titled "AI-Generated Algorithmic Virality," investigates the rapidly evolving landscape of synthetic AI content and its potential for manipulation and dissemination across social media platforms, primarily focusing on TikTok and Instagram. The research delves into the phenomenon of "AI slop," which refers to the proliferation of low-quality, often nonsensical, AI-generated content designed to mimic genuine information and exploit algorithmic virality. The study examines various aspects of this issue, including the creation and distribution of deepfakes and other synthetic imagery, the automation of AI slop generation through agentic accounts, and the challenges of labeling AI content to mitigate the spread of disinformation. A key component of the investigation is the analysis of visibility of labels on TikTok and Instagram, assessing the effectiveness of current labeling mechanisms. The report outlines a ‘topology’ of AI slop examples, demonstrating how this content is used, including instances of AI slop as satire and its role in generating plausible disinformation. Furthermore, the research explores the use of synthetic storytelling and the historical manipulation of past-futures through AI-generated imagery. The study also analyzes the creation and distribution of generative AI content on both TikTok and Instagram, specifically examining the presence and impact of the ‘AI Label’ on these platforms. A significant portion of the report is dedicated to the taxonomy of agentic AI accounts, identifying patterns in their behavior and contribution to the spread of AI slop. The research concludes with a summary of findings and recommendations. The report is funded by a consortium of foundations including Open Society Foundations, Luminate, Omidyar Network, and Limelight Foundation, highlighting the importance of this research in addressing the growing threat of AI-generated disinformation. The document also includes acknowledgements and contact information for AI Forensics.
