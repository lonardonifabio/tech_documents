Consultation Paper on Guidelines on Artificial Intelligence Risk Management

Authors: Monetary Authority of Singapore (MAS)

This consultation paper, titled ‘Guidelines on Artificial Intelligence Risk Management,’ is being released by the Monetary Authority of Singapore (MAS) to proactively address the growing risks associated with the increasing use of Artificial Intelligence (AI) within the financial sector. The core objective of the document is to establish clear supervisory expectations for financial institutions (FIs) regarding their AI risk management practices. Specifically, the Guidelines outline MAS’ approach to overseeing AI risk, focusing on key areas such as AI risk management systems, policies, procedures, and life cycle controls. The document emphasizes the need for FIs to develop and maintain capabilities and capacity related to AI, encompassing a broad range of AI applications including Generative AI and emerging technologies like AI agents. MAS intends for these Guidelines to be broadly applicable across the financial sector, adapting to the varying sizes and risk profiles of different FIs. However, recognizing the rapid evolution of AI, MAS has explicitly stated its commitment to regularly review and update these Guidelines to ensure they remain relevant and effective. The paper serves as an invitation for feedback from FIs and other stakeholders, encouraging input to refine and improve the proposed framework. The document’s purpose is to foster a robust and responsible approach to AI adoption within Singapore’s financial landscape, mitigating potential risks while allowing for continued innovation.
