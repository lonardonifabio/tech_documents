Europe and the geopolitics of AGI: The need for a preparedness plan

Authors: Maximilian Negele, Daan Juijn, Afek Shamir, David Janku, Bengüsu Özcan, Lisa Soder, Lucia Velasco, Max Reddel, Michiel Bakker, Lorenzo Pacchiardi, Maksym Andriushchenko

This RAND Europe research report, titled "Europe and the geopolitics of AGI: The need for a preparedness plan," addresses the critical need for proactive planning in response to the potential emergence of Artificial General Intelligence (AGI). The document, authored by a multidisciplinary team from leading research institutions including RAND, Stanford, Oxford, MIT, Cambridge, and Tübingen, examines the geopolitical implications of AGI, specifically focusing on Europe's preparedness. It argues that a comprehensive preparedness plan is essential to mitigate risks and capitalize on opportunities presented by AGI, considering factors such as strategic competition, economic disruption, and societal transformation. The research highlights the urgency of developing robust governance frameworks, fostering international cooperation, and investing in research and development to ensure a beneficial and secure future with AGI. The report emphasizes the need for a holistic approach, integrating technological, economic, and political considerations. It details the potential impacts of AGI across various sectors and proposes actionable recommendations for policymakers and stakeholders. The document underscores the importance of proactive engagement and strategic foresight to navigate the complex challenges and opportunities associated with this transformative technology. Furthermore, it reinforces RAND Europe's commitment to rigorous research and analysis, emphasizing quality, objectivity, and ethical behavior in its research process. The research team's affiliations – RAND Center on AI, Security, and Technology, Centre for Future Generations, and other prominent universities and institutes – lend significant credibility to the findings and recommendations. The report's focus on Europe reflects a specific concern about the continent's position within the global AGI landscape and the need for tailored strategies to address unique challenges and opportunities.
