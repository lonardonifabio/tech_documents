Authors: KPMG

KPMG’s insights are derived from direct client engagements and internal work, highlighting the need for a leadership-driven approach. Crucially, the paper stresses the importance of preparing the workforce through targeted training to adapt to the demands of this new AI paradigm. The document underscores that this isn't merely a technological transition but a fundamental shift in leadership and organizational strategy, urging proactive investment and positioning to capitalize on the potential of AI agents. The core message is that organizations that fail to prepare will be left behind as agentic AI reshapes the future of work and value creation.
