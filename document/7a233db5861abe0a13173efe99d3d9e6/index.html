IBM Institute for Business Value | Research Insights: Agentic AI’s Strategic Ascent

Authors: IBM Institute for Business Value

This IBM Institute for Business Value research report, titled "Agentic AI’s Strategic Ascent," examines the evolving landscape of Artificial Intelligence (AI) investment and adoption within businesses. The core finding highlights a significant shift: while the majority of current AI investments are directed towards optimizing existing processes – a trend driven by incremental gains – a substantial 78% of C-Suite executives believe that realizing the full potential of agentic AI necessitates a fundamentally new operating model. The report emphasizes that companies demonstrating proficiency in three key AI adoption areas are 32 times more likely to achieve top-tier business performance, indicating a critical need for strategic, transformative AI initiatives. Looking ahead to 2027, expectations are rising dramatically; nearly twice as many executives anticipate AI agents will be capable of autonomous decision-making within processes and workflows compared to the present. The document underscores the ‘trust deficit’ – the need for AI systems to demonstrate their reasoning and processes to gain acceptance. It advocates for a redesigned operating model as a key differentiator for competitive advantage. The research provides an ‘Action Guide’ to help businesses navigate this transition, focusing on rethinking how work is structured and executed with agentic AI. Ultimately, the report argues that AI transformation isn't simply a prediction, but a pressing deadline demanding a proactive and strategic approach to unlock significant business value. The document provides a framework for leaders to address the challenges and opportunities presented by agentic AI, emphasizing the importance of trust, a new operating model, and a commitment to transformative change.
