Tech Trends 2026

Fourthly, the report tackles "The AI infrastructure reckoning," emphasizing the need to optimize compute strategies in the context of inference economics. Fifthly, it examines "The great rebuild," focusing on architecting tech organizations to be ‘AI-native.’ Finally, the report considers "The AI dilemma," addressing the critical aspects of securing and leveraging AI for cyber defense. The overarching theme is a shift from proof-of-concept projects to operationalizing AI-driven processes, driven by the realization that intelligent operations are key to competitive differentiation. The report anticipates a significant increase in the scaling of AI initiatives across various industries, reflecting a move towards practical application and strategic implementation of AI technologies.
