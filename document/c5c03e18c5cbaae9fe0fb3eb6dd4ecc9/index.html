Responsible Oversight of Artificial Intelligence for Clinical Research Professionals

Authors: Association of Clinical Research Professionals (ACRP)

This publication, produced by the Association of Clinical Research Professionals (ACRP), addresses the critical need for responsible oversight of artificial intelligence (AI) within the context of clinical research. The document comprehensively examines the evolving regulatory landscape surrounding AI, focusing on trends in general oversight, including the implications of the EU Artificial Intelligence Act and various sectoral approaches. It delves into the development of Good Machine Learning Practices and their impact on clinical research workflows. A key area of focus is the research surrounding AI as a clinical product, specifically addressing the regulatory considerations for AI that isn't classified as a medical device, alongside the crucial aspects of de-identification of personal data used for training and validating AI algorithms. The document highlights the importance of human-in-the-loop systems and the intersection of AI with cybersecurity concerns. Furthermore, it explores practical use cases of AI across the clinical research lifecycle, emphasizing ethical responsibility for sharing lessons learned. Finally, the publication considers the impact of AI on the clinical research workforce and concludes with a closing section. Appendices provide further detail on limitations, de-identification processes within the United States, and anonymized examples of AI usage. The overall aim is to equip clinical research professionals with the knowledge and understanding necessary to navigate the complexities and ensure the ethical and responsible implementation of AI technologies.
