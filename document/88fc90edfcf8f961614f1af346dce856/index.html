The Geopolitics of AI: Decoding the New Global Operating System

Authors: JPMorganChase Center for Geopolitics

This report, published by the JPMorganChase Center for Geopolitics, analyzes the evolving geopolitical landscape shaped by the rapid advancement and deployment of Artificial Intelligence (AI). The core argument centers around the diverging strategies of the United States and China, with Beijing prioritizing state-led self-reliance, cost-effective open-source development, and export, while Washington focuses on private-sector innovation, infrastructure investment, and defense integration. This fundamental difference represents a critical geopolitical fault line, influencing national choices regarding AI development. The document highlights the increasing trend of tech sovereignty, where numerous nations and local authorities are asserting control over AI infrastructure, talent, and governance, leading to the fragmentation of the AI field and the imposition of distinct regulatory frameworks. Furthermore, the report identifies energy and hardware – specifically semiconductors, critical minerals, and electricity capacity – as emerging chokepoints determining a nation's ability to scale AI technologies. The document details the strategic repositioning of capital, noting the rise of Middle Eastern sovereign wealth funds utilizing energy resources to become significant players in AI infrastructure development. Finally, it examines the transformative impact of AI on defense and deterrence, asserting that military organizations capable of rapidly integrating AI will gain decisive advantages through technologies like swarming drones and AI-driven decision-making systems. The executive summary reinforces these key findings, emphasizing the urgent need to understand and navigate the complex geopolitical implications of AI’s global spread. The report’s focus is on understanding how AI is reshaping international relations and strategic competition, providing insights for clients navigating these global challenges. The document anticipates a fragmented AI ecosystem, driven by differing national approaches and regulatory environments, and underscores the importance of considering energy and hardware constraints in AI development strategies. It’s a forward-looking analysis of a technology with profound geopolitical consequences.
