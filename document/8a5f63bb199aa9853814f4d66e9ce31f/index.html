This document, titled "AI Regulation: Global Guide," provides a comprehensive overview of the evolving regulatory landscape surrounding Artificial Intelligence across twelve key jurisdictions. It addresses the increasing complexity arising from the implementation of AI-specific laws like the EU AI Act, alongside proposed new legislation, and the continued application of existing laws. The guide’s primary objective is to equip readers with the necessary knowledge to navigate the regulatory challenges associated with their AI projects, considering factors such as AI-specific laws, data protection regulations, HR and employment considerations, discrimination concerns, medical devices and healthcare regulations, and financial services guidance. The document specifically examines the regulatory approaches in Australia, Canada, China, France, Germany, Hong Kong, Italy, the Netherlands, Singapore, South Africa, the United Kingdom, and the United States. Each jurisdiction's approach is briefly outlined, highlighting key aspects such as the Australian government’s AI Action Plan (which focuses on creating an environment for AI development and adoption without introducing comprehensive legislation), the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner’s (OAIC) guidance on applying privacy laws to AI, and the general absence of standalone AI legislation in many of the covered countries. The guide’s structure is organized by jurisdiction, offering a detailed look at the specific regulations and guidelines impacting AI development and deployment. It serves as a valuable resource for organizations seeking to understand and comply with the diverse and rapidly changing legal frameworks governing AI globally. The document’s scope extends beyond simply listing laws; it aims to provide actionable insights for navigating the complexities of AI regulation, supporting responsible AI innovation and adoption.
