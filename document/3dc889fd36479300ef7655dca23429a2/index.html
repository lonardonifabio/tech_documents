The ROI of AI in Financial Services

Authors: Google Cloud, National Research Group

This report investigates the return on investment (ROI) of Artificial Intelligence (AI) within the financial services industry, specifically examining how AI agents are contributing to growth, cost reduction, and risk mitigation. The research is based on a survey of 556 leaders from global financial services companies, encompassing roles such as CEOs, CIOs, CFOs, CISOs, and various other leadership positions across diverse geographic regions including North America, Europe, Asia-Pacific, and Latin America. The survey, conducted between April 18th and June 3rd, 2025, utilized a sample size of 150 CEOs, 215 CFOs, 109 CISOs, and so on, with over 100 full-time employees across companies generating over $10 million in annual revenue. The findings reveal that over half of executives are already utilizing AI agents, with applications ranging from simple, single-task agents to complex, multi-agent systems capable of autonomous action under user control. Despite this adoption, many organizations remain in the initial stages of agentic maturity. The report highlights a significant shift, indicating a move from planning to active implementation of AI within financial institutions. Historically, the implementation of agentic systems was hampered by the need for intricate, rule-based programming or extensive machine learning model training. However, the advent of Generative AI (Gen AI) is dramatically simplifying this process, enabling more accessible and efficient deployment of AI agents. The research underscores the potential of AI to drive revenue growth, optimize operational costs, and strengthen risk management strategies within the financial sector. The data is presented as aggregate figures for the MEA region, US, Canada, UK, Germany, France, Nordics, Japan, India, Australia, South Korea, Taiwan, Mexico, Brazil, Indonesia, Saudi Arabia, Turkey, Kuwait, South Africa, and UAE.
