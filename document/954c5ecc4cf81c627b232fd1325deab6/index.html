Strengthening Board Oversight of AI – The A.I. GUARD Framework

Authors: Mahi Dontamsetti, Jim Routh

This white paper, titled "Strengthening Board Oversight of AI – The A.I. GUARD Framework," published in October 2025 by Mahi Dontamsetti and Jim Routh, addresses the increasing importance of board oversight in the context of rapidly evolving Artificial Intelligence (AI) technologies. The document recognizes that for boards, especially those within Fortune 1000 companies, the core question isn't simply whether AI oversight is needed, but rather how to effectively manage AI initiatives across the entire organization to maximize business value. It introduces the A.I. GUARD Framework, a strategic governance model designed to equip boards with the tools and knowledge to fulfill their fiduciary responsibilities regarding AI. The framework is built upon seven key steps that boards can implement to ensure responsible AI development, deployment, and ongoing monitoring. These steps encompass not only technical considerations but also crucial aspects like regulatory compliance and maintaining public trust. The authors draw upon over 40 years of combined experience in board interactions to provide a practical and actionable guide. The paper highlights the significant risks associated with AI alongside its potential benefits, emphasizing the need for proactive governance. Ultimately, the A.I. GUARD Framework aims to provide boards with a structured approach to navigate the complexities of AI, fostering innovation while mitigating potential harms and upholding ethical standards.
