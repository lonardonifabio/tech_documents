LLM use in the workplace: Qualitative interviews with LLM power users and early adopters

Authors: Jamie Elsey, Willem Sleegers, David Moss

This research report, published in June 2025, presents the findings of qualitative interviews conducted with LLM power users and early adopters. The study, undertaken by Rethink Priorities, aims to understand how Large Language Models (LLMs) are currently being utilized within various workplace settings. The research focuses on gathering insights directly from individuals who are actively leveraging LLMs, providing a grounded perspective on the practical applications, challenges, and potential impacts of this technology. Rethink Priorities, a research and implementation group, developed this report as part of its mission to identify and address pressing global issues. The team, led by Jamie Elsey, Willem Sleegers, and David Moss, collaborated closely with foundations and impact-focused non-profits, reflecting the organization's commitment to collaborative problem-solving. The interviews explored a range of use cases, from content creation and knowledge management to automation and strategic decision-making. Key themes emerging from the interviews likely include the specific tasks where LLMs are proving most effective, the skills and training required to utilize them effectively, the ethical considerations surrounding their deployment, and the potential for disruption across different industries. The report’s findings are intended to inform further research and strategic interventions aimed at maximizing the benefits of LLMs while mitigating potential risks. The research is funded by Open Philanthropy (Open Philanthropy Project LLC), and the views expressed are not necessarily endorsed by the funding organization.
