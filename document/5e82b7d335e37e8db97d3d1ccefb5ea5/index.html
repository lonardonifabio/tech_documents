Guide on AI and Human Rights – Methodology and Acknowledgements

Authors: ECNL, DIHR

This document serves as a methodological overview and acknowledgements section for a guide concerning the intersection of Artificial Intelligence and Human Rights. The guide was developed with funding from the European Artificial Intelligence & Society Fund and is released under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International license. It highlights the significant contributions of Algorithm Watch and Michele Loi, particularly regarding the questionnaire template. The development process involved extensive input from a diverse range of stakeholders, including legal experts (Gianclaudio Malgieri from Leiden University), human rights organizations (Isabelle Schipper from the Netherlands Institute for Human Rights, Iris Muis and Julia Straatman from Utrecht Data School), consulting firms (Patricia Shaw from Beyond Reach Consulting Limited), and numerous civil society colleagues. The methodology is rooted in materials and experiences from ECNL and DIHR, supplemented by insights gained from three workshops held in Berlin, Copenhagen, and Brussels. Furthermore, the guide leverages publicly available information, including academic literature and existing AI impact assessment methodologies, such as the Fundamental Rights and Algorithms Impact Assessment utilized by the Government of Netherlands. The Excel FRIA Template, specifically its context analysis section, was developed through a careful review of similar questionnaires within the field of AI impact assessment. This approach demonstrates a commitment to a robust and multi-faceted understanding of the potential impacts of AI on human rights, incorporating both theoretical frameworks and practical experiences. The document emphasizes a collaborative approach, reflecting the importance of diverse perspectives in addressing the complex challenges posed by AI technology. It’s a foundational element of a larger guide, providing the underlying rationale and acknowledging the collective effort that shaped its creation.
