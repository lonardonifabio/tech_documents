Authors: Sean Musch, Michael Borrelli, Charles Kerrigan, Alisar Mustafa, Eevamaija Virtanen, Tulsi Soni, Diane Tea EU

This document, titled "Providers of General - Purpose AI Models : A Breakdown", analyzes the rapid expansion of generative AI models between January 2023 and April 2025, focusing on trends across text, image, and audio modalities. The report highlights a dramatic increase in the number of models, particularly in text-to-text systems, which surged from six to 616, fueled by foundation model scaling, open-weight releases, and the democratization of fine-tuning. Text-to-image models saw a significant rise from seven to 187, reflecting the maturing diffusion model ecosystem and commercial applications in creative and marketing fields. Audio-to-text systems experienced more modest growth, indicating a functional maturity in transcription and speech-to-text technologies. Notably, text-to-audio models emerged later, suggesting the initial stages of growth as generative speech, music, and multimodal interfaces gain prominence. The document emphasizes that this proliferation of models presents both significant opportunities and a degree of fragmentation within the AI landscape. The analysis is presented by the OECD.AI team and anticipates publication in December 2025, reflecting the accelerating pace of innovation in generative AI. The report's core argument centers on the unprecedented acceleration in language-based AI innovation and the evolving competitive dynamics across different AI modalities. It provides a snapshot of the AI landscape as of October 2025, offering valuable insights into the trajectory of generative AI development.
