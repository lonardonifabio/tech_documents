EU AI Ecosystem and AI Regulatory Sandboxes: Synergies between AIRS Services and AI Innovation Ecosystem Actors

Authors: Unknown, Unknown

This report, titled ‘EU AI Ecosystem and AI Regulatory Sandboxes: Synergies between AIRS Services and AI Innovation Ecosystem Actors,’ explores the potential synergies between the European AI Regulatory Sandboxes (AIRS) initiative and the broader AI innovation ecosystem. Released as a preliminary assessment in July 2025, the document focuses on aligning regulatory sandboxes with ecosystem offerings, utilizing the AIRS Operational Pipeline, and understanding the technological maturity levels of AI technologies relevant to sandboxes. The report begins with an overview of the AI Regulatory Sandboxes, detailing the building blocks and menu associated with them. It then examines the alignment of these sandboxes with the existing AI Innovation Ecosystem, mapping AIRS Building Blocks and ecosystem services, and analyzing their positioning within the AIRS Operational Pipeline. A key section investigates the Technological Readiness Level (TRL) scale for AI technologies, exploring its implications for the AIRS Pipeline and considering approaches beyond the TRL scale. The report further delves into operational challenges and provides recommendations. An annex provides a comprehensive mapping of key actors within the European AI ecosystem, including European Digital Innovation Hubs (EDIHs), the EuroHPC Joint Undertaking, AI Factories, Testing and Experimentation Facilities (TEFs), the AI on Demand Platform, Data Spaces, and initiatives under the AI Continent Plan. The document emphasizes the importance of establishing communication channels across the entire ecosystem. Importantly, it is noted as a ‘living document’ and subject to updates based on developments within the EUSAiR program and feedback. The project is part of the Digital Europe Programme, commencing in December 2024 and lasting 24 months.
