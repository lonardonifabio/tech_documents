2025 Peregrine Report: 208 Expert Proposals for Reducing AI Risk

Authors: Maximilian Schons, Samuel Härgestam, Gavin Leech, Raymund Bermejo

The 2025 Peregrine Report is a comprehensive collection of 208 expert proposals aimed at mitigating the risks associated with transformative Artificial Intelligence (AI) development. Developed in collaboration with Halcyon Futures, the report addresses a critical gap in existing resources – the lack of a readily available, actionable list of interventions suitable for a rapidly approaching, high-impact AI future. The core premise of the report centers around a hypothetical scenario where unlimited resources and global talent are available, prompting the authors to identify the most impactful interventions capable of significantly improving humanity’s trajectory concerning AI over the next two years. The goal is to foster a sense of optimism regarding AI’s potential, suggesting that proactive measures are on track to ensure a positive outcome. The report’s methodology involved 48 in-depth interviews with key personnel at leading AI organizations including OpenAI, Anthropic, Google DeepMind, Mila, and AMD, as well as representatives from the EU AI Office and AI Safety Ins. It contextualizes its findings by referencing and complementing other relevant research, specifically the IAPS AI Reliability Survey (2025) and the Risk & Reward, 2024 AI Assurance Technology Market Report (Juniper Ventures, 2024). The report’s focus on a near-term, high-impact scenario underscores the urgency of addressing AI risk mitigation strategies. It represents a strategic effort to consolidate diverse expert opinions into a single, actionable resource, providing a valuable framework for investment and research prioritization within the rapidly evolving field of AI safety and governance. The report's aim is to provide a roadmap for navigating the complexities of AI development and deployment, ensuring a future where transformative AI benefits humanity rather than posing existential threats.
