DeepLearning101

Authors: NguyễnVũHưng, VuHungNguyen

DeepLearning101 is a comprehensive guide to deep learning theory and practice, authored by NguyễnVũHưng and VuHungNguyen. Published on October 15th, 2025, and licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CCBY4.0) license, this book aims to bridge the gap between basic and advanced deep learning resources. It's designed for students, researchers, and practitioners seeking a solid foundation in this transformative field. The author initially created the book as study notes for themselves, recognizing a lack of resources that adequately catered to their background. The book provides a ‘compressed’ version of existing deep learning books, focusing on the most important concepts and mathematical formulas in a structured and coherent manner. It assumes a fair understanding of mathematics, algorithms, and programming, with supplemental learning provided throughout the book. The early chapters cover mathematical foundations, and examples are used to reinforce concepts. The target audience includes individuals with a technical background looking to gain a comprehensive understanding of deep learning principles. The book's structure is intended to be accessible while still providing the necessary depth for serious learners. The license allows for redistribution and adaptation of the work, provided appropriate attribution is given to the original authors. The book’s creation reflects a desire to offer a focused and practical resource for those navigating the complexities of deep learning.
