Guidelines on Scope of Obligations for GPAI Models

Authors: European Commission

This document outlines the European Commission's guidelines regarding the scope of obligations associated with General Purpose AI (GPAI) models. Released in August 2025, the guidelines aim to establish a framework for managing the risks and responsibilities related to the development and deployment of these models. The core focus is on 'general-purpose AI models,' defined as those capable of performing a wide range of tasks. The document meticulously details the criteria for classifying a model as general-purpose, emphasizing the importance of understanding the model's lifecycle – from development to deployment and ongoing monitoring. A key element is the identification of models posing 'systemic risk,' which necessitates specific notification procedures. The guidelines provide a classification system for determining systemic risk, and mandates notification requirements for models identified as such. The document's purpose is to promote responsible AI development and deployment within the European Union, addressing potential harms and ensuring accountability.
