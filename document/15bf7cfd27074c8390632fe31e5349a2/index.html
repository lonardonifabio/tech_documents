CCAPAC Report: AI and Cybersecurity

Authors: Coalition for Cybersecurity in Asia-Pacific (CCAPAC)

The CCAPAC Report: AI and Cybersecurity, published in 2023, provides an overview of the intersection between Artificial Intelligence (AI) and cybersecurity within the Asia-Pacific region. The report, produced by the Coalition for Cybersecurity in Asia-Pacific (CCAPAC), highlights the organization's mission to positively influence the cybersecurity landscape through policy analysis, stakeholder engagement, and capacity building initiatives. The document begins with an introduction setting the stage for the report's focus. It then delves into the current AI landscape, detailing the various technologies and their applications across diverse sectors. Specifically, it examines AI's presence and impact within key industries. A significant portion of the report is dedicated to analyzing the specific cybersecurity risks associated with AI. This includes a thorough examination of data security vulnerabilities, focusing on how AI systems can be exploited to compromise sensitive information. Furthermore, the report investigates model security risks, addressing concerns about the integrity and robustness of AI models themselves. This involves scrutinizing potential weaknesses that could be leveraged by malicious actors. The document also considers infrastructure risks, recognizing the vulnerabilities inherent in the hardware and software systems that support AI operations. Finally, it explores application risks, acknowledging the security challenges posed by the deployment and use of AI-powered applications. The report’s aim is to provide a comprehensive understanding of the evolving relationship between AI and cybersecurity, offering insights and recommendations for stakeholders seeking to mitigate risks and promote responsible AI adoption within the Asia-Pacific region. The Coalition for Cybersecurity in Asia-Pacific (CCAPAC) is actively involved in shaping this environment through collaborative efforts and knowledge sharing. The report’s structure, with chapters dedicated to specific risk categories, demonstrates a systematic approach to understanding and addressing the complex challenges presented by AI’s increasing role in cybersecurity.
