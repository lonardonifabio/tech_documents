State of AI Safety in China (2025)

Authors: Gabriel Wagner, Jason Zhou, Kwan Yee Ng, Brian Tse

This report, titled "State of AI Safety in China (2025)", provides an analysis of the evolving landscape of AI safety governance within China. Authored by Gabriel Wagner, Jason Zhou, Kwan Yee Ng, and Brian Tse, the document examines both domestic and international approaches to AI safety. The report’s structure is divided into two main sections: ‘Domestic Governance’ and ‘International Governance’. The ‘Domestic Governance’ section delves into the national guidance, legislation, and regulatory frameworks established within China to manage AI risks. It specifically addresses overarching national guidance, relevant legislation and regulations, and the development of standards for AI development and deployment. Furthermore, the report investigates the role of government-backed AI safety and governance institutions within China. The ‘International Governance’ section analyzes China’s engagement in global AI governance efforts. This includes examining China’s actions at the United Nations, bilateral engagements with other nations, and multilateral collaborations. The report’s focus is on understanding China’s strategic positioning and influence within the broader international discussions surrounding AI safety and responsible AI development. The document was published by Concordia AI in July 2025 and provides a snapshot of the situation at that time, offering insights into the complex and rapidly changing field of AI safety, particularly concerning China’s approach.
