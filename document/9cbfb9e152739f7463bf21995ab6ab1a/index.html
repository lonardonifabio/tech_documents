MDR versus EU AI Act: A Mapping Exercise

Authors: Sean Musch, Michael Borrelli, Charles Kerrigan, Teddy Ramanakasina, Harvey Castro, Joris Krijger

This report, produced by AI & Partners, provides a mapping exercise comparing the Medical Device Regulation (MDR) with the proposed EU AI Act. AI & Partners, a firm dedicated to defending digital rights and promoting responsible AI development, outlines their approach to navigating the complex regulatory landscape surrounding artificial intelligence. The organization’s mission centers on empowering organizations to build and deploy AI systems that are both trustworthy and sustainable, mitigating the risks associated with uncontrolled AI adoption. Specifically, the report addresses the potential for confusion, risk, and reputational damage stemming from unknown AI usage. AI & Partners utilizes a multi-faceted strategy, including direct technical support, policy engagement, global advocacy, grassroots professional services, and regulatory interventions, alongside participation in industry groups like AI Commons. The core of their work involves equipping clients with tools to identify, monitor, and govern all AI systems, ensuring responsible AI usage and fostering trust. The document highlights the importance of proactive governance to avoid negative consequences and emphasizes AI & Partners’ commitment to supporting organizations in building AI systems they can confidently stand behind. The report’s purpose is to guide organizations through the evolving regulatory environment and to provide a framework for responsible AI implementation.
