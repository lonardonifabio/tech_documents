Australia – Our Gen AI Transition

Authors: Gmyrek, Berg, Bescond

This document, titled "Australia – Our Gen AI Transition," analyzes the potential impact of Generative AI (Gen AI) technologies on the Australian workforce, specifically focusing on occupations predominantly held by women. The core argument centers around the need for increased investment in Gen AI technologies and related skills to address the challenges faced by already strained workforces. The report highlights opportunities for improved job quality and productivity, particularly within female-dominated sectors like care economies (e.g., healthcare, childcare). It leverages data from the Skills Tracker project (JSA analysis of PLIDA data, 2025) and DOM 2022-23 data to assess the ‘exposure’ of various occupations to Gen AI, categorized as either ‘Augmentation’ or ‘Automation’ levels. Table 1 details the exposure levels for common occupations for women, including Sales Assistants (General), Registered Nurses, General Clerks, Aged and Disabled Carers, Child Carers, Receptionists, Primary School Teachers, and Office Managers. The analysis reveals that while some roles, like Office Managers, exhibit higher potential for automation, others, such as Sales Assistants and Registered Nurses, are more likely to be augmented by Gen AI. The report emphasizes the importance of informed adoption of Gen AI to meet increasing demand and address unmet needs, particularly within sectors where women are heavily represented. Furthermore, Table 2 expands the analysis to younger workers (15-24 years), providing a comparative view of Gen AI exposure across occupations within this demographic. The document’s findings suggest a strategic investment in Gen AI skills and technology is crucial for Australia to effectively navigate the transition and maximize the benefits of these technologies while mitigating potential negative impacts on the workforce, especially within sectors reliant on female labor. The data presented aims to inform policy decisions and workforce development strategies related to Gen AI adoption.
