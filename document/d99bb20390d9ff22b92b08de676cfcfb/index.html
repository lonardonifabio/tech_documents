Artificial intelligence (AI) and human rights: Using AI as a weapon of repression and its impact on human rights

Authors: H. Akin ÜNVER

This in-depth analysis (IDA), produced by the European Parliament’s Directorate General for External Policies Policy Department EP/EXPO/DROI/FWC/2019 -01/Lot6/1/C/30, investigates the concerning trend of utilizing artificial intelligence (AI) as a tool for repression and its detrimental effects on human rights. The document, requested by the DROI subcommittee, focuses on ‘algorithmic authoritarianism,’ examining the key actors, specific cases, and techniques employed in this context. It delves into the legal, regulatory, and diplomatic frameworks surrounding AI-based biases and deliberate misuse of AI in decision-making processes. The analysis highlights the increasing relevance of this issue for the European Parliament, aligning with its core mission of protecting human rights within the rapidly evolving landscape of digital transformation. The IDA specifically addresses the global patterns and quickly advancing technologies contributing to algorithmic authoritarianism. It seeks to foster a broader comprehension of the intricate policy, regulatory, and diplomatic hurdles at the convergence of technology, democratic governance, and human rights considerations. The document’s scope includes an examination of how AI is being leveraged to exacerbate repressive agendas, particularly in the context of digital transformation. Ultimately, the analysis intends to provide actionable insights for policymakers and stakeholders navigating the complex challenges posed by the intersection of AI and human rights, emphasizing the need for proactive measures to mitigate the risks of algorithmic bias and misuse.
