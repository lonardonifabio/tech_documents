AI FOR EVERYONE? Critical Perspectives

Authors: Pieter Verdegem

This book, "AI FOR EVERYONE? Critical Perspectives," addresses the growing trend of technological determinism and solutionism surrounding Artificial Intelligence. It argues that a critical examination of AI's implications is urgently needed, particularly concerning power dynamics and potential inequalities. The book’s core mission is to provoke debate around fundamental questions about AI’s nature, its impact, and the conditions necessary for it to genuinely benefit everyone. Structured into three parts, the book begins with "AI: Humans vs. Machines," which critically analyzes the human-machine dichotomy. The second part, "Discourses and Myths about AI," delves into the underlying metaphors and policies shaping the perception of AI, aiming to establish normative standards for desirable AI. Finally, "AI Power and Inequalities" investigates the practical challenges arising from AI implementation. The book assembles a diverse group of scholars from various disciplines and geographical locations, offering a crucial perspective on a concept frequently overhyped. Pieter Verdegem, the Senior Lecturer in Media Theory at Westminster School of Media and Communication, leads this critical investigation, highlighting the need for a more nuanced and equitable approach to AI development and deployment. The work emphasizes the importance of considering power structures and potential negative consequences, advocating for a thoughtful and responsible approach to this transformative technology. It’s a call to action, urging readers to move beyond simplistic assumptions and engage in a rigorous assessment of AI’s role in shaping our future.
