Global Catastrophic Risks 2026

Authors: Fatima Denton, Johan Rockström, Manjana Milkoreit, David Obura, Wilfred Wan

The "Global Catastrophic Risks 2026" report, published by the Global Challenges Foundation in 2026, examines potential global catastrophic risks, focusing on Earth system stability and the implications of various escalating threats. The report identifies four key risks, each analyzed through the lens of expert perspectives from leading organizations and academic institutions. Risk 1 investigates the concept of Earth system tipping points and the associated responsibility for mitigating potential disasters, featuring contributions from the UNU-INRA and the Potsdam Institute for Climate Impact Research. Risk 2 explores the challenges of global governance concerning nature and biodiversity, drawing on the expertise of IPBES and CORDIO East Africa. Risk 3 addresses the escalating risks posed by Weapons of Mass Destruction and multi-domain escalation, informed by the Stockholm International Peace Research Institute's (SIPRI) Weapons of Mass Destruction Programme. Finally, Risk 4 delves into the increasingly concerning role of Artificial Intelligence (AI) within military decision-making processes, acknowledging the potential for amplified risks. The report emphasizes the need for imaginative thinking and proactive responsibility in addressing these complex and interconnected challenges. It highlights the importance of collaboration across disciplines and international organizations to prevent potentially irreversible consequences. The document is a foundational report outlining critical global risks, utilizing a multidisciplinary approach to assess and understand the potential for catastrophic events. The report’s creation involved a collaborative effort, bringing together researchers, directors, and experts from diverse fields, reflecting a holistic approach to global risk assessment. The inclusion of figures like Fatima Denton and Johan Rockström, prominent voices in their respective fields, lends significant weight to the report’s findings. The report’s focus on AI in military decision-making is particularly relevant given the rapid advancements in the technology and its potential impact on global security.
