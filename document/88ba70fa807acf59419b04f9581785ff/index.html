AI Agents As Employees

Authors: Sandy Carter, Nikhil Kassetty, Manas Talukdar, Yoshita Sharma, Olga Magnusson, Neeraj Madan, Priyanka Shrivastava, Bill Lesieur, Sangeetha Rajkumar

This research paper, "AI Agents As Employees," explores the emerging trend of AI agents replacing traditional AI tools within organizations. It highlights the shift from AI primarily serving as a supportive tool for human tasks – through methods like prediction, classification, and optimization – to AI agents that actively drive action and make autonomous decisions. The document focuses on the rise of large language models (LLMs) like GPT-4 and Claude, which are generative and reactive, responding to prompts and providing outputs. However, the core argument is that a new paradigm is emerging with AI agents, which are proactive and autonomous entities capable of initiating tasks, evaluating information, and executing decisions based on pre-defined objectives. The paper anticipates that this evolution will significantly impact various industries, including customer service, logistics, financial analysis, and product design, by enhancing efficiency and unlocking new business models. The research delves into the capabilities of these AI agents, emphasizing their ability to move beyond simple response generation and towards genuine autonomous operation within the enterprise. The document is targeted towards business readers and utilizes tags such as ‘Digital Workforce,’ ‘Human–AI Collaboration,’ and ‘Autonomous AI’ to categorize its focus.
