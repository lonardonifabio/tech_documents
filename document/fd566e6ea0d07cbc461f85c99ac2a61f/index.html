IITill EGYPT Artificial Intelligence Readiness Assessment Report 2025

Authors: UNESCO Office for Egypt and Sudan, Dr. Nuria Sanz, Ms. Areej Atalla, H.E. Dr. Amr Talaat, Dr. Hoda Baraka, Ms. Yomna Omran

This document is the Artificial Intelligence Readiness Assessment Report 2025, produced by the UNESCO Office for Egypt and Sudan in collaboration with the Government of Egypt. The report’s primary objective is to evaluate Egypt’s preparedness for the ethical and responsible application of Artificial Intelligence. The assessment was spearheaded by the Social and Human Sciences Unit, under the direction of Ms. Areej Atalla, and guided by Dr. Nuria Sanz, Director of the Office. A key element of the report’s development involved the Government of Egypt’s proactive engagement, specifically translating the Readiness Assessment Methodology questionnaire into Arabic to broaden its applicability across Arabic-speaking nations in the region. Significant contributions were made by H.E. Dr. Amr Talaat, Minister of Communications and Information Technology, and his team, including Dr. Hoda Baraka, ICT Minister’s Advisor on Talent Development and AI National Lead, and Ms. Yomna Omran, AI Governance and Coordination Lead. The Ministry of Communications and Information Technology (MCIT) played a crucial role as the coordinating entity, organizing national consultations, gathering expert opinions, and supplying vital data. The report’s creation was entirely dependent on the MCIT’s sustained support and leadership. The document highlights Egypt’s commitment to responsible AI development and the collaborative efforts undertaken to assess and improve the nation’s readiness within this rapidly evolving technological landscape. The translation of the questionnaire into Arabic underscores a strategic approach to knowledge dissemination and application, aiming to foster AI development across a wider geographical area. The involvement of key figures within the Egyptian Ministry of Communications and Information Technology demonstrates a concerted national effort to align AI strategies with ethical considerations and broader developmental goals. Ultimately, this report serves as a foundational document for future AI initiatives within Egypt, providing a critical self-assessment and a roadmap for continued progress.
