The dawn of agentic AI: Orchestration, governance, and best practices

Authors: Deloitte AI Institute

This document, released in October 2025, introduces the Deloitte AI Institute's focus on ‘the dawn of agentic AI,’ emphasizing orchestration, governance, and best practices within the rapidly evolving artificial intelligence landscape. The Institute’s core mission is to assist organizations in navigating this complex ecosystem, providing insights and guidance across various stages of the AI journey. It specifically targets decision-makers – from board members and C-Suite leaders to data scientists – offering a collaborative approach that integrates with academic research, startups, and established AI product leaders. The Institute’s activities encompass discussions around AI risks, policies, ethics, the future of work, talent development, and practical AI use cases. A key element of the Institute’s strategy is to foster human-machine collaboration, aligning with the ‘Age of With’ concept. By combining Deloitte’s extensive AI expertise with the Institute’s network and research, the organization aims to deliver actionable perspectives, enabling organizations to make informed AI decisions and ultimately achieve success. The document highlights the importance of a holistic approach, considering not just the technological aspects of AI, but also the broader societal and ethical implications. The Institute’s work is geared towards supporting organizations in all phases of their AI adoption, from initial strategy development to the practical implementation and ongoing management of AI solutions. Ultimately, the goal is to transform AI from a potentially daunting challenge into a strategic asset, driving innovation and competitive advantage.
