AI’s Next Frontier for Financial Services Leaders

Authors: From discovery

This report examines the rapidly evolving landscape of artificial intelligence (AI) adoption within the financial services industry, specifically focusing on the shift from generative AI to agentic AI. Initially, financial institutions experimented with generative AI tools primarily for prompt-based assistance, but the sector is now experiencing a significant acceleration in the adoption of agentic AI – systems designed to autonomously plan, decide, and execute multi-step workflows. Over half of financial services executives are currently utilizing AI agents to varying degrees, indicating a substantial change in the industry's approach to AI implementation. The rise of agentic AI represents a fundamental transformation, demanding a strategic vision alongside disciplined change management to successfully navigate this transition. This transition unlocks unprecedented value for both financial institutions and their customers. The report analyzes the key growth drivers behind this adoption, including investments in generative AI for operational efficiency and customer interaction innovation. It further explores the emerging economies that are being shaped by AI's evolution from simple assistance to autonomous action. The document highlights the need for a proactive approach to leverage AI's full potential and emphasizes the importance of strategic planning and effective change management to capitalize on this new frontier in financial services. The core message is that the industry is moving beyond simply 'helping' with AI, towards systems that can actively 'do' things autonomously, fundamentally altering the way financial services operate and deliver value.
