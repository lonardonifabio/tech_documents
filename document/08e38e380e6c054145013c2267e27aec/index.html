Microsoft Digital Defense Report 2025

Authors: Amy Hogan-Burney, Igor Tsyganskiy

The Microsoft Digital Defense Report 2025, a Threat Intelligence report, provides a comprehensive analysis of the evolving cybersecurity landscape and offers strategic recommendations for organizations to enhance their defenses. This report focuses heavily on the tactics employed by threat actors, particularly in 2025, and highlights the critical need for proactive security measures. It details the 'threat landscape,' examining how attackers are adapting their strategies, including the increasing use of user impersonation, access brokers, and novel initial access methods like 'logging in.' The report identifies key emerging threats, such as those targeting the research and academia sector, and analyzes prevalent attack techniques like password spray attacks. A significant portion is dedicated to ransomware, detailing a specific incident where a global impact attack was successfully contained within two minutes. The report emphasizes the importance of identity management, exploring the shift in threats from end-users to workloads, and outlines the cybercrime economy driven by identity-related vulnerabilities. Furthermore, it presents a 'Top 10 recommendations' based on the findings, offering actionable insights for organizations to mitigate risks. The report’s structure includes an introduction, an overview of the report’s unique vantage point, and appendices to aid navigation. It’s designed to provide a strategic roadmap for organizations seeking to understand and combat the increasingly sophisticated threats targeting their operations. The document also includes links to Microsoft's corporate responsibility, cybersecurity news, and LinkedIn page for cybersecurity policy updates, indicating a broader commitment to cybersecurity awareness and collaboration. The report’s focus on speed of response – the two-minute ransomware containment – underscores the critical role of rapid detection and mitigation in modern cybersecurity.
