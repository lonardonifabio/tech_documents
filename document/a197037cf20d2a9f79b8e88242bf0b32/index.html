University of Oxford Artificial Intelligence and Cybersecurity: Balancing Risks and Rewards WHITE PAPER

Authors: University of Oxford, Global Cyber Security Capacity Centre, World Economic Forum

This white paper, published in January 2025 by the World Economic Forum in collaboration with the Global Cyber Security Capacity Centre and the University of Oxford, addresses the critical intersection of Artificial Intelligence (AI) and cybersecurity. The document focuses on the transformation of industries in the age of AI, emphasizing the need for a balanced approach to managing the risks and rewards associated with widespread AI adoption. It begins with an introduction outlining the scope of AI's impact and then delves into the evolving cybersecurity landscape. A key theme is the ‘shift left’ strategy, advocating for proactive cybersecurity measures integrated early in the AI development lifecycle. This ‘shift left’ approach is further expanded upon with ‘shift left and expand right’, suggesting a continuous iterative process. The paper highlights the importance of taking an enterprise-wide view of AI cyber risk, recognizing that organizational context significantly influences the specific risks involved. It stresses the need for senior leadership to actively engage in shaping AI governance. The document provides actionable steps for managing AI cyber risk, including understanding how an organization's unique circumstances contribute to the risk profile. Furthermore, it acknowledges the potential rewards of AI while urging a thorough assessment of the associated dangers. The white paper’s core argument is that a strategic and informed approach to AI governance is essential for organizations to harness the benefits of AI while mitigating the potential cybersecurity threats. The document is a contribution to a project insight area or interaction facilitated and endorsed by the World Economic Forum, and it is intended to provide guidance for organizations navigating the complexities of AI and cybersecurity in a rapidly evolving technological landscape. The paper’s findings and conclusions represent a collaborative effort and do not necessarily reflect the views of the World Economic Forum or its members.
