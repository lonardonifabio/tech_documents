The AI Tech Stack: A Primer for Tech and Cyber Policy

Authors: Kemba Walden, Devin Lynch

This report serves as a foundational primer for public policy and cybersecurity professionals seeking to comprehend the rapidly evolving and complex landscape of Artificial Intelligence (AI). Specifically, it addresses the intersection of AI with cybersecurity concerns, emphasizing the need for deliberate and layered security solutions across the entire AI technology stack. The document outlines the various components of this stack, recognizing its dynamic and decentralized nature. The core objective is to equip readers with the knowledge necessary to strategically approach security challenges within each layer of the AI tech stack, considering both the interconnectedness and potential vulnerabilities. The report is produced by the Paladin Global Institute, an organization dedicated to safeguarding global critical infrastructure from cyber, AI, and deep tech threats. Their mission involves research, advocacy, policy recommendations, and collaboration between public and private sectors to foster innovation and enhance online safety. Key figures involved in the report's creation include Kemba Walden, the President of the Paladin Global Institute and former Acting United States National Cyber Director, and Devin Lynch, Senior Director and former Director for Cyber Policy & Strategy Implementation at the Office of the National Cyber Director (ONCD). The document acknowledges contributions from a team of reviewers, including Roberta Campbell, Eric Wengrowski, Ben Colman, Fred Heiding, Chris Inglis, Ciaran Martin, and David Hoffm, highlighting the collaborative effort behind this strategic analysis. The report’s focus on the AI tech stack underscores the importance of a holistic security approach, recognizing that vulnerabilities can exist at multiple levels of the system, from data collection and processing to deployment and governance. It’s a call to action for policymakers and cybersecurity experts to proactively address the risks associated with AI’s increasing influence across various sectors.
