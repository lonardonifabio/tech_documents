Template on Public Summary of Training Content for GPAI Models

Authors: European Commission

This document outlines a template designed for the European Commission to create public summaries detailing the training content utilized for Generative Pre-trained Transformer (GPT) AI models, specifically within the GPAI initiative. The primary goal is to provide a standardized approach for transparency and accountability regarding the data and processes involved in training these advanced AI models. The template addresses several key considerations, including a comprehensive scope of the training data, requiring sufficient details to be documented. It emphasizes the need for a balanced approach when dealing with trade secrets and confidential business information, ensuring that proprietary data is appropriately protected while still allowing for sufficient disclosure. Furthermore, the document stresses the importance of simple, uniform, and effective reporting, facilitating clear communication and understanding of the model's training. The template also incorporates considerations for modifications of existing general-purpose AI models and updates, acknowledging the dynamic nature of AI development and the need to track changes in training data and methodologies. The overall intention is to establish a framework that promotes responsible AI development and deployment by fostering trust and understanding among stakeholders. This structured approach is crucial for the GPAI initiative, which aims to advance the development and use of generative AI models while upholding ethical standards and safeguarding against potential risks. The template’s creation reflects a broader trend within the European Union to regulate and oversee the development of AI technologies, aligning with the EU's commitment to responsible innovation and the protection of fundamental rights. The document serves as a practical tool for organizations involved in training GPAI models, ensuring they meet regulatory requirements and maintain transparency regarding their training processes. It’s a key component of the broader GPAI ecosystem, supporting the initiative's goals of promoting trustworthy and beneficial AI.
