The AI Technology Stack and Why It Matters for AI Policy and Governance

This document, published by the International Technology Innovation Council (ITIC) – represented by www.itic.org – provides an explanation of the AI Technology Stack and its significance for AI policy and governance. It addresses the common misconception of AI solely focusing on end-user applications like chatbots and large language models (LLMs) such as GPT and Claude. Instead, it highlights the crucial underlying infrastructure and technologies – the AI Technology Stack – that enable AI development and deployment. The document outlines a five-layer framework: Infrastructure, Data, Model Development and Operations, Application, and Governance. Each layer represents a stage in the AI lifecycle, detailing the various providers and technologies involved. Specifically, it clarifies the roles of hyperscalers offering full-stack solutions, managed MLOps tools, foundation-model APIs, and vendor-built end-user services. The document emphasizes the importance of understanding this stack for policymakers, allowing them to assess the broader ecosystem and potential impacts of AI technologies. It’s a foundational piece for anyone seeking to understand the complexities of modern AI development and the regulatory considerations surrounding it. The ITIC, through this publication, aims to promote innovation worldwide by clarifying the technical underpinnings of AI and fostering informed discussions about its governance. The document’s focus on a layered approach provides a structured way to analyze the diverse components of the AI landscape, from the physical infrastructure to the application-level services, and the governance mechanisms that oversee them. The inclusion of hyperscalers and MLOps tools underscores the shift towards cloud-based AI solutions and the increasing reliance on specialized tools for model development and operations. Ultimately, the document serves as a valuable resource for policymakers, industry professionals, and researchers seeking to navigate the rapidly evolving world of artificial intelligence.
