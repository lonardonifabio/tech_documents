Frequently Asked Questions Data Act

Authors: European Commission

This document, dated 12 September 2025, Version 1.3, presents a set of Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs) related to the Data Act (Regulation (EU) 2023/2584). The Data Act aims to establish harmonized, horizontal rules to ensure fairness in the allocation of value generated from data across market actors, while protecting investments in data-generation technologies. Published approximately a year before the Data Act's entry into application, these FAQs are a product of extensive stakeholder interactions and are intended as a ‘living document,’ subject to updates as needed. It’s crucial to understand that this document does not represent the official position of the European Commission and the responses provided do not expand upon or modify existing legislation. The views expressed are not authoritative and cannot influence future decisions by the European Commission, including potential legal actions before the Court of Justice of the European Union. The document serves as a resource for stakeholders navigating the implementation of the Data Act and encourages users to contact the European Commission with any unanswered questions. The FAQs are designed to assist in understanding the legal provisions and the associated obligations and rights. The document emphasizes the iterative nature of the Data Act’s development and the ongoing need for clarification and adaptation.
