The General -Purpose AI Code of Practice : Copyright Chapter

Authors: Alexander Peukert, Céline Castets -Renard

This document outlines the ‘Copyright Chapter’ of The General -Purpose AI Code of Practice, prepared by Working Group 1. It focuses on establishing a robust copyright policy within the context of developing and deploying general-purpose AI. The core objective is to ensure responsible and ethical AI development by addressing intellectual property rights. Specifically, the document details a commitment to drawing up, maintaining, and implementing a comprehensive copyright policy. This policy aims to mitigate potential legal risks associated with AI systems, protecting both the creators of AI models and the data used to train them. The ‘Copyright Chapter’ serves as a foundational element of the broader AI Code of Practice, emphasizing the importance of respecting and adhering to copyright laws. The document’s structure includes objectives, recitals, and a detailed breakdown of a specific measure – 1.1 – which mandates the creation and ongoing management of a copyright policy. The working group, co-chaired by Alexander Peukert and Céline Castets -Renard, has produced this chapter as part of a larger initiative to guide the responsible development and use of AI. The document’s publication date is July 2025, indicating its relevance to future AI practices. The emphasis on a ‘to-date’ policy highlights the dynamic nature of AI and the need for continuous adaptation to evolving legal landscapes. This chapter is a critical component of the overall AI Code of Practice, promoting transparency and accountability within the AI community.
