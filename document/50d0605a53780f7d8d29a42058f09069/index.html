Development in the Future of Work 2025: Perspective on Evolving Trends in L&D

Authors: McKinsey’s Research and Innovation Learning Lab

This document, produced by McKinsey’s Research and Innovation Learning Lab, analyzes the evolving trends in Learning and Development (L&D) within the context of the future of work. It highlights a world characterized by significant disruption – including economic volatility, geopolitical tensions, rapid technological advancements, and widespread organizational change – leading to the blurring of traditional boundaries between roles, systems, and influence. The core argument is that simply reacting to or establishing a new working order is insufficient; organizations and individuals must ‘bounce forward,’ fundamentally reimagining how work is done, how learning and development occur, and how organizations operate as resilient, adaptive systems. The report moves beyond a simple trends digest and functions as a navigational tool, focusing on three key macrotrend areas: fluid development ecosystems, responsible AI adoption, and adaptability and resilience. These themes are interconnected and represent fundamental shifts in the landscape of people development. The document emphasizes a proactive approach, requiring a commitment to evolving models, tools, and mindsets to successfully navigate the complexities of the future of work. It suggests a need for organizations to embrace change and develop strategies that are not just reactive but fundamentally adaptive and forward-thinking, recognizing the interconnectedness of learning, technology, and the overall organizational structure.
