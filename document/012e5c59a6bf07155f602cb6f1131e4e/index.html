AI Action Plan for Justice

Authors: Lord Timpson, Prime Minister, Lord Chancellor

This policy paper, published on July 31, 2025, outlines the Ministry of Justice’s strategy for integrating Artificial Intelligence (AI) across the entire justice system. The document, authored by the Prime Minister, Lord Chancellor, and Lord Timpson, represents a pioneering effort to leverage AI for improved public services. It begins with a commitment to creating a more productive and agile state, emphasizing the role of AI and technology in enhancing efficiency within public services. The core of the plan focuses on three key areas: strengthening foundational elements, embedding AI throughout the justice system, and investing in the people and partners involved. Specifically, the plan aims to simplify, accelerate, and personalize interactions between the public and the justice system. The document highlights the need for a transformation in the public’s experience, driven by AI’s ability to adapt and respond to individual needs. The plan’s success hinges on a collaborative approach, involving strategic investments in human capital and partnerships. The policy paper’s publication signifies a significant step towards modernizing the justice system and aligning it with the demands of the 21st century. The document’s accessibility through the national archives website underscores the government’s commitment to transparency and public engagement.
