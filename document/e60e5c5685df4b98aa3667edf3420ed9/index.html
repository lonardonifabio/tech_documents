EU AI Act Apply AI Strategy: Plan for AI Adoption January 2026

Authors: Sean Musch, Michael Borrelli, Charles Kerrigan, Wojciech Woloszyk (Wołoszyk), Hanım Eken, Dana Lihotina, Ahmed Noureldeen, PhD, MBA

This document outlines a strategic plan for AI adoption within the European Union, specifically focusing on the application of the EU AI Act. It identifies four key roles – The Doer, The Transformer, The Leader, and The Guardian – designed to facilitate practical implementation, drive value creation, establish strategic governance, and ensure responsible and trustworthy AI development. The strategy acknowledges the significant growth of the EU Applied AI startup ecosystem, currently valued at €161 billion, which has experienced exponential expansion over the past decade. The document details a historical analysis of this growth, starting from modest increases between 2015 and 2017, peaking at €31 billion and €44 billion in 2018 and 2019 respectively, driven by increasing investor confidence and market validation. A dramatic acceleration in enterprise value occurred after 2020, reaching €75 billion by 2020 and continuing to climb. The document emphasizes the importance of navigating the complexities of AI strategy and identifying strategic opportunities, highlighting the need for strong data, platforms, and security to support AI initiatives. The overall aim is to provide a framework for EU organizations to effectively leverage AI while adhering to the principles of the EU AI Act, fostering innovation and responsible AI adoption within the European market. The plan suggests a phased approach, starting with frontline implementation ('The Doer') and progressing through value transformation ('The Transformer'), strategic leadership ('The Leader'), and responsible governance ('The Guardian').
