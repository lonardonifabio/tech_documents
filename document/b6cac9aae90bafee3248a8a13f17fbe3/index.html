Providers of General - Purpose AI Models : A Breakdown

Authors: Sean Musch, Michael Borrelli, Charles Kerrigan, Alisar Mustafa, Eevamaija Virtanen, Tulsi Soni, Diane Tea EU

This document, titled "Providers of General - Purpose AI Models : A Breakdown", analyzes the rapid expansion of generative AI models between January 2023 and April 2025, primarily focusing on text-to-text, text-to-image, and audio-to-text systems. The report, produced by OECD.AI and dated December 2025, highlights an unprecedented acceleration in language-based AI innovation, largely fueled by foundation model scaling, open-weight releases, and the democratization of fine-tuning. Specifically, the number of text-to-text models surged from six to 616, driven by advancements in large language models. Text-to-image models saw a more moderate but consistent growth, increasing from seven to 187, reflecting the maturation of diffusion models and commercial applications in creative industries. Audio-to-text systems experienced the slowest growth, indicating a functional maturity in transcription and speech-to-text technologies. Notably, text-to-audio models represent a nascent area of growth, aligning with the emerging trends of generative speech, music, and multimodal interfaces. The document emphasizes that this proliferation of models presents both significant opportunities and a degree of fragmentation within the AI landscape. The analysis suggests a competitive landscape being redefined across AI modalities, with text models leading the acceleration curve while image systems mature and audio applications begin to emerge. The report underscores the importance of understanding this dynamic shift to navigate the evolving AI market.
