Beyond the Hype: South African Attitudes to AI and the Future

Authors: Leah Junck, Imani Maliti

This research report, produced by the Global Center on AI Governance in collaboration with the Human Sciences Research Council of South Africa (HSRC), delves into the attitudes of the South African population towards Artificial Intelligence (AI) and its potential impact on the future. The study, scheduled for release in September 2025, investigates the current level of familiarity with AI, exploring the diverse sources of information shaping public perceptions. It identifies key findings regarding early impressions of AI, highlighting both positive and negative sentiments, and examines views on responsible AI implementation and use. A core focus is on anticipated effects of AI over the next decade, considering potential social and economic transformations. The report proposes a reorientation of AI governance, investments, and civil society agendas, emphasizing the need for baseline awareness, critical AI literacy, and decentralized peer-learning initiatives. It addresses concerns about holding digital platforms accountable for AI influence and explores the implications for labour markets and just transitions. The research advocates for asserting human control and directing AI investment towards social benefit, positioning civil society as a crucial catalyst and watchdog. Ultimately, the study leverages public perceptions to inform the development of meaningful engagement strategies. The report includes detailed working analyses and supplementary charts to support its findings, providing a comprehensive understanding of the complex landscape of AI attitudes in South Africa. The research aims to contribute to informed policy decisions and responsible AI development within the country.
