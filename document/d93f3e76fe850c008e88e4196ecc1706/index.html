AI Model Risk Management Framework

This document outlines the AI Model Risk Management (MRM) Framework, a comprehensive approach to managing the risks associated with the deployment and use of artificial intelligence models. It begins with an overview of the framework's structure, including a table of contents, acknowledgements, and an executive summary. The intended audience for this framework is broad, encompassing anyone involved in the development, deployment, or governance of AI models. The scope of the framework addresses the entire lifecycle of an AI model, from initial design and data preparation through ongoing monitoring and risk assessment. A key element of the framework is the establishment of four core pillars: Model Cards, Data Sheets, Risk Cards, and Scenario Planning. Model Cards provide standardized documentation for AI models, detailing their purpose, performance, limitations, and intended use. Data Sheets offer transparency into the data used to train and validate models, ensuring data quality and identifying potential biases. Risk Cards focus on identifying and assessing the potential risks associated with model deployment, while Scenario Planning allows for proactive consideration of various potential outcomes. The document highlights the numerous benefits of implementing a robust MRM framework, including improved model reliability, reduced operational risk, enhanced regulatory compliance, and increased trust in AI systems. Ultimately, this framework provides a structured approach to managing the complexities of AI model risk, promoting responsible innovation and maximizing the value of AI technologies. The document emphasizes the importance of a proactive and systematic approach to risk management within the rapidly evolving field of artificial intelligence.
