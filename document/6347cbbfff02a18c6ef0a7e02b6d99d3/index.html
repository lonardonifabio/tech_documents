The Impact of Human-AI Interaction on Discrimination

Authors: Gaudeul, A., Arrigoni, O., Charisi, V., Escobar Planas, M., Hupont Torres, I.

This research report, titled "The Impact of Human-AI Interaction on Discrimination," investigates the role of human oversight in AI-based decision support systems, specifically within the contexts of lending and hiring. The study presents a large-scale case study examining how human intervention can mitigate potential discriminatory outcomes arising from AI algorithms. The research focuses on the critical need for human oversight when AI systems are utilized for making decisions that significantly impact individuals' opportunities, such as loan applications and job recruitment. It highlights the potential for bias to be embedded within AI models due to biased training data or algorithmic design, and consequently, the importance of human judgment to identify and correct these biases. The report emphasizes that while AI can offer efficiency and scale in decision-making, it is not a substitute for ethical considerations and human understanding. The case study analyzes scenarios where human oversight can effectively challenge AI-driven decisions, ensuring fairness and preventing discriminatory outcomes. The research contributes to the broader discussion surrounding Responsible AI, advocating for a collaborative approach between humans and AI systems, where human expertise is leveraged to ensure that AI is used ethically and responsibly. The document details the methodology used, the findings of the case study, and recommendations for best practices in implementing AI systems in sensitive areas like lending and hiring, with a strong emphasis on accountability and transparency. The study's findings underscore the necessity of continuous monitoring and evaluation of AI systems to identify and address any emerging biases. The report is a valuable resource for policymakers, researchers, and practitioners involved in the development and deployment of AI technologies, particularly those concerned with fairness, equity, and non-discrimination. It provides a practical framework for integrating human oversight into AI workflows to minimize the risk of perpetuating or amplifying existing societal biases.
