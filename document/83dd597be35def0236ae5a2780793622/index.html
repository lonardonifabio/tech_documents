This document, the "AI Ecosystem Brief 2025 Edition Finland," is a publication by AI & Partners, released in September 2025. It focuses on the organization's mission to defend and extend digital rights for users globally who are considered at risk due to the development and deployment of artificial intelligence. The report highlights AI & Partners' approach, which combines direct technical support, policy engagement, global advocacy, grassroots professional services, regulatory interventions, and participation in industry groups like AI Commons. The core argument is that AI should be used to unlock potential rather than cause harm, and the organization aims to assist clients in building AI systems they can trust and confidently stand behind. AI & Partners emphasizes the importance of responsible AI development and deployment, addressing the potential risks associated with losing control of AI systems. The report details the organization's strategy for navigating the evolving landscape of AI regulation and promoting ethical practices within the industry. It underscores the need for proactive engagement in shaping the future of AI to ensure it aligns with fundamental human rights and fosters innovation while mitigating potential negative consequences. The document serves as a strategic overview of AI & Partners' activities and their commitment to responsible AI governance. It’s a publication intended to inform stakeholders about the organization's role in advocating for user rights and promoting trustworthy AI solutions.
