AI Act Guide Version 1.1

Authors: Minezk, European Commission

This document, the "AI Act Guide Version 1.1," serves as a supplementary resource designed to facilitate understanding of the European Union's AI Act. Released in September 2025, the guide is intended for individuals and organizations involved in developing or considering the use of AI systems, particularly those who may encounter the AI Act's regulations. Crucially, it emphasizes that this guide does not create any legal rights and that the official AI Act text always holds precedence. The guide provides an introductory overview of key aspects of the AI Act, including its definition of AI, the designation of prohibited AI systems, and the specific rules governing General Purpose AI models. The AI Act itself is a comprehensive legal framework established for the entire European Union, aiming to govern the development and deployment of Artificial Intelligence. Its primary objective is to safeguard the safety, health, and fundamental rights of individuals within the EU. The regulation imposes obligations on businesses, government entities, and other organizations to ensure responsible AI practices. The guide highlights the importance of adhering to these regulations, allowing organizations to operate with greater certainty regarding compliance. Furthermore, the document directs readers to the European Commission's website (ondernemersplein.nl) for the most up-to-date guidelines and references. Feedback on the guide is encouraged and will be utilized to refine future iterations. The guide's creation reflects the significant legal and ethical considerations surrounding AI development and implementation within the EU, signaling a proactive approach to managing the risks associated with this rapidly evolving technology. The document's focus on clarity and accessibility underscores the need for readily available resources to support organizations navigating the complexities of the AI Act.
