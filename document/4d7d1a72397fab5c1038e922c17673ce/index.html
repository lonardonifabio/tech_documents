Multinational Advanced AI Development: Costs and Strategic Implications

Authors: Pilz, Cottier

This document analyzes the substantial financial barriers associated with developing and maintaining 'frontier' AI models, particularly focusing on the challenges faced by individual nations and the potential benefits of collaborative efforts.
