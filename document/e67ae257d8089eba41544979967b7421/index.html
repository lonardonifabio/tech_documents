2025 OT Security Financial Risk Report

Authors: Marsh McLennan, Dragos

This comprehensive 2025 OT Security Financial Risk Report, produced by Marsh McLennan, delivers a critical assessment of the financial consequences stemming from cyber incidents targeting Operational Technology (OT) systems. The report’s core focus is on quantifying potential future losses resulting from OT cyber breaches, utilizing statistical modeling and analysis conducted by the Marsh McLennan Cyber Risk Intelligence Center (CRIC). A key element of the study involves identifying and evaluating the effectiveness of various OT cyber risk controls, assigning a quantifiable risk reduction impact to each. The research leverages data compiled and modeled by CRIC, providing insurance statisticians with a robust framework for understanding and mitigating these risks. Specifically, the report delves into the ‘What’s At Risk’ aspect, estimating the average at risk within BI claims and assessing the likelihood of impact. Furthermore, it identifies the top three challenges associated with managing and insuring OT cyber risk. The report offers strategic insights tailored for risk managers and insurance stakeholders, outlining actionable steps and considerations. It incorporates findings from SANS ICS and recommendations from Dragos, providing a multi-faceted approach to addressing the evolving threat landscape. The methodology includes a detailed examination of data derived from cyber breaches, allowing for a more accurate prediction of future losses. The report’s conclusions are designed to inform decision-making and guide investment in appropriate security controls, ultimately reducing the financial vulnerability of organizations reliant on OT systems. The study’s findings are presented with a clear focus on actionable intelligence, enabling stakeholders to proactively manage and mitigate the significant financial risks associated with OT cyber security.
