AI & PPPs: Unlocking Sustainable, Resilient Infrastructure for the SDGs

Authors: UNECE

This policy brief, part of a series focused on Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Public-Private Partnerships (PPPs), explores the potential of AI to revolutionize infrastructure development and support the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs). The core argument centers around the possibility of lower transaction costs and accelerated PPP processes through AI implementation, leading to greater efficiency and effectiveness in infrastructure projects. However, the brief also emphasizes the critical need to carefully assess and mitigate the inherent risks associated with utilizing AI within these complex projects. The UNECE (United Nations Economic Commission for Europe) is the driving force behind this policy brief series, collaborating with leading experts from diverse sectors – governments, the private sector, academia, civil society, and international organizations – to provide a comprehensive understanding of the topic. The series utilizes a multi-faceted approach, including policy briefs, webinars, and podcasts, to engage a broad audience and foster informed discussion. Specifically, the brief delves into how AI is currently being applied in infrastructure projects, highlighting its capabilities in predicting future infrastructure needs, generating insightful reports and analyses, and processing large datasets. It examines the dual nature of AI's impact, acknowledging both the significant opportunities and potential drawbacks. The document clearly states that the views expressed are those of the authors and do not represent the official positions of the United Nations or its member states. The overarching goal is to provide a balanced perspective on the integration of AI into PPPs and infrastructure, ultimately contributing to the development of more sustainable and resilient infrastructure solutions aligned with global development priorities. The document's focus on lifecycle considerations underscores the importance of strategic planning and risk management throughout the entire project duration.
