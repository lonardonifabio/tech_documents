Prompt Engineering for LLMs: The Art and Science of Building Large Language Model–Based Applications

Authors: John Berryman, Albert Ziegler

This book, "Prompt Engineering for LLMs," authored by John Berryman and Albert Ziegler, delves into the critical skill of prompt engineering for large language models (LLMs). It addresses the burgeoning impact of LLMs, which are transforming industries by automating tasks and tackling complex problems, and highlights the need for specialized expertise to effectively utilize these models. The book provides a comprehensive guide, blending both the theoretical foundations and practical techniques required to master prompt engineering. John Berryman, founder of Arcturus Labs and a former GitHub Copilot engineer, brings his experience in LLM-based application development and search expertise, having authored "Relevant Search" (Manning). Albert Ziegler, Head of AI at XBOW and a founding engineer for GitHub Copilot, contributes his deep understanding of model interaction and prompt engineering systems, particularly in the context of the first successful industry-scale LLM product. The core focus is on understanding how to communicate effectively with AI, translating ideas into a format suitable for LLMs. Readers will gain insights into LLM architecture and learn the methodologies for crafting prompts that elicit desired responses. The book emphasizes the importance of both the philosophical underpinnings and the practical techniques involved in prompt engineering, equipping individuals with the knowledge and confidence to develop the next generation of LLM-powered applications. It's designed for those seeking to leverage the full potential of LLMs, moving beyond simply using them to understand how to truly control and shape their output. The book aims to bridge the gap between the rapidly evolving technology of LLMs and the practical skills needed to build successful applications. It’s a valuable resource for anyone involved in developing or deploying LLM-based solutions, covering topics from model architecture to prompt design and optimization.
