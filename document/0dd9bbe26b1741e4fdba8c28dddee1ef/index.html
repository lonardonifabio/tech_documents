AI Governance in the Boardroom

Authors: Institute of Directors Expert Advisory Group on Science, Innovation and Technology, Michael Ambjorn, Phil Clare, Paul Corcoran, Jonathan R. Everhart, Faisal Khan, Rob Noble, Pauline Norstrom, Yinka Olarinde, Erin Young

This IoD Business Paper, "AI Governance in the Boardroom," addresses the critical governance questions boards must consider when dealing with Artificial Intelligence. Produced by the Institute of Directors Expert Advisory Group on Science, Innovation and Technology in collaboration with the IoD’s Head of Innovation and Technology Policy, the paper provides a framework for responsible AI adoption. It highlights the need for proactive governance, starting with understanding the evolving regulatory and (geo)political landscape surrounding AI. The document emphasizes the importance of continuous monitoring and auditing of AI systems, including their principles, processes, and controls. A key component is the development of robust impact and risk assessments, taking into account the broader business implications of AI deployment. The paper advocates for a structured approach, utilizing reflective checklists to guide board discussions. Furthermore, it stresses the significance of technical infrastructure integration and ongoing measurement of AI usage. The IoD’s Policy Voice Survey on AI adoption is referenced, providing valuable insights into current trends. The document’s core message is that boards must actively shape the governance of AI to mitigate risks and maximize opportunities. The paper’s collaborative development involved a diverse group of experts, including CEOs of Prop Tech Denmark, Queen Mary Innovation, Global ReEnergy Holdings, and leaders from Anekanta®AI and Siep Global Consulting Ltd, demonstrating a broad perspective on the challenges and considerations surrounding AI governance. The paper’s aim is to equip boards with the knowledge and tools necessary to navigate the complexities of AI and ensure its responsible and strategic implementation.
