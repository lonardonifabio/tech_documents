AI Incident Response: Adapting Proven Complex Systems Engineering Practices for AI-Enabled Systems

Authors: Heather Frase

This document, authored by Heather Frase of Veraitec, explores the critical need for a new approach to incident response specifically tailored for AI-enabled systems. Published in November 2025, it argues that current incident response methodologies are insufficient for addressing the unique complexities and risks associated with AI. The core of the document centers around a seven-step process grounded in proven complex systems engineering practices, designed to provide a structured framework for managing and mitigating AI incidents. It identifies significant gaps in existing capabilities, stemming from the inherent complexity of AI-enabled systems and a lack of foundational structures. The document advocates for an ‘ecosystem approach,’ emphasizing collaboration among key stakeholders – AI deployers, developers, assurance organizations, and standards bodies – to maximize benefits. Specifically, it outlines a detailed seven-step incident response loop, integrating with existing frameworks. The document delves into defining what constitutes an ‘AI incident,’ recognizing the need for specialized definitions. It then provides actionable recommendations for each key stakeholder group, focusing on implementation strategies and the importance of coordinated efforts. The core argument is that by adapting established complex systems engineering principles, organizations can proactively manage the risks and effectively respond to incidents within increasingly prevalent AI-driven environments. The document’s purpose is to establish a robust and adaptable framework for handling the novel challenges presented by AI systems, moving beyond traditional IT incident response models. It highlights the urgency of addressing this issue and provides a roadmap for building a more resilient and secure AI landscape. The document is intended for a technical audience including AI developers, system engineers, and those involved in risk management and assurance within the context of AI deployments.
