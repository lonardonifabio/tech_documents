This document, titled "AI Ecosystem Brief 2025 Edition," is a publication by AI & Partners, released in November 2025, focusing on the responsible development and deployment of Artificial Intelligence. The core message centers around mitigating the risks associated with uncontrolled AI adoption, emphasizing the need for organizations to proactively manage and govern their AI systems. AI & Partners positions itself as a solution provider, aiming to help clients build and maintain trust in AI by offering tools for identification, monitoring, and governance of AI systems. A key concern highlighted is the significant amount of AI inventory – approximately 80% – that remains unknown to clients, leading to potential confusion, risk, and reputational damage. The document outlines a strategy to address this by automating the generation of an AI inventory, encompassing algorithms, LLMs, prompts, and models from both internal and external vendors. Furthermore, it stresses the importance of continuous monitoring of deployed AI systems for performance drift, suggesting a proactive approach to ensuring ongoing safe AI usage. The brief advocates for a shift from reactive responses to leading AI initiatives, allowing organizations to control and direct their AI investments effectively. The document's purpose is to guide organizations in navigating the complexities of the evolving AI landscape and to establish a framework for responsible AI implementation. It underscores the critical need for transparency, control, and continuous oversight to avoid potential pitfalls and maximize the benefits of AI technology.
