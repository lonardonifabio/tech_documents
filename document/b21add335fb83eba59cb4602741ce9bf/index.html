Model AI Governance Framework vs. EU AI Act A Mapping Exercise

Authors: Sean Musch, Michael Borrelli, Charles Kerrigan, Dr. Nadia Morozova, Mark Minevich

This report, published by AI & Partners, provides a mapping exercise comparing the Model AI Governance Framework with the EU AI Act. It highlights the critical need for robust AI governance, emphasizing the potential risks associated with uncontrolled AI adoption, including confusion, risk, and reputational damage. The document argues that AI should unlock potential rather than cause harm, advocating for responsible AI handling to build trust and innovation. AI & Partners’ mission is to assist organizations and clients in establishing and maintaining safe AI usage through tools for identification, monitoring, and governance. The report underscores the importance of proactive AI governance to mitigate risks and ensure responsible AI development and deployment, aligning with the broader goals of the EU AI Act. The authors, including Sean Musch and Michael Borrelli, represent AI & Partners, a company dedicated to defending digital rights and advocating for responsible AI practices. The report’s purpose is to guide organizations in navigating the evolving landscape of AI regulation and governance, promoting a future where AI benefits humanity without causing undue harm. It’s a strategic document designed to help businesses and organizations proactively manage their AI initiatives and ensure compliance with emerging regulations.
