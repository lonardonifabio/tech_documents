OPINION ON AI GOVERNANCE AND RISK MANAGEMENT EIOPA -BoS-25-360

Authors: EIOPA

This document, titled "OPINION ON AI GOVERNANCE AND RISK MANAGEMENT EIOPA -BoS-25-360", is an opinion issued by the European Insurance and Occupational Pensions Authority (EIOPA) concerning the governance and risk management of Artificial Intelligence (AI) within the insurance sector. The opinion is based on several key regulatory frameworks, including Regulation (EU) No 1094/2010, the Insurance Distribution Directive (Directive (EU) 2016/972), the Solvency II Directive (Directive 2009/138/EC), the Digital Operational Resilience Act (Regulation (EU) 2022/2554), and Commission Delegated Regulations 2015/35 and 2017/2358. Specifically, it addresses the activities of insurance undertakings and intermediaries as they relate to the use of AI systems across the insurance value chain. The document highlights the significant role AI is expected to play in the ongoing digital transformation of the insurance industry. It’s a foundational opinion designed to guide supervisory practices and ensure consistent approaches to AI governance and risk management within the European Union’s insurance landscape. The opinion’s scope encompasses the activities of both insurance companies and intermediaries, emphasizing the need for robust governance frameworks to mitigate the risks associated with AI implementation. The document’s purpose is to establish a common supervisory culture and consistent practices, aligning with the regulatory requirements outlined in the referenced directives and regulations. It’s a strategic document intended to shape the future of AI adoption within the insurance sector, promoting responsible innovation and minimizing potential risks.
