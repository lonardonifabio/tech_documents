Notified Bodies Assessing AI System Conformity EU AI Act September 2025

Authors: Sean Musch, Michael Borrelli, Charles Kerrigan, Jean Gan, Umang Mehta, Cecilia Lascialfari, Mehmet Tahir Ağrak

This document, published by AI & Partners, focuses on the assessment of AI system conformity in relation to the EU AI Act, scheduled for implementation by September 2025. It highlights AI & Partners' mission to defend and extend the digital rights of users globally, particularly those at risk due to the potential harms of unregulated AI. The organization advocates for responsible AI development and deployment, emphasizing the importance of trust and innovation within the field. AI & Partners employs a multi-faceted approach, combining technical support, policy engagement, global advocacy, grassroots professional services, regulatory interventions, and participation in industry groups like AI Commons. The core belief is that AI should unlock potential rather than cause harm, and the organization aims to assist clients and organizations in building and deploying AI systems they can trust and stand behind, mitigating the risks associated with unknown AI adoption, such as confusion, risk, and reputational damage. The report details their commitment to responsible AI usage and their role in navigating the evolving regulatory landscape surrounding AI, particularly in the context of the EU AI Act. Contact information and the organization's website are provided for further inquiries.
