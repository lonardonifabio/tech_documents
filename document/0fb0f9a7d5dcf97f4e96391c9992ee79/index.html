Reporting Requirements for High-Risk AI Incidents Resulting in Fatality

Authors: Unknown

This document outlines the immediate reporting requirements for AI incidents, specifically focusing on those involving high-risk AI systems that result in a fatality. The core principle established is prioritizing human life and ensuring rapid transparency following such incidents. Upon becoming aware of an incident involving a high-risk AI system and a suspected causal link to a death, the provider is mandated to submit an initial report within 10 days. This deadline is irrespective of whether a causal link has been definitively established, emphasizing the urgency of the situation. The purpose of this immediate reporting is to facilitate prompt investigations by relevant authorities, allowing them to issue safety recommendations or enact regulatory actions. Providers are obligated to cooperate fully with these investigations, including refraining from modifying the AI system without prior coordination with authorities. The document addresses the practical considerations surrounding reporting, specifically allowing for the submission of a partial report initially to trigger an immediate response. This preliminary report serves as an alert and initiates the authorities' response processes. However, a comprehensive and detailed follow-up report is then required as soon as possible, allowing providers time to gather all necessary information. The structure of the complete report is defined, demanding a detailed account of the incident, the specific AI system involved, any identified failures or malfunctions, and a thorough assessment of the associated risks. The overall strategy aims to balance the need for immediate notification with the requirement for a complete and accurate investigation, mitigating potential delays that could exacerbate the situation or introduce further complications. The document underscores the critical role of proactive reporting in managing high-risk AI technologies and safeguarding public safety. It’s a regulatory framework designed to ensure accountability and rapid response in the event of adverse outcomes linked to advanced AI systems.
