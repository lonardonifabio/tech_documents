Guidance on AI and Children 3.0

Authors: UNICEF

This document, titled "Guidance on AI and Children 3.0," released in December 2025, provides updated recommendations for governments and businesses to develop AI policies and systems that prioritize and protect children's rights. The core of the guidance focuses on establishing a ‘child-centred AI’ approach, recognizing the significant and rapidly increasing influence of AI in children’s lives. It outlines ten key requirements for such systems, including ensuring regulatory frameworks and oversight, prioritizing child safety and data privacy, combating discrimination, promoting transparency and accountability, and ultimately, supporting children’s development and well-being. The document stresses the importance of inclusion and preparing children for the future impacts of AI. The guidance is underpinned by UNICEF research, which indicates a substantial and growing adoption of AI among children globally. Specifically, data reveals that 67% of UK teens utilize AI, 39% of American elementary students learn through AI applications, and 37% of children aged 9-11 in Argentina rely on ChatGPT for information. The document highlights the need for proactive measures to mitigate potential risks and ensure that AI benefits children positively. It emphasizes the importance of ongoing research and adaptation as AI technology continues to evolve. The document's intention is to establish a framework for responsible AI development and deployment, safeguarding children's rights and fostering a future where AI serves their best interests. The document also includes cross-cutting recommendations and considerations, implementation strategies, and acknowledgements, along with an ‘About Us’ section detailing UNICEF’s mission and activities. The document’s focus on data privacy and ethical considerations reflects a growing concern about the potential impact of AI on vulnerable populations, particularly children.
