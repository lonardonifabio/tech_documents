Technology Trends Outlook 2025

Authors: Lareina Yee, Michael Chui, Roger Roberts, Sven Smit

The "Technology Trends Outlook 2025" report, published in July 2025, provides an annual analysis of key technology advancements and their potential impact on businesses. This edition focuses on identifying the most critical frontier technologies for companies in 2025, encompassing a wide range of innovations including robotics, mobility, bioengineering, space technologies, and energy sustainability. The report delves into the ‘AI revolution,’ specifically examining Agentic AI and broader Artificial Intelligence applications. A significant portion of the report is dedicated to understanding the evolving landscape of Compute and Connectivity frontiers, including application-specific semiconductors and advanced connectivity solutions. Furthermore, it addresses the growing importance of Cloud and Edge computing, alongside the emerging field of Immersive-reality technologies. Crucially, the document also addresses critical considerations surrounding Digital trust and cybersecurity, and the potential of Quantum technologies. The report highlights the accelerating demand for computing power, the increased attention from management teams and the public, and the resulting acceleration of experimentation within various sectors. It’s a comprehensive overview of the technological shifts shaping the business world in 2025, offering insights into strategic investments and operational adaptations. The report’s structure includes detailed sections on each of these key areas, providing a granular understanding of the technological landscape and its implications.
