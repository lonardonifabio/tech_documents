Global AI Governance Law and Policy

Authors: IAPP, HCLTech

This article series, co-sponsored by HCLTech and published by IAPP, provides a comprehensive overview of the evolving landscape of artificial intelligence governance laws and policies across a range of jurisdictions globally. Launched in 2024, the series addresses the rapid development and diverse risks and opportunities presented by AI-powered technologies. The focus is on key markets including Australia, Canada, China, the European Union, India, Japan, Singapore, South Korea, the United Arab Emirates, the United Kingdom, and the United States. Each article examines the specific laws, policies, and contextual history related to AI governance within each jurisdiction, considering both the strategic and compliance aspects. The series delves into various approaches to AI governance, including voluntary frameworks, sectoral initiatives, and comprehensive legislative measures. A key element of the series is the inclusion of ‘agentic AI,’ recognizing it as a new frontier for organizations seeking to leverage this technology. The articles analyze how critical areas such as intellectual property and agentic AI present unique challenges and opportunities for businesses and governments. The series aims to offer a snapshot of distinct AI governance approaches in these key global markets, reflecting the ongoing evolution of this critical field. The content is designed to inform legal professionals, policymakers, and technology leaders about the latest developments and considerations in AI governance worldwide. The series highlights the diverse and dynamic nature of AI policy development, emphasizing the need for proactive and adaptable strategies.
