2025 Responsible AI Impact Report

Authors: All Tech Is Human (ATIH)

The 2025 Responsible AI Impact Report, produced by All Tech Is Human (ATIH), addresses the critical need for a more considerate and public-interest-focused approach to the rapid development of Artificial Intelligence. This inaugural report delves into the most urgent risks associated with AI's advancement, outlines emerging safeguards, and highlights public-interest solutions designed to shape AI's impact on society. The report’s primary goal is to provide a strategic roadmap for ATIH’s activities in the coming year, focusing on how they intend to influence the trajectory of Responsible AI (RAI). It examines the state of RAI throughout 2025, recognizing the significant contributions made by civil society organizations in expanding and enriching this evolving field. ATIH’s definition of RAI emphasizes a multi-faceted approach, encompassing robust regulation and guardrails, effective governance through documentation, standardization, and benchmarking, and rigorous assessment, evaluation, and red-teaming processes. The report reflects ATIH’s core belief in a human-centered future of AI, prioritizing agency in desired outcomes and rejecting the notion of ‘tech determinism.’ Specifically, the organization is concentrating on AI models that minimize potential harm, particularly in applications where risks have been thoroughly assessed and appropriately mitigated, and on the ethical deployment of AI, ensuring that high-level ethical principles are translated into operational practices. The document underscores the importance of a collaborative effort between technology developers, civil society, and policymakers to navigate the complex challenges at the intersection of technology and society, ultimately fostering a Responsible AI ecosystem that benefits all stakeholders. The report’s focus on 2025 indicates a forward-looking perspective, aiming to proactively address emerging concerns and guide the development of RAI strategies.
