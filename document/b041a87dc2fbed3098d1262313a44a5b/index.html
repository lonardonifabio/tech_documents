Data Act versus EU AI Act: A Mapping Exercise

Authors: Sean Musch, Michael Borrelli, Charles Kerrigan, Richard Self, Lord Chris Holmes

This report, published by AI & Partners, provides a mapping exercise comparing the Data Act and the EU AI Act. AI & Partners, a non-profit organization, focuses on defending digital rights and promoting responsible AI development. The organization’s mission is to mitigate the risks associated with uncontrolled AI adoption, which can lead to confusion, reputational damage, and loss of control. They achieve this by offering tools and services to help organizations identify, monitor, and govern their AI systems, ensuring responsible AI usage for both the organization and its clients. The report highlights the importance of proactive governance and risk management within the rapidly evolving landscape of AI regulations, particularly in the context of the EU AI Act and the Data Act. AI & Partners advocates for a future where AI unlocks potential rather than causing harm, emphasizing the need for trust and accountability in AI development and deployment. The organization’s approach combines technical support, policy engagement, advocacy, and regulatory interventions to safeguard digital rights and promote responsible AI practices globally. The document details the organization's commitment to providing solutions that allow clients to maintain control and build AI systems they can stand behind, ultimately fostering innovation and trust in the artificial intelligence age. It underscores the critical role of organizations like AI & Partners in navigating the complexities of emerging AI regulations and ensuring a future where AI benefits humanity.
