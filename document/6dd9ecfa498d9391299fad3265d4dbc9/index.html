Draft Guidance Article 73 AI Act - Incident Reporting (High -Risk AI Systems)

Authors: European Commission

This document presents draft guidance related to the reporting of incidents involving high-risk AI systems, specifically in the context of the European Commission's proposed AI Act. The core objective is to establish a framework for systematically identifying, documenting, and reporting adverse events stemming from the deployment or operation of AI systems classified as high-risk. The guidance aims to ensure accountability and transparency within the AI ecosystem, mitigating potential harms and fostering responsible innovation. It begins with a background outlining the need for robust incident reporting, particularly given the potential impact of high-risk AI systems on individuals and society. A key section defines critical terms, including ‘AI Act,’ ‘Incident,’ and ‘Malfunction,’ establishing a common understanding for stakeholders. The document meticulously dissects the concept of ‘directly or indirectly’ caused incidents, clarifying the scope of responsibility. The guidance emphasizes the importance of thorough investigation and documentation of any malfunction or unintended consequence. It's designed to support organizations developing and deploying high-risk AI systems in meeting the requirements of the AI Act, promoting proactive risk management, and ultimately, building trust in AI technologies. The document serves as a foundational resource for compliance and responsible AI development, contributing to the broader regulatory landscape surrounding artificial intelligence. The intention is to provide practical tools and definitions to facilitate effective incident reporting, thereby strengthening the safeguards embedded within the AI Act. This draft guidance is crucial for organizations navigating the complexities of high-risk AI and ensuring alignment with evolving regulatory standards. The document's creation reflects the European Commission's commitment to a safe and ethical AI framework.
