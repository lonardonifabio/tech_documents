Scenarios for the Global Economy Dialogue Series: Four Futures for Jobs in the New Economy: AI and Talent in 2030

Authors: World Economic Forum

This white paper, published by the World Economic Forum in January 2026, explores the potential impacts of artificial intelligence (AI) on the global economy and the future of work by examining four distinct scenarios for jobs in 2030. The document addresses the accelerating commercialization of emerging technologies, particularly AI, and its effect on business models, talent acquisition, and overall economic trends. It highlights the significant uncertainty surrounding AI's advancement and the diverse perspectives of business executives regarding its implications. The paper investigates how AI's transition from experimental phases to widespread integration is intensifying these uncertainties. A core focus is on understanding the varied responses and preparations businesses should undertake to navigate these potential shifts. Specifically, it delves into the implications for businesses, outlining the challenges and opportunities presented by AI's increasing presence. Furthermore, the white paper provides actionable guidance on how businesses can proactively prepare for any of the four envisioned future scenarios, emphasizing the need for adaptability and strategic foresight. The document includes sections on contributing authors and endnotes, offering further context and supporting information. The World Economic Forum emphasizes that the findings and conclusions presented are a collaborative effort, representing a facilitated process endorsed by the organization but not necessarily reflecting the views of all its members, partners, or stakeholders. The document's purpose is to contribute to a broader dialogue surrounding the future of work in an AI-driven economy, prompting critical analysis and strategic planning for businesses and economies worldwide. The analysis considers the potential disruption to existing workflows and the need for new skillsets within the workforce. It underscores the importance of understanding and responding to the evolving landscape shaped by AI’s rapid development.
