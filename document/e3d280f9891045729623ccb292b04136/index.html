AI/ML CheatSheet

This document serves as a comprehensive cheat sheet for individuals seeking a foundational understanding of Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Machine Learning (ML). It begins with an introductory overview, defining AI and ML, differentiating between Narrow AI and General AI, and outlining the core concepts within ML, including supervised, unsupervised, and reinforcement learning. Key terminology such as Model, Feature, Target, and Algorithm are clearly defined. The document then delves into the mathematical underpinnings of ML/AI, covering Linear Algebra (vectors, matrices, tensors, eigenvalues, eigenvectors), Probability and Statistics (mean, variance, standard deviation, Bayes Theorem, probability distributions), and Calculus (derivatives, gradients, gradient descent). A significant portion is dedicated to Data Preprocessing, detailing techniques for cleaning data (handling missing values and outliers), normalizing and standardizing data, encoding categorical variables (one-hot encoding, label encoding), and feature scaling (min-max, z-score). Furthermore, it explores feature engineering methods like polynomial features and binning, alongside strategies for addressing imbalanced datasets using techniques like SMOTE, undersampling, and oversampling. The cheat sheet then systematically introduces various Supervised Learning Algorithms, starting with Linear Regression (simple and multiple), Gradient Descent and the Normal Equation, and Regularization methods (L1, L2). It also covers Logistic Regression, including binary and multiclass classification, the sigmoid function, and cost functions. Finally, it introduces K-Nearest Neighbors (KNN) with distance metrics (Euclidean, Manhattan) and the importance of selecting the optimal 'K'. The document concludes with an overview of Support Vector Machines (SVMs), explaining concepts like hyperplanes, margins, and the use of kernel functions. This cheat sheet provides a concise and practical guide for those new to AI and ML, offering a quick reference to essential concepts and algorithms. It's designed to be a valuable resource for students, researchers, and professionals seeking a rapid introduction to the field.
