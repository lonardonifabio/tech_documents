Incident Report for Serious Incidents under the AI Act (High -risk AI systems)

This document presents an incident reporting template specifically designed for serious incidents involving high-risk AI systems as defined under the AI Act. The template is intended for use by market surveillance authorities to systematically record and report such incidents. It focuses on gathering crucial information related to the incident itself, including the date and time of occurrence, the type of incident, and the classification of the severity. Key sections cover administrative details such as the responsible market surveillance authority, the reference number assigned to the incident, and the submitter's information. The template meticulously details the classification of the incident, encompassing categories like death, harm to a person's health, disruption of critical infrastructure, infringement of fundamental rights, and harm to property or the environment. It also includes fields for specifying the type of report (initial, follow-up, combined, final - reportable, or final - non-reportable) and, for initial and follow-up reports, the anticipated date for the next report. The document’s purpose is to standardize incident reporting for high-risk AI systems, facilitating effective monitoring and enforcement of the AI Act. The template’s structured approach ensures consistent data collection, enabling authorities to analyze trends, identify potential risks, and take appropriate action. The inclusion of the manufacturer awareness date is crucial for tracing the root cause of the incident and assessing the manufacturer's responsibilities. Ultimately, this reporting template serves as a vital tool for maintaining safety and accountability within the high-risk AI system landscape.
