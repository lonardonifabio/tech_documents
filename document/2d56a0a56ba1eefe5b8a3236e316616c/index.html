AI for infrastructure resilience

Authors: Deloitte Center for Sustainable Progress

This document, "AI for infrastructure resilience," published in June 2025, explores the critical role of Artificial Intelligence (AI) in bolstering the resilience of infrastructure systems globally. It addresses the escalating pressures faced by infrastructure – including extreme weather, aging assets, and the energy transition – and argues for a proactive approach to risk management. The report outlines a three-phase strategy: Plan (prevention), Respond (detection and reaction), and Recover (post-incident). Specifically, it details how AI can be utilized across these phases, focusing on real-time hazard detection, predictive maintenance, and optimized recovery processes. The document emphasizes the potential economic benefits of AI-powered resilient infrastructure, highlighting reduced vulnerability through robust planning and preventative measures, and mitigation of hazards through real-time detection and reactive measures. It also addresses barriers to AI implementation within infrastructure and proposes a forward-looking approach. The report includes supporting appendices detailing the estimation of economic value, assessment of hazard costs, and calculation of resilience enabled by AI. The core argument centers on leveraging AI's capabilities to transform infrastructure management from a reactive to a proactive and adaptive system, ultimately enhancing stability and minimizing disruption. The document’s scope extends to various infrastructure sectors, acknowledging the interconnectedness of systems and the need for integrated resilience strategies. It’s a strategic overview designed to inform decision-makers on the opportunities and challenges associated with deploying AI to safeguard critical infrastructure assets.
