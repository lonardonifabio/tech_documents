Global Risks Barometer (GRPS) Findings - 2024

Authors: Unknown

The 2024 Global Risks Barometer (GRPS) findings paint a concerning picture of the global outlook, highlighting significantly heightened short-term risks and a persistent sense of instability across various time horizons. The report reveals that a substantial 64% of respondents believe the global outlook is unsettled over the next 10 years, a figure that rises to 40% over the two-year timeframe, with only 1% anticipating a calm outlook across any horizon. This assessment is driven by escalating global risks characterized by increased interconnectivity, velocity, and scale, leading to a competitive landscape where cooperative mechanisms are failing. The data indicates a shift towards confrontation, with governments withdrawing from multilateral frameworks and eroding trust – a critical element for international collaboration. Specifically, the GRPS shows a 14 percentage-point increase in respondents expressing turbulent or stormy views over the next two years, reflecting a heightened sense of immediate danger. While the longer-term outlook (10 years) shows a slight improvement compared to last year, with a decrease from 62% to 57% in those selecting turbulent or stormy, it still represents a significant portion of concern. A small increase (3 percentage points) in respondents choosing ‘calm or stable’ and a 2 percentage point increase in ‘unsettled’ further complicates the picture. The report underscores the fragility of the multilateral system, citing declining trust, reduced transparency, and a disregard for the rule of law as key drivers of instability. Heightened protectionism is exacerbating these issues, increasing the likelihood of conflict and disrupting international trade and investment. Geoeconomic confrontation is identified as a primary concern, suggesting a growing divide between nations and a potential for further escalation. The findings strongly suggest a move away from cooperation towards competition and a period of heightened uncertainty and risk across the global landscape. The data emphasizes the urgent need for proactive strategies to mitigate these risks and foster greater stability in an increasingly volatile world.
