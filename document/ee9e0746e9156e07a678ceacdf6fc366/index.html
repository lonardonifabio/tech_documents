This report, "The 2026 State of AI Agents Report," examines the current and future landscape of AI agent deployment within enterprises. It details how AI agents are transitioning from experimental technologies to core production infrastructure, fundamentally altering organizational needs for AI. The report highlights a shift from simple task automation to strategic impact, moving beyond single-function pilots towards cross-functional deployments. A key focus is on the requirements for AI agents handling proprietary data, including security, regulatory compliance, and resilience against adversarial attacks like jailbreaks. The research, conducted in partnership with Material, surveyed over 500 technical leaders in the United States, representing diverse company sizes and industries. The findings reveal a technology in transition, emphasizing the move towards autonomous workflows and agentic systems capable of handling complex, multi-step processes, such as multi-step coding workflows and cross-functional business processes. The report anticipates a significant increase in AI agent adoption by 2026, driven by the need for agents that can deliver strategic impact and operate securely within regulated environments. The research explores the evolving use cases of AI agents and the methods for measuring their return on investment (ROI), indicating a growing emphasis on quantifying the value of these automated systems. The document provides an outlook and perspectives on the future of AI agents, suggesting a continued evolution of technology and its integration into various business operations.
