AI Act Guide Version 1.1

Authors: Wes Cockx, Google DeepMind / BetterImages of AI / AI large language models

This document, the AI Act Guide Version 1.1, serves as a supplementary resource designed to provide accessible understanding of the European Union’s AI Act. Prepared as a tool for individuals and organizations developing or considering the use of AI systems, it clarifies key aspects of the regulation. Crucially, it emphasizes that this guide does not create any legal rights or obligations; the official AI Act text always holds precedence. The guide focuses on providing insight into the AI Act's scope, which governs Artificial Intelligence across the entire EU. The regulation’s primary objective is to safeguard the safety, health, and fundamental rights of individuals. It establishes rules for responsible AI development and deployment by businesses, government entities, and other organizations. At the time of publication, the AI Act addressed the definition of AI, specifically identified prohibited AI practices, and provided guidance on General Purpose AI models. The guide encourages feedback to be submitted to ai-verordening@minezk.nl to facilitate improvements in future versions. Furthermore, it directs readers to ondernemersplein.nl for the most current version of the document and links to the latest guidelines from the European Commission. The document highlights the importance of adhering to the AI Act to ensure responsible AI practices and protect fundamental rights within the EU.
