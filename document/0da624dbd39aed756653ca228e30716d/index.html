National Artificial Intelligence Strategy

Authors: Federal Ministry of Communication, Innovation and Digital Economy (FMCIDE)

This document outlines the National Artificial Intelligence Strategy, developed by the Federal Ministry of Communication, Innovation and Digital Economy (FMCIDE) in September 2025. The strategy acknowledges significant contributions from various stakeholders including the Lagos Business School, Data Science Nigeria, the National Information Technology Development Agency (NITDA), the Nigeria Communications Commission (NCC), Galaxy Backbone (GBB), and other key organizations within Nigeria. The core of the strategy centers around recognizing the transformative potential of Artificial Intelligence (AI) and its implications for Nigeria's development, particularly within the African context. It identifies AI as a driver of global innovation and a key factor in reshaping industries and nations. The strategy focuses on leveraging AI to address critical socio-economic challenges faced by Nigeria, capitalizing on the country's position as the most populous nation on the continent. The document is structured around strategic objectives and pillars, incorporating risk mitigation strategies. It details a forward-looking approach to AI adoption, aiming to foster innovation, economic growth, and societal progress through responsible and strategic implementation. The strategy’s development involved extensive consultations and feedback collection, highlighting a collaborative effort to shape Nigeria’s AI future. The document’s appendices provide supporting information and further details on the strategic framework. The overarching goal is to position Nigeria as a leader in AI development and application, contributing to the nation’s overall advancement and competitiveness.
