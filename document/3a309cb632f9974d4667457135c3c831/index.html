Upstream, downstream, and in between: Navigating the GPAI value chain under EU Law

Authors: Martin Ebers, Emmanuel Vargas Penagos

This article delves into the complexities of the GPAI (Generative AI) value chain within the context of European Union law, specifically focusing on the AI Act. It begins with an introduction outlining the risks and opportunities presented by General Purpose AI (GPAI) and then details the structure of the GPAI value chain, encompassing upstream, downstream, and intermediate stages. The document critically examines the regulatory responses within the EU, particularly concerning the AI Act, and highlights the dynamic nature of these value chains as they evolve. A key focus is on the obligations placed upon GPAI model providers, differentiating between those offering general models and those posing systemic risks. The analysis then shifts to the responsibilities of downstream providers who utilize these models, exploring the challenges and requirements associated with integrating GPAI models into new applications. The article provides a structured approach to understanding the legal landscape surrounding GPAI, aiming to guide stakeholders through the obligations and considerations under the AI Act. It emphasizes the need for careful navigation of the value chain to ensure compliance and mitigate potential risks associated with the deployment of Generative AI technologies within the EU framework. The document is a valuable resource for legal professionals, AI developers, and businesses operating in the European market seeking to understand and comply with the evolving regulations governing GPAI.
