Mapping the Potential: Generative AI and Public Sector Work

Authors: Youmna Hashem, Jonathan Bright, Sukankana Chakraborty, Kate Onslow, Anton Poletayev, John Francis, Saba Esnaashari

This report, published in June 2025, investigates the potential for generative AI adoption within Great Britain’s public sector. Utilizing time use data from the Office for National Statistics (ONS), the research identifies specific opportunities for AI implementation. The study, conducted by The Alan Turing Institute, leverages data from the ONS’ public sector time use survey. Key contributors include researchers from The Alan Turing Institute, Pattrn Analytics & Intelligence Ltd, and the Office for National Statistics. The report’s findings were rigorously reviewed by the ONS’ time use and household satellite team, public sector time use survey working group, Department for Transport’s AI team, and the Chief Scientific Advisor. The research aims to provide actionable insights for policymakers regarding the strategic deployment of generative AI technologies within the public sector, focusing on optimizing workforce productivity and service delivery through a data-driven approach. The report’s methodology relies heavily on the ONS’s extensive time use data, offering a robust foundation for assessing the feasibility and impact of AI solutions. The collaboration between The Alan Turing Institute, the Office for National Statistics, and the Department for Transport underscores a commitment to evidence-based policymaking in the age of artificial intelligence. The use of CC BY -SA 4.0 licensed artwork adds to the open and accessible nature of the research.
