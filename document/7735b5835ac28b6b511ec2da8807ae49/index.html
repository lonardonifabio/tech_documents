How People Around the World View AI

Authors: Jacob Poushter, Moira Fagan, Manolo Corichi

This Pew Research Center report, released in October 2025, examines global public opinion regarding artificial intelligence (AI). The study reveals a significant shift in sentiment, with more individuals expressing concern than excitement about AI's development and deployment. A key finding highlights a notable decline in trust in the United States and China to effectively regulate AI, favoring the European Union's approach. The research surveyed public opinion across 25 countries within the Asia-Pacific region, focusing on awareness of AI technology and the associated feelings of concern or excitement. The report delves into the reasons behind this cautious approach, likely influenced by anxieties surrounding job displacement, ethical considerations, and the potential misuse of AI. It’s important to note that Pew Research Center operates as a nonpartisan, nonadvocacy fact tank, providing data-driven research across a broad range of topics, including technology, politics, social trends, and international affairs. Their methodology incorporates public opinion polling, demographic research, computational social science, and data science techniques. The study’s findings contribute to a broader understanding of the global perception of AI, which is increasingly seen as a transformative technology with significant societal implications. The report’s release in 2025 suggests a period of heightened scrutiny and debate surrounding AI’s trajectory, reflecting growing public awareness and a desire for responsible governance. The research methodology emphasizes a comprehensive approach, considering diverse cultural contexts and socioeconomic factors that shape individual attitudes towards AI. The report’s findings are intended to inform policymakers, researchers, and the public about the evolving landscape of AI perceptions and the need for proactive strategies to mitigate potential risks and maximize the benefits of this rapidly advancing technology. The data-driven research conducted by Pew Research Center provides valuable insights into the complex relationship between technology and society, particularly as AI continues to reshape industries and daily life. The report’s focus on the Asia-Pacific region is particularly relevant given the region's significant economic growth and increasing investment in AI development.
