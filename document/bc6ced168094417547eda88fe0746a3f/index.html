AI’s Next Frontier for Financial Services Leaders

Authors: From discovery

This report examines the accelerating adoption of artificial intelligence (AI) within the financial services industry, specifically focusing on the shift from generative AI to agentic AI. Initially, financial institutions experimented with AI tools primarily for prompt-based assistance, but the sector is now experiencing a significant wave of adoption centered around agentic AI – systems designed to autonomously plan, decide, and execute multi-step workflows. Over half of financial services executives are currently utilizing AI agents to varying degrees, indicating a rapid and substantial change. The report highlights that this transition represents a new frontier for AI in finance, demanding a strategic vision alongside disciplined change management to fully realize the potential. It analyzes the key growth drivers behind this adoption, including the investment in generative AI for operational efficiency and customer interaction improvements. Furthermore, it explores the emerging economies that are being shaped by AI's evolution from simple assistance to autonomous action. The document emphasizes that successfully navigating this shift will unlock unparalleled value for both financial institutions and their customers, ultimately transforming the future of the industry. The core argument is that the industry is moving beyond simply 'helping' with AI, towards AI systems that can independently perform complex tasks and workflows, representing a fundamental change in how financial services are delivered and managed.
