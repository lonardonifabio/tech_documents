Frequently Asked Questions Data Act

Authors: European Commission

This document, dated 12 September 2025, Version 1.3, presents a set of Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs) related to the Data Act (Regulation (EU) 2023/2584). It was published approximately a year before the Data Act's entry into application and serves as a resource for stakeholders involved in implementing the legal provisions. The Data Act aims to establish harmonized, horizontal rules to ensure fairness in the allocation of value generated from data across market actors, while protecting those who invest in data-generation technologies. The FAQs are the result of extensive stakeholder interactions and are intended to be a ‘living document,’ subject to updates as needed. Crucially, this document is not an official position of the European Commission and does not extend or modify existing legislation. The responses provided are for informational purposes only and do not constitute authoritative statements or predetermine future actions. The document encourages users to contact the European Commission with any unanswered questions and provides guidance for filing complaints related to the Data Act’s implementation. The core purpose of the Data Act is to promote a fair and transparent data ecosystem, addressing concerns about value capture and investment in data-generation technologies. This FAQ document is a key tool for navigating the complexities of the regulation and ensuring compliance among stakeholders.
