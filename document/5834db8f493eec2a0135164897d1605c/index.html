The AI Policy Playbook: Navigating AI policy development through an African and Asian lens

Authors: Aubra Anthony (Carnegie Endowment for International Peace), Deshni Govender, Nadine Dammaschk, Golo Rademacher, Kim Willet, Gustavo Fonseca Ribeiro, Sheila Kibughi

This document, titled 'The AI Policy Playbook,' provides insights into navigating AI policy development with a specific focus on the African and Asian contexts. Developed with support from FAIR Forward, GIZ, and the Africa-Asia AI Policymaker Network, it represents a deep dive into challenges and proposes solutions based on key findings. The playbook is a collaborative effort, drawing expertise from various organizations and individuals including Carnegie Endowment for International Peace, GIZ, and the Africa-Asia AI Policymaker Network. It highlights the need for policy development informed by the unique circumstances and priorities of African and Asian nations. The document emphasizes a practical approach, offering a framework for addressing the complexities of AI policy. It’s a resource designed to assist policymakers in formulating effective strategies for responsible AI adoption and governance. The content is underpinned by a commitment to open access, licensed under the Attribution 4.0 International license, encouraging proper citation and attribution of the source material. The production and design of the document were handled by Design for development, and the authors include a diverse group of AI advisors and experts from various organizations, reflecting a global perspective on AI policy challenges and opportunities. The document's creation is supported by a network of stakeholders dedicated to fostering responsible AI development and deployment.
