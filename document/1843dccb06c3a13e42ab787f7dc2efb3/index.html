AI as a Healthcare Ally: How Americans are navigating the system with ChatGPT

Authors: OpenAI

This document analyzes the growing trend of Americans utilizing ChatGPT for healthcare-related inquiries and navigation. Key findings reveal a significant portion of ChatGPT's global conversations – over 5% – are focused on healthcare, translating to billions of messages weekly. Within OpenAI's user base of over 800 million regular users, approximately one in four submits a healthcare prompt each week, and more than 40 million individuals turn to ChatGPT daily with healthcare questions. The research highlights a widespread dissatisfaction with the US healthcare system, evidenced by low patient satisfaction scores (C+ for access, D+ for costs) and a significant percentage (70%) believing the system faces major problems or a crisis. Further investigation reveals that three in five Americans perceive the current system as 'broken,' citing hospital costs (87%), poor healthcare access (77%), and a shortage of nurses (75%) as critical issues. ChatGPT is emerging as a valuable ally for both patients and providers, empowering individuals to self-advocate, navigate the complex healthcare system, and ultimately, support better healthcare outcomes. The document underscores the potential of AI to address a persistent and worsening pain point within the American healthcare landscape, offering a means for individuals to gain greater control and understanding within a system often perceived as confusing and challenging. The analysis suggests a growing reliance on AI-powered tools to bridge the gap between patients and the healthcare system, particularly in addressing concerns related to access, cost, and quality of care. The data presented points to a significant shift in how Americans are seeking information and support regarding their health, leveraging the accessibility and conversational capabilities of ChatGPT.
