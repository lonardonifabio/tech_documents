EU AI Act Fundamental Rights Impact Assessments (FRIAs): An Overview

Authors: Sean Musch, Michael Borrelli, Charles Kerrigan, Jessica Chapplow, Elena Knezovic, Susanne Chishti, Andrey Prozorov

This report, published by AI & Partners, provides an overview of Fundamental Rights Impact Assessments (FRIAs) in the context of the EU AI Act. AI & Partners is an organization dedicated to defending and extending the digital rights of users globally, particularly those at risk, through a multifaceted approach encompassing technical support, policy engagement, advocacy, regulatory interventions, and participation in industry groups like AI Commons. The core mission is to ensure AI development and deployment prioritize responsible innovation and minimize potential harm, recognizing the risks associated with uncontrolled AI systems. Specifically, the report highlights the dangers of unknown AI adoption, which can lead to confusion, risk, and reputational damage. AI & Partners aims to mitigate these risks by enabling safe AI usage for organizations and their clients, offering tools for identification, monitoring, and governance of all AI systems. This proactive approach allows organizations to move from reactive responses to leading AI development, fostering trust and long-term innovation. The organization’s philosophy centers on the belief that AI should unlock potential rather than cause harm, emphasizing the importance of responsible handling and control. The document details the organization's activities and contact information, reinforcing their commitment to safeguarding digital rights in the age of artificial intelligence. The report's focus on FRIAs suggests a proactive stance on anticipating and addressing potential ethical and legal challenges posed by AI technologies.
