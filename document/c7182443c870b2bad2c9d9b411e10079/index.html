COMMUNICATION FROM THE COMMISSION TO THE EUROPEAN PARLIAMENT AND THE COUNCIL - Apply AI Strategy

Authors: European Commission

This document, COM(2025) 723 final, released by the European Commission on October 8, 2025, outlines the ‘Apply AI Strategy’ designed to strategically leverage Artificial Intelligence within the European Union. The strategy recognizes the rapid advancement of AI technologies, including advanced general-purpose AI models, AI agents, digital twins, and specialized small AI models, as a significant opportunity for the EU. Despite a robust industrial base and a thriving startup environment, AI adoption remains limited, with only 13.5% of businesses and 12.6% of SMEs utilizing these technologies. The strategy builds upon the AI Continent Action Plan and the EU’s commitment to human-centric and trustworthy AI, aligning with the AI Act. The core objective is to significantly increase AI adoption across the EU, particularly among SMEs and small mid-caps, to drive productivity growth and enhance the competitiveness of European industries, especially those considered strategically important. Specifically, the strategy aims to foster vertical integration by facilitating AI integration within various sectors. Furthermore, it seeks to unlock the broader societal benefits of AI, including advancements in areas like healthcare diagnostics, by promoting the responsible and ethical development and deployment of AI technologies. The document emphasizes a proactive approach to ensure the EU remains at the forefront of AI innovation and maintains its technological sovereignty. The strategy represents a key initiative to transform the EU's economic landscape and address the challenges and opportunities presented by the rapidly evolving AI landscape. The document highlights the need for a coordinated effort across the EU to maximize the benefits of AI while mitigating potential risks.
