AI Applications in BRIDGE Projects

Authors: BRIDGE Project Team, European Commission

This document outlines an action focused on analyzing the application of Artificial Intelligence (AI) within the BRIDGE project portfolio. The primary objective is to comprehensively assess the current state of AI development and utilization across these projects, detailing their maturity levels, associated benefits, and inherent limitations. A key component involves understanding the specific data and infrastructure requirements necessary for successful AI deployment within the context of energy systems. The action seeks to gather practical experience, including insights into challenges encountered and factors contributing to project success, to inform future collaboration and the development of robust frameworks. The initiative directly aligns with the European Commission’s Digitalisation of Energy Action Plan, which prioritizes AI as a central element of the EU’s digital energy transition. This plan emphasizes the need for standardized and trustworthy AI systems, reflecting a broader global trend recognizing AI's potential to enhance the reliability and efficiency of energy systems. Specifically, the document highlights applications of AI in areas such as utility operations, asset management, predictive analytics, and customer service. Furthermore, it acknowledges the critical challenges – encompassing operational, regulatory, and ethical considerations – that must be addressed to facilitate the scalable implementation of AI. The document draws upon existing Horizon Europe and Horizon 2020 projects that have actively leveraged AI for tasks like energy forecasting, grid flexibility coordination, market design, and energy data sharing. Ultimately, the action aims to consolidate practical knowledge gained from the BRIDGE projects, identifying common barriers and enablers, and providing actionable recommendations for future AI initiatives within the energy sector. The document's focus on standardization and trustworthiness reflects a commitment to responsible AI development and deployment, aligning with broader strategic goals for the EU’s energy transition.
