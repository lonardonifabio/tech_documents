DPIA on EduGenAI

Authors: Sjoera Nas, Jacob Gursky

This document presents a Data Protection Impact Assessment (DPIA) specifically focused on the EduGenAI system. EduGenAI is an AI-powered web application designed to provide users with secure and privacy-conscious access to various generative AI tools. Functioning as a privacy proxy, it bridges the gap between users and different generative AI services, including both locally hosted large language models and cloud-based providers. A key feature of EduGenAI is its ability to offer multimodal access, allowing users to seamlessly switch between different AI systems during a prompt dialogue. Furthermore, the system incorporates a robust filtering mechanism to mask personal data, safeguarding user privacy and the rights of individuals mentioned within the prompts. The application’s architecture includes access to one or more search engines, enabling users to ‘update’ the generated information. The DPIA highlights the importance of mitigating potential privacy risks associated with the use of generative AI, particularly concerning data protection and compliance with relevant regulations. The document details the steps taken to assess and address these risks, emphasizing the system's design choices and implemented safeguards. The report’s evolution reflects a collaborative process involving SURF, with multiple revisions and refinements based on feedback and final checks. The final public version represents a polished and reformatted document, suitable for distribution and review. The core objective of the DPIA is to ensure that EduGenAI operates in a manner that respects data protection principles and minimizes the potential for harm to individuals.
