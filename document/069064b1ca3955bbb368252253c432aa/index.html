First Draft Code of Practice on Transparency of AI-Generated Content

Authors: Kalina Bontcheva, Anja Bechmann, Dino Pedreschi, Giovanni De Gregorio, Christian Riess, Madalina Botan

This document presents the initial draft of a Code of Practice concerning the transparency of AI-generated content, specifically developed under the framework of the EU's AI Act. The Code aims to establish guidelines for both providers and deployers of generative AI systems. It focuses on marking and detecting AI-generated and manipulated content, as well as the labeling of deepfakes and AI-generated text. The document is structured around two key sections: Section 1 outlines the rules applicable to providers of generative AI systems, referencing Article 50(2) and (5) of the AI Act. Section 2 details the rules for labeling deepfakes and AI-generated/manipulated text, targeting deployers of AI systems also in accordance with Article 50(4) and (5) of the AI Act. The introductory statements by the Chairs and Vice-Chairs emphasize the importance of this first draft and its alignment with the overarching goals of the AI Act, which seeks to ensure transparency and accountability in the development and deployment of AI technologies. The document serves as a foundational step in establishing standards for responsible AI content generation, mitigating potential risks associated with deceptive or misleading AI-produced material. The working group structure, comprised of two working groups with chairs and vice-chairs, suggests a collaborative approach to developing this code of practice. The document's focus on practical implementation and adherence to the AI Act highlights a commitment to regulatory compliance and ethical considerations within the rapidly evolving field of generative AI. Further development and refinement of this code are anticipated based on feedback and ongoing advancements in AI technology.
