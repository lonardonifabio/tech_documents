CockroachDB: The Definitive Guide

Authors: Guy Harrison, Jesse Seldess, Ben Darnell, Rob Reid

This comprehensive guide, now in its second edition, delves into CockroachDB, a distributed SQL database designed for scaling applications to handle massive workloads. The book begins with a historical overview of database evolution, starting with pre-relational databases and the rise of the relational model, highlighting the limitations of traditional RDBMS systems in the context of the internet and the emergence of NoSQL databases. It then explores the key drivers behind the creation of CockroachDB, emphasizing its design goals centered around resilience, scalability, and data consistency. The core of the book focuses on CockroachDB's architecture, detailing the cluster architecture, the software stack, and the underlying SQL layer. A significant portion is dedicated to explaining how CockroachDB translates SQL queries into operations within its key-value store, illustrating the internal representation of tables and column families, and the use of indexes for efficient data retrieval. The guide provides real-world examples of CockroachDB's adoption by prominent companies like Netflix, Devsisters, DoorDash, Bose, Form3, Hard Rock Digital, Spreedly, and Route, showcasing its capabilities in diverse applications. The book aims to equip readers with a deep understanding of CockroachDB's architecture and operational considerations, making it a valuable resource for developers and database administrators seeking to build highly scalable and resilient applications. The second edition incorporates updates and refinements based on the latest CockroachDB releases, ensuring readers have access to the most current information. It covers topics such as transactions, the SQL language, and the NoSQL movement, providing a holistic view of the database landscape and CockroachDB's position within it. The book's structure is designed to progressively build understanding, starting with foundational concepts and moving towards more advanced topics related to distributed data management.
