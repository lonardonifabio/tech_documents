AI Agents in Action: Foundations for Evaluation and Governance

Authors: World Economic Forum, Capgemini

This white paper, published by the World Economic Forum in November 2025, delves into the foundational aspects of evaluating and governing AI agents. It addresses the evolving technical landscape of AI agents, starting with the software architecture, communication protocols, and crucial cybersecurity considerations. The document meticulously outlines the necessary foundations for robust AI agent evaluation and governance, encompassing classification methodologies, detailed evaluation frameworks, comprehensive risk assessments, and a progressive governance approach. A key focus is on establishing clear guidelines and processes for assessing the performance, safety, and ethical implications of AI agents. The paper recognizes the increasing trend towards multi-agent ecosystems and explores the challenges and opportunities presented by these complex systems. It emphasizes the importance of proactive risk management and the development of adaptable governance structures to ensure responsible AI agent deployment. The white paper’s collaborative nature, facilitated and endorsed by the World Economic Forum, highlights a broad understanding of the field, aiming to provide a comprehensive resource for organizations navigating the complexities of AI agent development and implementation. The document’s scope extends beyond simple technical specifications, advocating for a holistic approach that integrates technical, operational, and ethical considerations. Furthermore, it suggests a phased approach to governance, acknowledging that the requirements for AI agent oversight will evolve as the technology matures and its applications expand. The inclusion of cybersecurity considerations underscores the critical need to protect AI agents and the systems they interact with from potential threats. Ultimately, the paper seeks to establish a framework for organizations to confidently and responsibly harness the power of AI agents, promoting innovation while mitigating associated risks. The document’s structure – beginning with technical foundations and progressing to governance considerations – reflects a systematic approach to understanding and managing this rapidly developing technology. It’s a valuable resource for anyone involved in the design, deployment, or oversight of AI agents, offering insights into best practices and future trends.
