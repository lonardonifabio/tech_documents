How agentic AI can change the way banks fight financial crime

Authors: Alexander Verhagen, Angela Luget, Olivia Conjeaud, Vasiliki Stergiou, Debanjan Banerjee

This document explores the challenges banks face in combating financial crime, highlighting the significant investment in Know Your Customer (KYC) and Anti-Money Laundering (AML) activities with limited success. Despite increasing spending – up to 10% annually in some advanced markets between 2015 and 2022 – the financial industry only detects approximately 2% of global financial crime flows. The article proposes agentic AI as a potential solution. Agentic AI represents an evolution of analytical AI, offering automation and productivity across the entire client lifecycle. The root of the problem lies in inefficiencies within banking operating models and workflows. The document draws upon research from QuantumBlack, AI by McKinsey, and McKinsey’s Financial Services and Risk & Resilience Practices. It references a 2022 report from LexisNexis Risk Solutions and a 2024 McKinsey KYC/AML benchmark study. The core argument is that by leveraging agentic AI's capabilities, banks can significantly improve their ability to detect and prevent financial crime, ultimately leading to a better return on investment in compliance efforts. The document emphasizes the need for a shift in approach, moving beyond traditional analytical AI to a more proactive and adaptive system.

Key Concepts:

  • AI-based solutions may be an accelerator in fighting financial crime.
  • Agentic AI offers automation and productivity throughout the client life cycle.
  • Banks are struggling to achieve a good return on investment in KYC/AML activities.
  • Redesigning operating models for AI-driven workflows.
  • Utilizing agentic AI squads for specific process steps.
  • Event-driven digital customer due diligence
