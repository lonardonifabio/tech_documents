AI Ecosystem Brief 2025 Edition Cyprus

Authors: Sean Musch, Michael Borrelli, Charles Kerrigan, Mariano Kostelec, Panis Pieri, Alexia Solomou, Christina Orfanidou, Annetta Benzar, Orfhlaith (Orla) Ni Chorcora, Kostiantyn Koshelenko, George Polemidiotis, Nikolas Xenofontos, Iosif Konstantinou, Panis Pieri

This document, the "AI Ecosystem Brief 2025 Edition Cyprus," published by AI & Partners in August 2025, outlines the organization's mission and approach to safeguarding digital rights within the evolving artificial intelligence landscape. AI & Partners focuses on defending users globally who are at risk due to the potential misuse of AI. The report highlights the organization's multi-faceted strategy, encompassing direct technical support, comprehensive policy engagement, global advocacy, grassroots professional services, regulatory interventions, and participation in industry groups like AI Commons. A core tenet of AI & Partners' work is the belief that AI should be a tool for unlocking potential rather than causing harm. The organization emphasizes the importance of responsible AI development and deployment, drawing attention to the negative consequences of losing control over AI systems. The brief details the organization's commitment to proactive measures, including regulatory interventions, to mitigate risks and promote ethical AI practices. It’s a publication designed to inform stakeholders about AI & Partners' role in navigating the complexities of the AI ecosystem and advocating for user rights. The document provides contact information for Michael Charles Borrelli, Director, and the organization's website for further details. The report’s purpose is to establish AI & Partners as a key player in ensuring responsible AI development and protecting users’ digital rights in the face of emerging AI technologies.

Key Concepts:

  • AI & Partners defends and extends the digital rights of users at risk around the world.
  • AI & Partners believes AI should unlock potential — not cause harm.
  • The organization aims to help you build AI responsibly.
  • Cyprus adheres to GDPR and its domestic implementation, ensuring data protection and limiting profiling.
  • AI governance strategy emphasizes ethical deployment, SME enablement, and digitally inclusive growth.
  • Legal alignment and sustainable innovation are key components of Cyprus’s AI strategy.
  • AI strategy development and implementation
  • Support for AI-driven entrepreneurship through investment networks and business angel communities
  • Bridging gaps in AI know-how through applied research collaborations and technical assistance
  • Developing a future-ready workforce through talent development and lifelong learning initiatives
