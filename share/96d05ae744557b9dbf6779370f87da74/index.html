Data Act Amendments

Authors: European Parliament

This document excerpt details the amendments adopted by the European Parliament on 14 March 2023 concerning the Data Act. The Data Act is a proposed regulation aimed at establishing harmonized rules for fair access to and use of data. The amendments were triggered by a referral back to the committee responsible, following Rule 59(4) of the ordinary legislative procedure. The European Parliament's changes are reflected within the document, with new or amended text highlighted in bold italics and deletions indicated by the symbol ▌. The process involved consideration of opinions from the European Economic and Social Committee and the Committee of the Regions. The document outlines the legal basis for the regulation, referencing the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union, specifically Article 114. It signifies a key stage in the development of the Data Act, representing the European Parliament's input and adjustments to the initial Commission proposal (COM(2022)0068 – C9-0051/2022 – 2022/0047(COD)). The document’s purpose is to provide a record of the amendments and the legislative process undertaken by the European Parliament, ultimately shaping the final form of the Data Act regulation. The reference to the ordinary legislative procedure highlights the collaborative nature of EU legislation, involving the Parliament, the Council, and the Commission.
