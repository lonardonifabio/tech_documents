Certification Scheme for Model Risk Management

Authors: ForHumanity Inc.

This document outlines a certification scheme developed by ForHumanity Inc., a 501(c)(3) public charity, specifically focused on Model Risk Management (MRM). The scheme aims to establish a robust framework for assessing and certifying ‘AAA Systems’ – systems that may or may not utilize Artificial Intelligence (AI). The core of the scheme centers around a modular approach, mirroring financial audit methodologies, to ensure comprehensive jurisdictional compliance. It details the scope of the certification, including requirements for modularity and validity periods. The document meticulously defines the target of evaluation, the determination process, and the territorial scope of the certification. A key element is the ‘AAA System Procurement’ section, suggesting a process for selecting and validating these systems. Furthermore, it emphasizes the importance of expert oversight, top management involvement, and adherence to relevant legal frameworks. The scheme incorporates ethical considerations, including consumer protection, data privacy, and the need for explainability and transparency when interacting with AI subjects. Human oversight and interaction are central to the process. The document stresses the need for robust data management and governance, alongside a focus on interoperability with existing standards. The certification process is designed to provide assurance regarding the quality and reliability of the AAA Systems, ultimately contributing to responsible AI adoption and mitigating potential risks. The scheme’s modular design allows for adaptability and scalability, reflecting the evolving landscape of AI and its integration into various systems. The document’s creation and distribution are governed by ForHumanity Inc., and all rights are reserved. The scheme’s development reflects a commitment to responsible innovation and the integration of AI within a framework of rigorous governance and ethical considerations. The certification process is intended to build trust and confidence in the use of AAA Systems, promoting transparency and accountability.

Key Concepts:

  • A certification scheme for Model Risk Management utilizing a modular approach.
  • The replication of a financial audit (AAA System) to assess financial systems.
  • The importance of governance and compliance within AAA Systems.
  • The role of human oversight and interaction with AI systems.
  • Operationalizing governance and accountability for public sector providers of AAA Systems.
  • Leverageable governance and oversight through ForHumanity certification schemes.
  • Risk management within the context of AI systems, specifically focusing on Algorithmic Risk Committee and Top Management and Oversight Bodies.
  • Moral obligations and rights of humans and nature
  • Committee accountability and oversight
  • Risk assessment for AI systems
