The AI Leadership Blueprint: A Practical Guide to Responsible Workforce Transformation

Authors: Jill Angerbauer, Penny Atkins, Jeb Dean, Meghan Dovick, Robin Huling, Mary Killela, JUDr. Nora Kurzova, Jimmy McDonough, Seneca Perri Moore, Christian Napier, Rebecca Peterson, Anneliese Pixton, Olivia Sanders, Carolyn Scheese, Jerome Soller, David Stringfellow, Kevin Williams

This document, "The AI Leadership Blueprint," provides a practical guide focused on responsible workforce transformation in the context of artificial intelligence. Authored by a diverse group of experts from various institutions including Harvard, the University of Utah, and the State of Utah, the blueprint addresses the strategic considerations and operational steps needed to successfully integrate AI technologies while mitigating potential risks. The authors leverage a combination of human expertise and AI-assisted tools, specifically utilizing Large Language Models (LLMs) such as OpenAI's ChatGPT, Tropics, Claude, Google's Gemini, Perplexity, and others, to generate the content. The blueprint emphasizes a holistic approach, encompassing not only technological implementation but also the necessary organizational and cultural shifts. It’s designed to guide leaders in navigating the complexities of AI adoption, ensuring that workforce transformations are aligned with ethical considerations and strategic goals. The document’s creation involved extensive research and consultation, aiming to provide actionable insights for organizations seeking to harness the power of AI responsibly. The content presented represents the collective viewpoints of the authors and does not constitute official policy or endorsement from any affiliated organizations. The document's core purpose is to offer a framework for proactive and strategic AI leadership, promoting a future where AI and human talent work together effectively.
