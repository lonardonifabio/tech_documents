National AI Plan 2025

Authors: Australian Government Department of Industry, Science and Resources

The National AI Plan 2025 is a strategic document outlining Australia's approach to artificial intelligence development and implementation. Published by the Australian Government Department of Industry, Science and Resources, the plan aims to foster innovation, economic growth, and societal benefit through responsible AI adoption. It establishes a framework for research, development, and deployment of AI technologies across various sectors, including healthcare, manufacturing, agriculture, and education. The plan emphasizes ethical considerations, safety standards, and workforce development to ensure AI systems are aligned with Australian values and priorities. Key components include investments in AI research, support for AI startups and businesses, and initiatives to address potential challenges such as bias, transparency, and accountability. The document details specific goals and targets related to AI adoption rates, research funding, and skills development. It also highlights the importance of collaboration between government, industry, and academia to drive progress in the field.
