Latvian Artificial Intelligence Strategy and Legal Framework

This document excerpt outlines Latvia's approach to Artificial Intelligence development, primarily driven by the "Artificial Intelligence Centre Law" adopted on March 6, 2025. The core objective is to establish a comprehensive legal framework for AI collaboration involving public, private, and academic entities, ensuring ethical, responsible, and secure AI applications. The Latvian National Artificial Intelligence Centre, under the guidance of the Ministry of Environmental Protection and Regional Development (VARAM), plays a crucial role in implementing the EU AI Act, signifying a strong governmental commitment. Latvia’s strategy adopts a context-aware approach, prioritizing AI adoption across the entire economy, with a particular emphasis on public administration. The national AI strategy from 2020 serves as the foundational document, emphasizing societal benefits and economic growth through AI. Although the excerpt doesn't explicitly mention "AI Sandboxes," the overall strategy’s focus on innovation within a secure and ethical environment suggests a similar intention to encourage responsible AI development. Key elements of Latvia’s framework include embedding AI governance within public administration and broader economic sectors, fostering partnerships, and prioritizing capacity building. The strategy’s alignment with a robust legal and ethical framework, coupled with collaborative efforts, demonstrates Latvia’s commitment to leveraging AI for economic growth and societal advancement. The document highlights the importance of state-led coordination in the responsible deployment of AI, reflecting a proactive and strategic approach to navigating the complexities of this rapidly evolving technology. The overarching goal is to create a sustainable and beneficial AI ecosystem within Latvia, aligning with European Union regulations and promoting innovation while mitigating potential risks.
