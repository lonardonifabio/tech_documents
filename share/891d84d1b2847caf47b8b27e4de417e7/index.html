Model AI Governance Framework vs. EU AI Act A Mapping Exercise

Authors: Sean Musch, Michael Borrelli, Charles Kerrigan, Dr. Nadia Morozova, Mark Minevich

This report, published by AI & Partners, provides a mapping exercise comparing the Model AI Governance Framework with the EU AI Act. It highlights the critical need for robust AI governance to mitigate risks associated with uncontrolled AI adoption, including confusion, reputational damage, and potential harm. The document emphasizes AI & Partners' mission to assist organizations in building and deploying AI systems that are trustworthy and responsible, focusing on tools for identification, monitoring, and governance. The report underscores the importance of proactive AI governance, particularly in light of the EU AI Act, and advocates for a framework that prioritizes ethical considerations and risk management. AI & Partners’ approach centers on enabling organizations to maintain control over their AI systems, fostering trust and innovation while safeguarding against negative consequences. The organization’s work involves direct technical support, policy engagement, global advocacy, and participation in industry groups like AI Commons, all aimed at defending digital rights and promoting responsible AI practices. The document positions AI & Partners as a key resource for navigating the complexities of AI governance and ensuring alignment with evolving regulatory landscapes. It’s a strategic document designed to guide organizations toward sustainable and ethical AI deployments.

Key Concepts:

  • AI & Partners defends and extends the digital rights of users at risk around the world.
  • AI should unlock potential — not cause harm.
  • We help you take control with tools to identify, monitor, and govern all AI.
  • Provenance as a signal of trustworthiness
  • Framework’s approach to balancing innovation and safety
  • Iterative improvements and flexible standards in AI governance
