Draft Guidance Article 73 AI Act - Incident Reporting (High -Risk AI Systems)

Authors: European Commission

This document presents draft guidance related to the reporting of incidents involving high-risk AI systems, specifically in the context of the European Commission's proposed AI Act. The guidance, dated September 2025, focuses on establishing a framework for identifying, documenting, and reporting incidents that may arise from the operation of these systems. It aims to ensure accountability and transparency surrounding the use of high-risk AI, contributing to the overall safety and ethical considerations outlined in the AI Act. The document begins with a background section outlining the objectives of the guidance. It then proceeds to define key terms, including ‘AI Act’, ‘Incident’, and ‘Malfunction’, clarifying the scope of reporting requirements. Specifically, it addresses the determination of whether an incident was ‘directly or indirectly’ caused by the AI system, a crucial element in establishing liability and responsibility. The guidance is intended to provide practical tools and standards for organizations deploying high-risk AI systems, facilitating compliance with the AI Act's requirements for risk management and mitigation. The document's purpose is to proactively address potential harms associated with these systems, promoting responsible innovation and safeguarding public interests. The draft guidance is a critical component of the AI Act's implementation, establishing a mechanism for continuous monitoring and evaluation of high-risk AI systems. It’s designed to foster a culture of vigilance and accountability, ultimately contributing to the safe and trustworthy development and deployment of AI technologies within the European Union. The document's creation reflects the EU's commitment to establishing robust regulatory frameworks for AI, prioritizing human safety and ethical considerations. The guidance's development is a key step in translating the AI Act's ambitious goals into actionable procedures.
