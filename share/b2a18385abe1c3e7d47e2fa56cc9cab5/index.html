0 EDPS DPIA Survey 2024 Report

Authors: EDPS

This report, titled '0 EDPS DPIA Survey 2024 Report,' presents an analysis of Data Protection Impact Assessments (DPIAs) conducted by EUIs (European Union Institutions) since 2018. The survey, released on September 8, 2025, focuses on understanding the landscape of DPIA practices within the EU institutions. The document’s primary objective is to evaluate the effectiveness and consistency of DPIA processes, identifying areas for improvement and ensuring compliance with data protection regulations. The report begins with an executive summary (not included in the excerpt) and then delves into the methodology employed for the survey. A key section examines the threshold assessment process, investigating whether DPIAs were conducted and analyzing the criteria identified and potentially missed or misinterpreted during these assessments. Specifically, the survey investigates whether the criteria used aligned with best practices. Furthermore, the report analyzes the number of DPIAs conducted by EUIs since 2018, providing quantitative data on the scale of DPIA activity. A significant portion of the report is dedicated to the methodology and templates used for DPIAs, including the utilization of guidance documents from the European Data Protection Supervisor (EDPS). The document also scrutinizes the existence and application of written procedures for adhering to the EUDPR (European Union Data Protection Regulation) framework. The analysis highlights instances where a written procedure was absent, comparing these practices against established best practices. The report’s findings are intended to inform future DPIA strategies and contribute to a more robust and compliant data protection environment within the EU institutions. The survey’s data collection and analysis are crucial for understanding the current state of DPIA implementation and identifying potential gaps in protection.
