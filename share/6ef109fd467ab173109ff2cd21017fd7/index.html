The GenAI Divide: State of AI in Business 2025

Authors: NANDA Aditya Challapally, Chris Pease, Ramesh Raskar, Pradyumna Chari

This report, published in July 2025, investigates the current state of Generative AI (GenAI) adoption within businesses, focusing on the gap between high investment and limited transformative impact. The research, conducted between January and June 2025, utilizes a robust multi-method approach, combining a systematic review of over 300 publicly disclosed AI initiatives with 52 structured interviews with organizational representatives and survey data collected from 153 senior leaders gathered at four major industry conferences. The core finding is that despite significant investment – estimated between $30 and $40 billion – only a small percentage of organizations are truly leveraging GenAI for substantial business transformation. The report identifies key reasons for this ‘GenAI Divide,’ including a ‘learning gap’ where pilot projects stall due to a lack of understanding and effective implementation strategies. It explores the success factors of organizations that are effectively ‘crossing’ this divide, examining both the approaches of ‘builders’ (those developing GenAI solutions) and ‘buyers’ (those adopting them). The research emphasizes the importance of addressing this gap through targeted learning, strategic implementation, and a clear understanding of how GenAI can drive genuine business value. Crucially, the report employs a rigorous methodology, anonymizing all company-specific data and quotes to maintain neutrality and comply with corporate disclosure policies and confidentiality agreements, preventing any perceived commercial bias. The findings are presented solely by the authors and reviewers, distancing them from any affiliated employer's positions. The document outlines a structured approach to understanding and addressing the challenges and opportunities presented by GenAI in the business landscape.

Key Concepts:

  • The GenAI Divide: A significant gap exists between GenAI adoption and actual business transformation.
  • AI Implementation Research: The report is based on research into AI implementation strategies.
  • Pilot Stall: A learning gap is hindering the success of AI pilot programs.
  • The emergence of 'shadow AI' as a parallel adoption of AI tools by employees.
  • The discrepancy between employee usage of AI tools and official enterprise initiatives.
  • The potential for personal AI tools to deliver better ROI than formal AI deployments.
  • The GenAI Divide: The gap between companies successfully leveraging GenAI and those struggling to do so.
  • Learning from Feedback: A key demand from executives (66%) regarding AI tools.
  • Retaining Context: Another critical requirement (63%) for successful AI systems.
  • Workflow Customization: Starting with customization at workflow edges and scaling into core processes.
