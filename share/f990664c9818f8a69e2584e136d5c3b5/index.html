This document, the "AI Ecosystem Brief 2025 Edition France," is a publication by AI & Partners, released in September 2025. It outlines the organization's mission to defend and extend the digital rights of users globally who are at risk due to the development and deployment of artificial intelligence. AI & Partners operates by combining technical support, policy engagement, advocacy, professional services, regulatory interventions, and participation in industry groups like AI Commons. The core belief driving their work is that AI should be a force for unlocking potential rather than causing harm. They highlight the negative consequences of losing control over AI systems and emphasize the importance of responsible handling to foster trust and innovation. The report details the organization's approach to navigating the evolving landscape of AI governance, particularly concerning the EU AI Act. AI & Partners’ activities are focused on ensuring ethical and responsible AI development and deployment, advocating for user rights, and contributing to industry standards. The document provides contact information for Michael Charles Borrelli, Director, and the AI & Partners website for further details. The organization’s work is centered around safeguarding digital rights in the age of AI, promoting responsible innovation, and engaging with regulatory bodies to shape the future of AI governance. It’s a strategic overview of AI & Partners’ role within the broader AI ecosystem, emphasizing their commitment to ethical considerations and user protection.
