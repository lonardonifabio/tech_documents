AI Ecosystem Brief 2025 Edition

Authors: Sean Musch, Michael Borrelli, Charles Kerrigan, Mario Brcic, Ph.D., Kristijan Vilibić, PhD Researcher, Elena Knezovic, Sarah Isufi, FHCA, Nat Schoole, William Bello, Neven Dujmovic, Katarina Bulic Bestulic, Kristijan Skarica, Robert Kopal, Ph.D., Assoc. Prof.

This document is the "AI Ecosystem Brief 2025 Edition", a publication produced by AI & Partners. It outlines the organization's mission to defend and extend the digital rights of users at risk globally, leveraging a multi-faceted approach including technical support, policy engagement, advocacy, regulatory interventions, and participation in industry groups like AI Commons. The report was authored by Sean Musch and Michael Borrelli, with contributions from a broader network of experts including Charles Kerrigan, Mario Brcic, Kristijan Vilibić, Elena Knezovic, Sarah Isufi, Nat Schoole, William Bello, Neven Dujmovic, Katarina Bulic Bestulic, Kristijan Skarica, and Robert Kopal. AI & Partners advocates for the responsible development and deployment of AI, emphasizing the importance of mitigating potential harm and unlocking AI's potential for positive impact. The organization's core belief is that AI should empower individuals rather than cause detriment. The document highlights a commitment to active engagement in shaping the future of AI regulation and governance, reflecting a proactive stance within the evolving landscape of AI development and its societal implications. It serves as a foundational piece for understanding AI & Partners' strategic direction and their role in navigating the complexities of the AI ecosystem. The report’s purpose is to showcase the organization’s dedication to digital rights and responsible AI practices, solidifying their position as a key player in the global conversation surrounding AI governance and ethical considerations.

Key Concepts:

  • AI should unlock potential, not cause harm.
  • Protecting digital rights of users at risk.
  • Advocacy and regulatory interventions in the age of AI.
