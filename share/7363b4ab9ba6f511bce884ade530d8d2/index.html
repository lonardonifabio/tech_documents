Dated as of June 30, 2025, it focuses on the role of the Italian Senate in the rapidly evolving field of Artificial Intelligence (AI), particularly generative AI. The document highlights the dual role of parliaments globally – as both regulators and users, and increasingly, as experimental environments. Specifically, the Senate della Repubblica has been actively experimenting with integrating AI technologies to enhance the efficiency of its legislative process, encompassing areas such as document management and workflow optimization. The analysis details the various stages of the legislative process where AI is being tested, aiming to improve operational effectiveness. The document emphasizes the importance of parliamentary involvement in shaping the future of AI, recognizing the need for legislative frameworks to address the challenges and opportunities presented by this technology.
