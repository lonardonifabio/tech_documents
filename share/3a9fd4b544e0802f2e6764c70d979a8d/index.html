EU AI Act Post -Market Monitoring Ongoing Oversight of AI Systems

Authors: Sean Musch, Michael Borrelli, Charles Kerrigan, Valeria Lazzaroli, Priyanka Chatterjee, Alan LIM, MBA, Tim Hillison

This document, published by AI & Partners, focuses on the ongoing post-market monitoring and oversight of AI systems within the context of the EU AI Act. It highlights AI & Partners' mission to defend and extend the digital rights of users at risk globally, particularly concerning the responsible development and deployment of artificial intelligence. The organization advocates for a future where AI unlocks potential rather than causing harm, emphasizing the importance of trust and accountability in AI systems. AI & Partners employs a multi-faceted approach, combining direct technical support, policy engagement, global advocacy, grassroots professional services, regulatory interventions, and participation in industry groups like AI Commons to achieve its goals. The report details the organization's commitment to enabling safe AI usage for both its clients and its own operations, recognizing the potential risks associated with uncontrolled AI development. It underscores the need for proactive oversight and responsible innovation to mitigate potential negative consequences. The document serves as a publication from AI & Partners, detailing their stance on the ethical and regulatory considerations surrounding AI, particularly in light of the EU AI Act. Contact information and a website link are provided for further inquiries. The core message is centered around building and maintaining trust in AI systems, ensuring they are developed and utilized responsibly for the long term.

Key Concepts:

  • AI & Partners defends and extends the digital rights of users at risk.
  • AI should unlock potential — not cause harm.
  • Safe AI usage for organizations and clients.
  • Proportionality in AI regulation based on risk levels.
  • Post-market monitoring requirements for AI systems, particularly for smaller providers.
  • Importance of comprehensive documentation for AI systems, including monitoring plans, data collection, and risk evaluations.
  • Post-market monitoring is crucial for maintaining stakeholder confidence and oversight.
  • Providers are responsible for maintaining comprehensive documentation and system logs related to their AI systems.
  • A robust post-market monitoring system is essential for identifying and addressing compliance issues and potential risks within AI systems.
  • The system design should be proportionate, scalable, and integrated into broader AI governance processes.
