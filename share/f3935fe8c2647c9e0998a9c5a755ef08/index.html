Japanese Government's AI Innovation Strategy and Funding (2025)

Authors: Keiji Tonomura, Yukiko Konno, Minh Thi Cao Koike, Yoshiteru Matsuzaki, Nagashima Ohno & Tsunematsu

This document outlines the Japanese government's ambitious strategy for advancing Artificial Intelligence (AI) innovation, particularly focusing on the fiscal year 2025 and beyond. A core element of this strategy involves substantial financial investment, totaling approximately JPY196.9 billion in 2025, with a long-term goal of JPY10 trillion by 2030 through the "AI and Semiconductor Industry Strengthening Framework." The framework’s initial implementation in 2025 includes JPY40 billion dedicated to fundamental research into innovative AI semiconductors, alongside JPY22 billion allocated to generative AI development for medical diagnostic support within the healthcare sector. Furthermore, the government is actively promoting AI adoption across key industries, including transportation, through targeted financial support. The strategic approach is underpinned by a series of sector-specific initiatives and policy frameworks, demonstrating a concerted effort to foster AI development and integration within Japan’s economy. The document highlights the government's commitment to not only advancing AI technologies themselves but also supporting the related semiconductor industry, recognizing the crucial interdependence of these fields. The "AI and Semiconductor Industry Strengthening Framework" represents a significant commitment to long-term AI innovation, signaling Japan's intent to remain a leader in both AI and semiconductor technologies. The document details the specific budgetary allocations and strategic priorities driving this national initiative, providing a snapshot of Japan's approach to navigating the rapidly evolving landscape of AI.
