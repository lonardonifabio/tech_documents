Japan’s AI Act versus EU AI Act A Mapping Exercise

Authors: Sean Musch, Michael Borrelli, Charles Kerrigan, Abhishek R. Sharma, Leah Ferentinos

This report, published by AI & Partners, provides a comparative analysis of the EU AI Act and the proposed Japan AI Act. The document highlights AI & Partners’ mission to safeguard digital rights and promote responsible AI development globally. AI & Partners advocates for a framework that balances innovation with ethical considerations, recognizing the potential risks associated with uncontrolled AI adoption, including confusion, risk, and reputational damage. The organization’s approach centers on enabling safe AI usage for both organizations and their clients through tools for identification, monitoring, and governance of AI systems. The report emphasizes the importance of proactive control and responsible handling of AI to foster trust and innovation. AI & Partners engages in direct technical support, policy engagement, global advocacy, grassroots professional services, regulatory interventions, and participation in industry groups like AI Commons to achieve these goals. The core argument is that a robust governance framework is essential to mitigate the negative consequences of AI and ensure its beneficial application. The document positions AI & Partners as a key player in navigating the evolving regulatory landscape and promoting ethical AI practices. It underscores the need for organizations to take a strategic approach to AI adoption, prioritizing control and responsible development. The report’s purpose is to assist organizations in understanding and responding to the challenges and opportunities presented by the upcoming AI regulations, ultimately contributing to a safer and more trustworthy AI ecosystem.
