Agents, robots, and us: Skill partnerships in the age of AI

Authors: Lareina Yee, Anu Madgavkar, Sven Smit, Alexis Krivkovich, Michael Chui, Maria Jesus Ramirez, Diego Castresana

This McKinsey & Company report, "Agents, robots, and us: Skill partnerships in the age of AI," explores the evolving relationship between humans and intelligent agents – including robots and AI systems – within the context of the current technological landscape. The research focuses on how organizations can strategically leverage these new technologies to foster effective skill partnerships, maximizing productivity and innovation. The document highlights the critical need for businesses to adapt their workforce strategies to complement the capabilities of AI, emphasizing the importance of developing new skills and fostering collaboration between humans and machines. It delves into the potential impact of automation on various industries and job roles, advocating for a proactive approach to talent management and organizational design. The report is produced by McKinsey's Global Institute, a research arm dedicated to providing data-driven insights for decision-making among businesses and policy leaders. The MGI’s research themes include productivity and prosperity, sustainable resource management, human potential, global connections, and future technologies and markets. The document stresses the importance of independent, fact-based research and outlines the MGI's editorial control and research focus. The core argument centers around the need for organizations to understand and manage the shift towards a collaborative environment where humans and AI agents work together, leveraging each other's strengths to achieve optimal outcomes. It’s a strategic analysis of how companies can navigate the complexities of AI adoption and build a future workforce equipped to thrive in this new era. The report’s publication date is November 2025, and it is a confidential and proprietary document owned by McKinsey & Company.
