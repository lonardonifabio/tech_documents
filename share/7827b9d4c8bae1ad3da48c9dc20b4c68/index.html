Artificial Intelligence Use and Oversight in Financial Services

Authors: United States Government Accountability Office

This report, issued by the United States Government Accountability Office (GAO) in May 2025 (GAO-25-107197), examines the increasing use of artificial intelligence (AI) within the financial services sector. The report highlights the dual nature of AI's application – offering significant benefits alongside considerable risks. Financial institutions are leveraging AI across various functions, notably automated trading, credit decisions, and customer service, with the potential to improve operational efficiency, reduce costs, and deliver more personalized customer experiences, such as tailored investment advice. However, the report underscores critical concerns surrounding AI's implementation. Specifically, it identifies potential biases in lending decisions stemming from biased data, data quality challenges that can compromise accuracy, significant privacy risks associated with AI-driven data processing, and the emergence of novel cybersecurity threats targeting AI systems. The GAO’s findings indicate that federal financial regulators currently rely on existing laws, regulations, and risk-based examinations to oversee AI. While some regulators have issued AI-specific guidance, particularly concerning AI use in lending, and have conducted AI-focused examinations, they are actively monitoring and assessing emerging AI risks. The report emphasizes the need for ongoing refinement of guidance and updates to regulations to proactively address these vulnerabilities. Notably, the National Credit Union Administration (NCUA) lacks certain key oversight tools, potentially impacting its ability to effectively monitor AI activities within credit unions. The GAO’s assessment suggests a dynamic landscape where regulators must continuously adapt their approach to ensure responsible AI adoption and mitigate associated risks within the financial services industry. The report’s timing, slated for May 2025, reflects the rapidly evolving nature of AI and its implications for financial stability and consumer protection.
