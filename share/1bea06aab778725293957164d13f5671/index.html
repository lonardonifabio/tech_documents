Machine Learning with Python for Everyone

Authors: MarkE.Fenner

This document excerpt is a preface and introduction to the "Machine Learning with Python for Everyone" book, part of the Pearson Addison-Wesley Data & Analytics Series. The series focuses on providing practical knowledge for data-driven problem-solving. This particular book centers around three core areas: Infrastructure (data storage, movement, and management), Algorithms (data mining and prediction), and Visualizations (representing data insights). The book aims to equip readers with the skills to build complete systems for applications like spam detection, recommendation engines, personalization, trend analysis, and gaining insights from user data. It emphasizes a holistic approach, connecting these three areas. The preface highlights the series' overall goal of providing readers with the tools and knowledge to effectively utilize data for various analytical and business applications. The book's content is designed for individuals seeking a comprehensive understanding of machine learning concepts and their practical implementation using Python. The series is distributed globally with locations including Boston, Columbus, New York, San Francisco, Amsterdam, Cape Town, Dubai, London, Madrid, Milan, Munich, Paris, Montreal, Toronto, Delhi, Mexico City, São Paulo, Sydney, Hong Kong, Seoul, Singapore, Taipei, and Tokyo. The excerpt also notes that many product designations are trademarks.

Key Concepts:

  • Practical knowledge for solving problems and answering questions with data.
  • How to store, move, and manage data.
  • How to mine intelligence or make predictions based on data.
  • How to represent data and insights in a meaningful and compelling way.
  • Building end-to-end systems for fighting spam, making recommendations, building personalization, detecting trends, patterns, or problems, and gaining insight from the data exhaust of systems and user interactions.
