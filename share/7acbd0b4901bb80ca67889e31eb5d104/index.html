RESPONSIBLE AI QUESTION BANK : A COMPREHENSIVE TOOL FOR AI RISK ASSESSMENT

Authors: Sung Une Lee, Harsha Perera, Yue Liu, Boming Xia, Qinghua Lu, Liming Zhu, Olivier Salvado, Jon Whittle

This research paper introduces the Responsible AI (RAI) Question Bank, a novel and comprehensive toolkit designed to address the growing need for robust AI risk assessment. Recognizing the rapid expansion of Artificial Intelligence and the subsequent urgency for responsible AI practices, the study identifies a significant gap – the lack of a truly comprehensive tool for evaluating potential risks associated with AI development and deployment. The RAI Question Bank tackles this challenge by providing a structured framework that integrates core AI ethics principles, including fairness, transparency, and accountability, into a series of targeted questions. This approach allows organizations to systematically identify and mitigate risks across diverse AI initiatives, aligning with evolving regulatory landscapes such as the EU AI Act. A central feature of the Question Bank is its ability to connect lower-level risk questions to higher-level themes and related concepts, preventing fragmented assessments and fostering a holistic evaluation process. The paper demonstrates the practical utility of the RAI Question Bank through detailed case studies, showcasing its application in assessing various AI projects – from pinpointing specific risk factors to informing strategic decision-making. Furthermore, the research highlights the tool's capacity to ensure compliance with relevant standards, proactively mitigate potential risks, and ultimately, contribute to the development of trustworthy and ethical AI systems. The work represents a significant advancement in the field of Responsible AI by offering organizations a practical and readily accessible resource for navigating the increasingly complex challenges of ethical AI development and governance. The systematic approach and integration of key ethical considerations make the RAI Question Bank a valuable asset for any organization committed to building and deploying AI responsibly.

Key Concepts:

  • A comprehensive AI risk assessment toolkit is lacking, necessitating the development of tools like the RAI Question Bank.
  • The RAI Question Bank integrates AI ethics principles (fairness, transparency, accountability) into a structured question format for risk identification.
  • The tool facilitates a systematic approach to linking risk questions, preventing siloed assessments and ensuring a cohesive evaluation process.
  • Framework for safe and responsible AI use in government services.
  • Risk assessment and mitigation strategies for AI technologies, including addressing emerging risks like generative AI.
  • Regulatory frameworks for AI, such as the EU AI Act, which classifies AI systems based on risk levels and establishes requirements for high-risk applications.
  • The RAI Question Bank is a tool for interpreting regulations and standards into assessment checklists to assess compliance.
  • AI risk assessment addresses challenges like biased data, algorithmic errors, and unintended consequences.
  • A structured and systematic approach to risk management, based on AI ethics principles, is necessary for managing complex AI risks.
  • The increasing demand for ethical AI requires human oversight of automation.
