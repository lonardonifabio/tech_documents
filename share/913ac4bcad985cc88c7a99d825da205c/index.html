Australian Public Service AI Plan: Adopting AI to deliver for Australians 20 25

Authors: Australian Public Service

This document outlines the Australian Public Service’s (APS) AI Plan, a strategic initiative designed to leverage Artificial Intelligence (AI) to improve services and outcomes for Australians by 2025. The plan emphasizes an adaptive and collaborative approach, recognizing the need for ongoing adjustments and partnerships. It establishes clear objectives focused on driving AI adoption across the APS, encompassing a defined scope of deliverables and actionable steps. Key elements include a detailed implementation timeline, a robust framework for trust – prioritizing transparency, ethics, and governance – alongside updates to government policy and guidance related to AI. The plan incorporates the AI Review Committee to oversee and guide the implementation, and establishes clear expectations for external service providers engaging in AI projects. Furthermore, the document highlights the importance of capability building and engagement within the APS workforce through foundational learning programs. The overarching goal is to ensure responsible and effective utilization of AI technologies to enhance government services and contribute to national priorities. The plan’s success hinges on a coordinated effort across the APS, with a strong emphasis on ethical considerations and continuous monitoring of progress. The document provides a roadmap for the APS to navigate the complexities of AI adoption, fostering innovation while upholding public trust and accountability. It’s a forward-looking strategy designed to transform the way the APS operates and delivers services, ultimately benefiting the Australian community.

Key Concepts:

  • Adopting AI to deliver for Australians 20 25
  • Taking an adaptive and collaborative approach for AI implementation
  • Establishing trust through transparency, ethics, and governance frameworks related to AI in the APS
  • An integrated approach combining Trust, People, and Tools to support AI adoption within the Australian Public Service (APS).
  • The importance of skilled people, clear policies, and effective AI tools for successful AI implementation.
  • Ongoing oversight and accountability arrangements for AI use by military and law enforcement agencies to ensure responsible and trustworthy use.
  • Ongoing and genuine consultation with staff and unions is crucial for the successful rollout of AI technologies.
  • Foundational AI literacy training will be mandated for all staff to ensure responsible and effective AI usage.
  • The government will build capability and confidence in public servants to use AI, alongside addressing the role of leaders in shaping AI adoption.
