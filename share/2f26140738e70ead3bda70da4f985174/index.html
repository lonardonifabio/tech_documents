Code of Practice for General -Purpose AI Models Safety and Security

Authors: Matthias Samwald, Yoshua Bengio, Marietje Schaake, Marta Ziosi, Daniel Privitera, Anka Reuel, Alexander Zacherl, Nitarshan Rajkumar, Markus Anderljung

This document outlines a Code of Practice designed to enhance the safety and security of general-purpose AI models within the European Union, aligning with the goals of the AI Act. The overarching objective is to foster the responsible development and deployment of human-centric and trustworthy AI, safeguarding fundamental rights and promoting innovation. Specifically, the Code serves as a guiding document for demonstrating compliance with Articles 53 and 55 of the AI Act, acknowledging that adherence doesn't guarantee full compliance. It aims to ensure that providers of general-purpose AI models meet their obligations under the AI Act, providing a framework for the AI Office to assess their adherence. The document emphasizes the need to mitigate potential harms associated with AI, including risks to health, safety, and fundamental rights such as democracy, the rule of law, and environmental protection. The Code's creation is directly linked to the implementation of the AI Act, reflecting a proactive approach to regulating the rapidly evolving field of artificial intelligence. It recognizes the importance of a multi-faceted strategy, combining regulatory oversight with industry self-regulation to achieve a balance between innovation and responsible AI development. The document’s structure suggests a collaborative effort, involving various working groups and individuals, highlighting the complexity and breadth of considerations surrounding the safe and secure deployment of general-purpose AI models. The inclusion of references to Article 1(1) AI Act underscores the document's alignment with the broader legislative framework for AI in the EU. Ultimately, the Code of Practice represents a critical step in establishing a robust and trustworthy ecosystem for AI within the Union, prioritizing ethical considerations and minimizing potential negative consequences.

Key Concepts:

  • The Code of Practice aims to improve the functioning of the internal market and promote the uptake of human-centric and trustworthy AI, while ensuring protection of health, safety, and fundamental rights.
  • The Code serves as a guiding document for demonstrating compliance with the AI Act obligations and enables the AI Office to assess provider compliance.
  • Adherence to the Code does not constitute conclusive evidence of compliance with the AI Act.
