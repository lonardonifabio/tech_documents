New Economy Skills: Building AI, Data and Digital Capabilities for Growth

Authors: World Economic Forum, Cognizant

This white paper, published by the World Economic Forum in collaboration with Cognizant, explores the critical need for developing AI, data, and digital skills to drive economic growth in the evolving global landscape. The document begins with a foreword emphasizing technology's role in fostering productivity and innovation. It then provides an executive summary outlining the key challenges and opportunities. The core of the paper is structured around two main sections: the first examines the current landscape of AI, data, and digital skills, defining these terms, analyzing supply and demand dynamics, and highlighting regional trends. Specifically, it delves into the existing skills gap and the increasing demand for professionals proficient in these areas. The second section, a ‘call to action,’ focuses on developing, assessing, and credentialing these digital skills. A significant portion of the paper is dedicated to practical strategies for assessing and developing these skills, including principles and best practices. It outlines a framework for credentialing, recognizing the importance of formal and informal learning pathways. The document concludes with a list of contributors and endnotes. The World Economic Forum emphasizes that the findings and conclusions presented are a collaborative effort, representing a project insight area, and do not necessarily reflect the views of all its members or stakeholders. The paper’s ultimate goal is to provide actionable insights and recommendations for organizations and individuals seeking to navigate the rapidly changing demands of the digital economy. It underscores the urgency of investing in and cultivating AI, data, and digital capabilities to ensure sustained economic growth and competitiveness. The document is a valuable resource for businesses, policymakers, and educational institutions looking to understand and address the skills gap in the new economy.
