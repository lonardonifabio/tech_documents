Draft Implementing Regulation for AI Regulatory Sandboxes December 2025

This document excerpt outlines the draft implementing regulation for AI regulatory sandboxes, scheduled for completion by December 2025. It serves as a preparatory document detailing the framework intended to facilitate the development and testing of innovative AI systems within a controlled environment. The core purpose of these regulatory sandboxes, as defined by Regulation (EU) 2024/1689, is to provide a supervised setting for AI system developers to conduct activities such as development, training, validation, and testing, including real-world trials, before broader market placement. The document emphasizes the need to prevent fragmentation within the AI regulatory landscape by establishing a unified approach overseen by competent authorities. Specifically, Section 1 of the regulation focuses on the recitals, clarifying the intent behind the sandboxes – to allow for the development and testing of innovative AI systems under regulatory supervision for a limited duration. The regulation aims to balance innovation with appropriate oversight, ensuring that AI systems are thoroughly evaluated and validated before being released to the public.
