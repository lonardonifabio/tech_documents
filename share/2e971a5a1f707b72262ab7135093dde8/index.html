General-Purpose AI Code of Practice

Authors: Signatories

This document outlines the ‘General-Purpose AI Code of Practice’, finalized in July 2025, which serves as a framework for promoting trustworthy and human-centric artificial intelligence within the European Union. The primary objective is to mitigate the potential harms of AI while fostering innovation, aligning with the principles of the AI Act (specifically Articles 53 and 55). The Code acts as a guidance document for AI model providers, particularly those considered to have systemic risk, requiring continuous assessment and mitigation of risks throughout the model's lifecycle. It’s designed to assist the AI Office in evaluating compliance of providers who choose to adopt the Code as a means of demonstrating adherence to the AI Act’s obligations. Crucially, the Code does not provide conclusive proof of compliance with the AI Act itself. The document emphasizes a lifecycle management approach, demanding ongoing risk assessment and mitigation strategies. The Signatories acknowledge that adherence to the Code doesn't automatically satisfy the legal requirements set forth in the AI Act. This framework is intended to support the broader goals of the AI Act, ensuring AI development and deployment are conducted responsibly and ethically, safeguarding fundamental rights and promoting a robust internal market. The document’s creation reflects a proactive approach to managing the risks associated with general-purpose AI models and aligns with the EU’s regulatory strategy for AI.

Key Concepts:

  • The Code of Practice aims to promote trustworthy AI and protect fundamental rights.
  • Providers of general-purpose AI models must comply with the AI Act.
  • Appropriate Lifecycle Management involves continuous risk assessment and mitigation throughout the model's lifecycle.
  • Estimates of timelines for models exceeding systemic risk tiers.
  • Justifications for estimates including assumptions and uncertainties.
  • Process for updating the Framework and confirming updates within a specified timeframe.
  • Post-market monitoring of AI systems
  • Collection of end-user feedback for monitoring
  • Establishing reporting channels for incident reporting
  • Model Usage and Expectations: Describes how the model is intended to be used in development, oversight, and evaluation.
