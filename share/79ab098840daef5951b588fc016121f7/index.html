Incident Report for Serious Incidents under the AI Act (High -risk AI systems)

This document presents an incident reporting template specifically designed for serious incidents involving high-risk AI systems as defined under the AI Act. The template is intended for use by market surveillance authorities to systematically record and report such incidents. It focuses on gathering crucial information related to the incident itself, including the date and time of occurrence, the type of incident, its classification based on potential harm (e.g., death, harm to health, disruption of critical infrastructure), and the details of the reporting submitter. The template emphasizes standardized reporting to ensure consistent data collection and facilitate effective monitoring and oversight of high-risk AI systems. Specifically, Section 1 outlines administrative information, detailing the responsible market surveillance authority, the incident's date and time, and the type of report (initial, follow-up, combined, final). It also includes a classification of the incident based on the potential harm caused. The template's purpose is to provide a structured approach to incident reporting, aligning with the requirements of the AI Act and supporting the ongoing assessment of risks associated with high-risk AI systems. The use of specific date and time formats (YYYY-MM-DD) ensures consistency and facilitates data analysis. The inclusion of the manufacturer awareness date is crucial for tracing the origin of the incident and understanding the potential design or operational issues that may have contributed to it. The template’s design is geared towards supporting regulatory compliance and proactive risk management within the context of the AI Act.
