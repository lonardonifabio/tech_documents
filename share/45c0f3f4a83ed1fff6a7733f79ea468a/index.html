The 2025 Chief Data Officer Study: The AI Multiplier Effect

Authors: IBM Institute for Business Value

This research report, part of the 2025 Chief Data Officer Study, focuses on the ‘AI multiplier effect’ – how organizations can accelerate growth by leveraging decision-ready data and AI. IBM, a recognized leader in data and AI solutions, highlights its capabilities in helping businesses transform through tailored strategies. The document emphasizes the importance of developing dynamic data strategies, implementing AI-enabled decision support systems, and establishing scalable enterprise data architectures. A key component of IBM’s approach is the watsonx™ platform, designed to empower organizations with AI for predictive analytics, real-time data processing, and automated decision-making. The report outlines strategic imperatives for data deployment, advocating for a mission-oriented approach to data utilization. It addresses scaling AI by providing ‘fast tracks’ for AI agents to access data, building resilient data pipelines to ensure uninterrupted data flow, and delivering data to every desk to foster innovation. Furthermore, the document explores how data can be used to identify and capitalize on emerging breakthroughs, driving growth. The core argument is that while the technology for AI is maturing, many organizations struggle to effectively utilize it due to fragmented data landscapes. IBM positions itself as a solution provider, offering integrated enterprise data architectures and tools like watsonx™ to overcome these challenges. The report’s structure, divided into sections like ‘Scale,’ ‘Resilience,’ and ‘Innovation,’ demonstrates a holistic approach to data and AI strategy, aiming to equip organizations with the necessary tools and insights to unlock the full potential of their data assets. The document also provides links to further resources, including IBM Consulting’s data and AI services, IBM Software’s Watson solutions, and IBM Research’s artificial intelligence innovations.

Key Concepts:

  • The AI multiplier effect – leveraging AI to accelerate growth.
  • Decision-ready data – data that is prepared for immediate use in decision-making.
  • Enterprise AI at scale – integrating AI with enterprise data for efficient operation.
  • CDOs need to focus on business objectives to drive enterprise goals in the age of AI.
  • Effective communication from CDOs regarding data's value is crucial for organizational success.
  • Clear metrics are needed to measure the value of data-driven business outcomes.
  • Continuous data collection and updating for real-time data availability.
  • Developing data products as reusable assets to improve data accessibility and security.
  • Shifting to a data-centric organizational approach, prioritizing data foundation over technology solutions.
