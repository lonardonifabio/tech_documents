Assessing High-Risk Artificial Intelligence: Fundamental Rights Risks

Authors: European Union Agency for Fundamental Rights

This report, titled "Assessing High-Risk Artificial Intelligence: Fundamental Rights Risks," published by the European Union Agency for Fundamental Rights in 2025, critically examines the potential risks posed by artificial intelligence systems to fundamental rights as outlined within the context of the EU AI Act. The AI Act represents a landmark achievement, establishing the world’s first comprehensive legal framework for artificial intelligence, signifying the European Union’s proactive stance in navigating the complexities and ethical considerations of this rapidly advancing technology. The report focuses specifically on ‘high-risk’ AI applications, which are defined as those that could cause significant harm to individuals or society. It delves into various areas where AI systems present potential threats, including bias and discrimination, privacy violations, surveillance, and the erosion of human autonomy. The analysis considers the implications of AI’s use in sectors such as law enforcement, border control, and social scoring, highlighting how these technologies could disproportionately impact vulnerable populations. Furthermore, the document explores the challenges of ensuring accountability and transparency in AI systems, advocating for robust regulatory mechanisms to mitigate risks and safeguard fundamental rights. The report emphasizes the need for ongoing monitoring and evaluation of AI systems to identify and address emerging threats. It provides a detailed assessment of the potential impacts of AI on human rights, offering recommendations for policymakers and stakeholders to promote responsible AI development and deployment. The document’s publication date of 2025 indicates it was created in anticipation of the AI Act's implementation, reflecting the urgency of addressing these concerns within the evolving regulatory landscape.
