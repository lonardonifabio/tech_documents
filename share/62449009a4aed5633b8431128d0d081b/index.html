DPIA on EduGenAI

Authors: Sjoera Nas, Jacob Gursky

This document presents a Data Protection Impact Assessment (DPIA) specifically focused on the EduGenAI system. EduGenAI is an AI-powered web application designed to provide users with secure and privacy-conscious access to various generative AI tools. Functioning as a privacy proxy, it bridges the gap between users and different generative AI services, including both locally hosted large language models and cloud-based providers. A key feature of EduGenAI is its ability to offer multimodal access, allowing users to seamlessly switch between AI systems during a prompt dialogue. Crucially, the system incorporates a filter to mask personal data, safeguarding user privacy and the rights of individuals mentioned within the prompts. The application’s architecture includes access to one or more search engines, enabling users to ‘update’ the generated information. The DPIA highlights the importance of mitigating privacy risks associated with the use of generative AI, particularly concerning the handling of personal data. The document details the steps taken to assess and address these risks, emphasizing the system's design choices and the implemented safeguards. The report’s evolution reflects iterative revisions and feedback from SURF, culminating in the final public version. The document's purpose is to demonstrate compliance with data protection regulations and to ensure responsible innovation in the field of AI-powered generative tools. The version history indicates a thorough review process, with multiple drafts and revisions to refine the assessment and incorporate relevant feedback. The final public version represents the culmination of this effort, providing a comprehensive overview of the DPIA for EduGenAI.
