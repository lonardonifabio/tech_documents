Sustaining Slovenia’s AI Talent Momentum: Strategic Priorities to Convert Steady Gains into a Robust, Competitive, and Innovation - Driven Ecosystem by 2030

Authors: Sean Musch, Michael Borrelli, Charles Kerrigan, Kristina Kočet Hudrap, Sara Kremsar, Milovan Tomašević, Aljaz (Al) Peklaj, DAVID HORVAT, Matija Jamnik, Klemen Selakovič, Sasa Sodja, Andrej P. Skraba

This document, titled "Sustaining Slovenia’s AI Talent Momentum," presents an AI Ecosystem Brief focusing on Slovenia’s progress in developing an AI-skilled workforce by 2030. The core analysis centers around a chart illustrating the concentration of LinkedIn members possessing AI engineering skills or holding AI-related occupations within Slovenia from 2016 to 2024. The data reveals a consistently upward trend, starting at 0.322 in 2016 and reaching 0.715 in 2024. This steady growth is attributed to several key factors, including robust capacity building within research institutions, the expansion of digital-intensive industries, and advancements in advanced manufacturing. Notably, the most significant acceleration in AI talent concentration occurred after 2020, indicating a successful strategy of cultivating an AI-skilled workforce. The document emphasizes the importance of stable educational pipelines, targeted national innovation initiatives, and integration into broader European Union digital programs as crucial components of this ongoing development. The analysis highlights Slovenia's approach as distinct from countries experiencing volatile AI talent growth, showcasing a more consistent and deliberate strategy. The brief aims to provide strategic priorities for maintaining and further strengthening Slovenia’s AI ecosystem, ultimately positioning it as a competitive and innovation-driven nation within the European AI landscape. The document’s purpose is to demonstrate Slovenia’s commitment to AI development and to outline the key elements needed to sustain this momentum.

Key Concepts:

  • Slovenia’s AI talent concentration is steadily increasing from 2016 to 2024.
  • The growth in AI talent in Slovenia is driven by stable educational pipelines and national innovation initiatives.
  • Slovenia’s AI ecosystem is characterized by consistent, gradual capacity building across various sectors.
  • Slovenian transposition of the EU AI Act into domestic law.
  • Establishment of an expert council to advise on AI regulatory and policy issues.
  • Creation of a national AI regulatory sandbox for safe testing of AI systems while ensuring compliance.
  • Slovenia's comprehensive approach to AI governance, deployment, and innovation is rooted in EU alignment, ethical safeguards, and institutional coordination.
  • The NpUI positions AI as a driver of social well-being, economic competitiveness, and public sector modernization, while centering trust, human-centricity, and international cooperation in its implementation.
  • The NpUI fosters a national AI ecosystem by linking universities, public research institutes, industrial R&D centers, and digital innovation hubs (DIHs).
