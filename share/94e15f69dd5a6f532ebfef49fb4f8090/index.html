The ROI of AI 2025: How Agents are Unlocking the Next Wave of AI-Driven Business Value

Authors: Google, Oliver Parker

This report examines the evolving landscape of Artificial Intelligence, specifically focusing on the shift towards agentic AI and its impact on business value. As the initial excitement surrounding AI diminishes, the emphasis has moved to practical applications and demonstrable returns on investment. The document highlights a transition from predictive AI to generative AI, culminating in the ‘agentic era,’ where AI agents autonomously execute tasks and make decisions under human oversight. Google’s perspective defines these agents as systems integrating advanced AI models with access to tools, enabling them to perform actions on behalf of users while maintaining control. The report emphasizes that companies rapidly adopting agentic AI are already realizing significant ROI, utilizing agents to enhance customer experiences, optimize employee productivity, and drive efficiency across departments like marketing, IT, and HR. This increased ROI is crucial for securing leadership buy-in and supporting broader AI scaling strategies. The core of the report provides blueprints derived from the strategies of successful early adopters, offering guidance for leaders seeking to translate agentic AI into tangible business outcomes. The document underscores the importance of a proactive approach to AI implementation, recognizing that early adoption and strategic deployment are key to unlocking the full potential of this technology. The findings suggest a clear pathway for organizations to leverage agentic AI, moving beyond theoretical applications to measurable business results. The report’s focus on ‘agentic maturity’ indicates a recognition that the journey towards fully realized AI benefits requires a phased approach, starting with targeted deployments and gradually expanding as capabilities and trust develop.

Key Concepts:

  • The shift in focus from ‘if’ to ‘how’ of AI implementation.
  • The evolution of AI from predictive to generative models.
  • AI agents' ability to independently execute tasks and make decisions with human oversight.
  • The concept of agentic maturity and its correlation with ROI.
  • Agentic AI adoption trends
  • AI agent deployment across industries
  • Gen AI journey stages
  • Survey data aggregation by industry
