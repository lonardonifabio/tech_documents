EU AI Act Apply AI Strategy

Authors: Sean Musch, Michael Borrelli, Charles Kerrigan, Wojciech Woloszyk, Hanım Eken, Dana Lihotina, Ahmed Noureldeen, PhD, MBA

This document outlines an ‘Apply AI Strategy’ plan for AI adoption, specifically referencing the EU AI Act. It focuses on defining distinct roles within an organization to effectively implement and govern AI initiatives. The core framework identifies four key roles: ‘The Doer,’ ‘The Transformer,’ ‘The Leader,’ and ‘The Guardian,’ alongside ‘The Protector’ and ‘The Strategist.’ ‘The Doer’ emphasizes practical implementation by frontline AI users. ‘The Transformer’ focuses on driving value and transforming operations and products through AI. ‘The Leader’ represents senior-level commitment and strategic guidance for AI governance. ‘The Guardian’ is responsible for ensuring responsible, legal, and trustworthy AI practices. ‘The Protector’ supports AI with robust data, platforms, and security measures. Finally, ‘The Strategist’ navigates the complexities of AI implementation and identifies strategic opportunities. The document appears to be a strategic guide or framework for organizations considering the adoption of AI, particularly in the context of regulatory frameworks like the EU AI Act. It's a high-level overview of how different roles should contribute to a successful AI strategy. The document's purpose is to provide a structured approach to AI adoption, emphasizing governance, responsible use, and strategic alignment. It suggests a multi-faceted approach to AI implementation, recognizing the need for diverse skillsets and responsibilities within an organization.
