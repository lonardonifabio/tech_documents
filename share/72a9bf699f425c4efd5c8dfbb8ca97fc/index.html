Road Safety Classification using Multimodal Deep Learning Resnet-50 and DenseNet-121 Models

Authors: IIT Bhubaneswar

This document outlines a research project conducted by IIT Bhubaneswar focused on road safety classification utilizing deep learning models. The core of the project involves developing and implementing ResNet-50 and DenseNet-121 models to analyze multimodal data related to road safety incidents. The data used in this study is derived from a dataset containing images captured at various locations, alongside associated metadata such as time of day, human activity, luminosity, terrain, presence of buildings, mechanical activity, nearby vegetation, and a safety rating.
