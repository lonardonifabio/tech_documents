AI AGENTS How Agents Are Accelerating the Next Wave of AI Value Creation

Authors: Vinciane Beauchene, Nicolas de Bellefonds, Djon Kleine, Rich Lesser, Amanda Luther, Tom Martin, Daniel Sack

This article, published by Boston Consulting Group in December 2025, explores the emerging trend of AI agents and their potential to revolutionize business operations. It argues that previous AI advancements primarily focused on logic and optimization (predictive AI) or creativity and synthesis (generative AI). However, the next wave of AI will center around agentic systems – systems capable of autonomously managing entire workflows, leveraging a company’s institutional knowledge to make judgments and drive performance. The article positions agentic AI as the ‘executive function’ that translates creative probability into tangible business results, particularly within process-heavy functions where execution is paramount. Early adopters are already demonstrating the impact of this technology, with a shipbuilder successfully reducing design effort by 45% and lead time by 80% through the implementation of orchestrator and worker agents. The document highlights a shift in AI value creation, moving beyond decision-making and content production to encompass operational execution. The core concept emphasizes the ability of these agents to not just analyze data but to actively manage and optimize workflows, fundamentally changing how businesses operate and generate value. The article suggests that agentic AI represents a significant step forward in AI's ability to integrate seamlessly into complex business processes, ultimately leading to increased efficiency and productivity. It’s a foundational piece discussing the transition from predictive and generative AI to a more proactive and autonomous AI landscape.

Key Concepts:

  • AI is transforming the way work gets done, with the next wave focusing on agentic systems.
  • Agentic AI will take on entire workflows, applying judgment shaped by a company’s institutional knowledge.
  • Agentic AI is opening value in process-heavy functions where execution defines performance.
  • Tech strategy is linked to competitive strategy.
  • Cloud and model providers are strategic alliances.
  • Modular systems are crucial to avoid vendor lock-in and protect data value.
  • Focus on a few ‘reshape and invent’ AI bets for significant impact.
  • Combine predictive, generative, and agentic AI for powerful systems.
