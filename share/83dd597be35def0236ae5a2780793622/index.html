AI Ecosystem Brief 2025 Edition Finland

Authors: Sean Musch, Michael Borrelli, Charles Kerrigan, Jukka Marttila, Andrey Prozorov, Cristina Andersson, Markku Rasanen, Dr. Eugene Grasin, Patrick Halford, Keir Finlow-Bates, Karoliina Partanen, Minna Hendolin, Jukka Märijärvi

This document, the "AI Ecosystem Brief 2025 Edition Finland," is a publication by AI & Partners, released in September 2025. It focuses on the organization's mission to defend and extend digital rights for users globally who are considered at risk due to the development and deployment of artificial intelligence. The report highlights AI & Partners' approach, which combines direct technical support, policy engagement, global advocacy, grassroots professional services, regulatory interventions, and participation in industry groups like AI Commons. The core argument is that AI should be used to unlock potential rather than cause harm, and the organization aims to assist clients in building AI systems they can trust and confidently stand behind. AI & Partners emphasizes the importance of responsible AI development and deployment, addressing the potential risks associated with losing control of AI systems. The report details the organization's strategy for navigating the evolving landscape of AI regulation and promoting ethical practices within the industry. It underscores the need for proactive engagement in shaping the future of AI to ensure it aligns with fundamental human rights and fosters innovation while mitigating potential negative consequences. The document serves as a strategic overview of AI & Partners' activities and their commitment to responsible AI governance. It’s a publication intended to inform stakeholders about the organization's role in advocating for user rights and promoting trustworthy AI solutions. The contact information provided allows interested parties to learn more about AI & Partners' services and initiatives.

Key Concepts:

  • AI & Partners defends and extends the digital rights of users at risk around the world.
  • AI & Partners believes AI should unlock potential — not cause harm.
  • AI & Partners exists to help you build AI you can trust and stand behind.
  • Finland’s regulatory focus on balancing innovation with accountability.
  • The Artificial Intelligence 4.0 programme defining Finland’s long-term AI vision.
  • Human-centricity and democratic values as core guiding principles for AI development.
  • Finland fosters regional excellence through research clusters and application hubs.
  • Public-private collaborations are crucial for developing scalable AI portfolios and testbeds.
  • Ethical and interoperable data infrastructures are promoted through initiatives like GAIA-X.
  • AI is being leveraged to drive sustainability and digital transformation in public services.
