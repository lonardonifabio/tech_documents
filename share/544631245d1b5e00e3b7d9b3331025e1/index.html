AI Adoption Playbook

Authors: Digital Cooperation Organization (DCO)

This document, titled "AI Adoption Playbook," is a guide designed to assist nations in progressing from initial AI readiness to full adoption and ultimately, leadership in the field. Prepared by the Digital Cooperation Organization (DCO), the playbook outlines a strategic approach for navigating the complexities of integrating AI into various sectors. The core purpose is to provide a framework for organizations and governments to systematically assess their current state, identify opportunities for AI implementation, and develop a roadmap for sustainable growth. Crucially, the document emphasizes a phased approach, starting with foundational readiness assessments and moving through stages of adoption and leadership.
