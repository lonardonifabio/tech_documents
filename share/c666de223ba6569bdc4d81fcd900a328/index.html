Generative AI for Health in Low & Middle Income Countries

This document explores the application of Generative AI (GenAI), specifically Large Language Models (LLMs) and Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) techniques, within Low- and Middle-Income Countries (LMICs). It provides a framework to guide the responsible and effective use of these technologies to address healthcare challenges. The report begins with key definitions, clarifying terms like LMICs, GenAI, LLMs, Human-in-the-loop (HITL), and RAG, highlighting their relevance to the context. A survey analysis is presented, followed by a robust framework outlining key principles and risks associated with GenAI implementation in healthcare settings. The document then delves into several case studies demonstrating successful GenAI deployments, including Jacaranda Health's PROMPTs, Viamo's Ask Viamo Anything (AVA) and Girl Effect's AVA, Audere's Self-Care From Anywhere, and Noora Health's initiatives. These case studies showcase diverse applications, from behavioral change interventions to personalized health support. The report concludes with key recommendations for stakeholders and acknowledges the research team and participating individuals involved in the study. The appendix contains additional references and supporting materials. The core focus is on leveraging GenAI to improve healthcare outcomes in resource-constrained environments, emphasizing the importance of responsible development and deployment, considering factors like data access, ethical considerations, and the need for human oversight through HITL and RAG approaches. The document underscores the potential of LLMs and RAG to enhance the accuracy and relevance of AI-driven solutions within the healthcare sector of LMICs, offering a practical guide for implementation and future research.
