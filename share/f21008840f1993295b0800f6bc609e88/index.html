CCAPAC Report: AI and Cybersecurity

Authors: Coalition for Cybersecurity in Asia-Pacific (CCAPAC)

The CCAPAC Report: AI and Cybersecurity, published in 2023, provides an overview of the intersection between Artificial Intelligence (AI) and cybersecurity within the Asia-Pacific region. The report, produced by the Coalition for Cybersecurity in Asia-Pacific (CCAPAC), highlights the organization's mission to positively influence the cybersecurity landscape through policy analysis, stakeholder engagement, and capacity building initiatives. The document begins with an introduction setting the stage for the report's focus. It then delves into the current AI landscape, detailing the various technologies and their applications across diverse sectors. Specifically, it examines how AI is being utilized and the potential impacts across industries. A significant portion of the report is dedicated to analyzing the specific cybersecurity risks associated with AI. This includes a detailed examination of data security risks, focusing on vulnerabilities related to the collection, storage, and processing of data utilized by AI systems. Furthermore, the report addresses model security risks, scrutinizing the potential for adversarial attacks and manipulation of AI models themselves. The document also considers infrastructure risks, acknowledging the security challenges posed by the underlying hardware and software supporting AI systems. Finally, it explores application risks, recognizing the security implications of deploying AI-powered applications. The report’s aim is to provide a comprehensive understanding of the evolving relationship between AI and cybersecurity, offering insights for stakeholders seeking to navigate this complex domain effectively. The Coalition for Cybersecurity in Asia-Pacific (CCAPAC) is actively involved in shaping policy and fostering collaboration to strengthen cybersecurity defenses across the Asia-Pacific region. The report serves as a valuable resource for policymakers, security professionals, and anyone interested in the future of AI and cybersecurity.
