ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE Federal Efforts Guided by Requirements and Advisory Groups

Authors: United States Government Accountability Office

This report, GAO-25-107933, released in September 2025, details the federal government's efforts in implementing artificial intelligence (AI) strategies. The United States Government Accountability Office (GAO) conducted this study to assess the landscape of AI-related requirements across various federal agencies. The report identifies 94 AI-related requirements, encompassing both government-wide mandates and those with significant implications for the entire federal government. A key finding is the diverse range of requirements stemming from federal laws, executive orders, and guidance documents. For example, the General Services Administration is mandated to establish an AI Center of Excellence, impacting all federal offices. Ten executive branch oversight and advisory groups play a crucial role in the implementation and oversight of AI within the government, including the National AI Advisory Committee and the National AI Initiative Office. The GAO's work underscores the government's recognition of AI's potential to enhance operational efficiency. The report provides a snapshot of these requirements as of July 2025, highlighting the ongoing efforts to integrate AI across the government. The study aims to inform Congress about the current state of AI implementation and to identify potential areas for further attention and coordination. The document serves as a valuable resource for understanding the complexities of AI governance within the U.S. federal government.

Key Concepts:

  • Government-wide requirements for AI adoption across agencies.

  • AI is a rapidly growing transformative technology with applications found in every aspect of modern life.
  • AI oversight within the federal government is not solely defined by government-wide requirements.
