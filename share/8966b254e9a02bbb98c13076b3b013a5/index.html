2025 Temasek × Resaro AI Assurance Forum

Authors: Chia Song Hwee, April Chin

The 2025 Temasek x Resaro AI Assurance Forum report focuses on accelerating the adoption of AI within organizations, particularly for Small and Medium Enterprises (SMEs). The core theme revolves around establishing robust AI assurance frameworks to mitigate risks and maximize the benefits associated with AI implementation. The document outlines key challenges such as misaligned incentives, a lack of common vocabulary, and accountability issues that often hinder successful AI adoption. It advocates a phased approach – starting with smaller implementations and scaling up as confidence grows. A significant portion of the report addresses technical hurdles, including the evolving nature of AI systems, access and transparency constraints, and the need for modular and interoperable standards. The report emphasizes the importance of ecosystem design principles and explores emerging solutions to these challenges. It highlights the need for a collaborative approach, involving all stakeholders, to ensure responsible and effective AI deployment. The document also includes contributions and insights from forum participants, providing a diverse range of perspectives on the critical considerations surrounding AI assurance. The inaugural forum, held on 26 May 2025, served as the foundation for this comprehensive analysis and recommendations. The report is a valuable resource for organizations seeking to navigate the complexities of AI adoption and establish a framework for ongoing assurance.

Key Concepts:

  • Investing in AI Assurance: Balancing risks and rewards.
  • Implementing AI Assurance: A phased approach (start small, then scale).
  • Challenges of AI Assurance: Misaligned incentives, lack of vocabulary, and accountability.
