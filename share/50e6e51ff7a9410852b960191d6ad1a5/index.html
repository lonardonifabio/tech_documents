China’s ascent to research pre-eminence in AI

Authors: Daniel W. Hook

This report, titled "DeepSeek and the New Geopolitics of AI," examines China’s rapid rise to a position of research pre-eminence within the field of Artificial Intelligence. The document is produced by Digital Science, a technology company dedicated to enhancing the efficiency and openness of the research process. Digital Science operates a portfolio of innovative brands including Altmetric, Dimensions, and others, all focused on streamlining research workflows. The report highlights the strategic importance of AI research and the competitive landscape, particularly focusing on China’s advancements. Daniel W. Hook, CEO of Digital Science and a researcher affiliated with several prominent institutions – including the Centre for Complexity Science at Imperial College London, the Department of Physics at Washington University in St. Louis, and the Research on Research Institute (RoRI) – authored the piece. The document emphasizes the need for accessible and comprehensive research knowledge systems, as exemplified by Dimensions, a platform developed in collaboration with over 100 leading research organizations. Dimensions integrates grants, publications, citations, alternative metrics, clinical trials, patents, and datasets, enabling users to quickly find relevant information, analyze research outcomes, and gain insights for strategic decision-making. The report underscores the evolving geopolitical dynamics surrounding AI research and the role of technology companies like Digital Science in supporting and facilitating advancements in this critical field. The inclusion of Dimensions and its capabilities demonstrates a commitment to providing researchers with the tools they need to navigate the complexities of modern research.
