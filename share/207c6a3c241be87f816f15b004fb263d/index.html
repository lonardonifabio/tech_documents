ISO 42001 Starter Guide: AI Governance and Implementation

Authors: Unknown, Unknown

This document serves as a starter guide for implementing the ISO 42001 standard, focusing specifically on its application within the context of Artificial Intelligence (AI) governance. It outlines the key roles and responsibilities associated with the standard, emphasizing alignment with regulations like GDPR and the EU AI Act. The core of the guide centers around establishing a robust AI governance framework, detailing the roles of critical personnel such as the Chief AI Officer (CAIO), AI Governance & Compliance Officer, AI Ethics Lead, Legal & Policy Counsel, and Stakeholder Engagement Lead. The CAIO is ultimately accountable for the AIMS initiative, setting strategic direction, managing resources, and securing budget approvals, reporting to senior management. The AI Governance & Compliance Officer is responsible for coordinating the ISO 42001 implementation, ensuring adherence to data protection regulations and developing necessary policies and procedures. The AI Ethics Lead plays a crucial role in integrating ethical considerations into the AI development lifecycle, conducting fairness and accountability assessments, and addressing potential sustainability concerns alongside ethical dilemmas. The Legal & Policy Counsel focuses on contract management, regulatory tracking, and legal risk mitigation, including intellectual property management. Finally, the Stakeholder Engagement Lead manages communication, transparency, and public trust, establishing feedback mechanisms. Beyond these core roles, the document also introduces specialized roles like Data Engineer/Data Steward, responsible for data pipeline design, quality, security, and documentation, and Data Scientist/ML Engineer, focused on model development and training. The guide utilizes a role and responsibility matrix to clearly define the expectations for each position. The document's purpose is to provide a foundational understanding of ISO 42001 and its application to AI systems, offering a structured approach to governance, compliance, and ethical considerations. It’s a practical guide for organizations seeking to adopt and implement this standard within their AI initiatives.
