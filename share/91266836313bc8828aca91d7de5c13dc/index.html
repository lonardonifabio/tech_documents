Human + Machine

Authors: Klaus Schwab, Bill Daugherty, Wade Wilson, Mark Anderson, Marc Benioff

‘Human + Machine’ is a comprehensive guidebook designed for leaders seeking to leverage the opportunities presented by Artificial Intelligence (AI) and the Fourth Industrial Revolution. The excerpt highlights the book's core argument: a reimagining of work and the development of ‘fusion skills’ are crucial for a positive future. It emphasizes that technology, particularly AI, is a primary driver of disruption and wealth creation across all sectors of the global economy. Daugherty and Wilson provide real-world examples of companies adapting their businesses and organizations to incorporate AI, framing this as the beginning of a significant business transformation. The authors stress the importance of investing in training and establishing ethical guidelines to ensure that the benefits of AI are distributed equitably across humanity. The book is presented as a roadmap for understanding the impact of AI and its role in driving future growth. The quotes from prominent figures like Klaus Schwab (founder of the World Economic Forum), Mark Anderson (Strategic News Service), and Marc Benioff (Salesforce) underscore the book's relevance and importance within the broader conversation surrounding AI's impact on business and society. The excerpt suggests a focus on human-machine collaboration, recognizing the need for both human expertise and technological capabilities to thrive in the evolving landscape. It advocates for proactive preparation – specifically, investing in training and establishing safeguards – to mitigate potential risks and maximize the positive outcomes of AI adoption. Ultimately, ‘Human + Machine’ positions itself as a critical resource for anyone serious about navigating the complexities and opportunities presented by the ongoing AI revolution and its transformative effects on industries and economies worldwide.
