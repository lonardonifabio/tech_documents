AI Infrastructure for the Agentic Era

Authors: Cisco

This Cisco white paper, titled "AI Infrastructure for the Agentic Era," explores the evolving landscape of artificial intelligence and the critical infrastructure requirements to support the rise of agentic AI. The document highlights the shift towards a workforce augmented by a diverse range of AI agents – including applications, agents, robots, and humanoids – working collaboratively with humans to manage complex workflows. It argues that this ‘Internet of Agents’ era will drive significant productivity gains and societal benefits. The paper details the rapid evolution of generative AI (GenAI), exemplified by the viral success of OpenAI’s ChatGPT-3 demo in November 2022, and emphasizes Cisco’s role in addressing the growing demand for AI infrastructure. Specifically, it focuses on the need for robust data centers, including mass-scale, large-scale, and edge AI data centers, alongside secure and efficient WAN interconnect solutions. Cisco aims to meet the diverse infrastructure needs across the entire AI lifecycle, from data preparation and processing to deployment and management. The white paper underscores the importance of a connected and orchestrated AI environment, where humans and AI agents seamlessly collaborate to optimize workflows and unlock new levels of operational efficiency. The document’s core message is that Cisco is positioning itself as a key enabler of this transformative shift, providing the necessary infrastructure to support the widespread adoption and successful implementation of agentic AI across various industries and applications. It anticipates a future where AI agents are integral components of the workforce, driving innovation and productivity.

Key Concepts:

  • The emergence of agentic AI introduces connected AI agents and people working together to orchestrate complex workflows.
  • The rapid evolution to agentic AI, exemplified by the viral success of ChatGPT-3, is driving demand for AI infrastructure.
  • Cisco is focused on meeting the diverse infrastructure needs of the AI lifecycle, including data centers and WAN interconnect.
  • The AI ecosystem relies on contributions from various participants.
  • Mass-scale AI data centers are optimized for pre-training and fine-tuning of Large Language Models (LLMs).
  • Distributed training across many GPUs is key to scaling AI performance.
  • Low latency is critical for AI fabric operation.
  • Cisco optics solutions are designed to meet the performance requirements of AI networks.
  • Optimized thermal management and port density are key considerations for AI server connectivity.
