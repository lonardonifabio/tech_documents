Cyber AI Profile: Cybersecurity Framework Profile for Artificial Intelligence

Authors: Katerina Megas, Barbara Cuthill, Marissa Dotter, Michael Garris, Ishika Khemani, Bronwyn Patrick, Noah Schiro, Julie Nethery Snyder, Mohammad Zarei

This document, NIST Internal Report NIST IR 8596 iprd, presents an Initial Preliminary Draft of the ‘Cyber AI Profile,’ a Cybersecurity Framework Profile specifically tailored for Artificial Intelligence (AI). Developed by the National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST), in collaboration with the Applied Cybersecurity Division of the Information Technology Laboratory, the National Cybersecurity Center of Excellence at MITRE, and the U.S. Department of Commerce, the report aims to provide a structured approach to understanding and managing the cybersecurity risks associated with AI systems. The document details a framework for integrating AI considerations into existing cybersecurity practices, focusing on the unique challenges posed by AI’s capabilities and vulnerabilities. It outlines key areas of concern, including bias in algorithms, adversarial attacks, data privacy, and the potential for AI systems to be used maliciously. The ‘Cyber AI Profile’ is intended to be a living document, continuously updated and refined as AI technology evolves and new threats emerge. The initial draft serves as a foundational resource for organizations seeking to proactively address cybersecurity risks within their AI deployments. The report includes a list of equipment and software used in the experimental procedure to ensure the accuracy and reproducibility of the research. The document's purpose is to facilitate a more robust and secure integration of AI into critical infrastructure and systems. It highlights the need for a specialized cybersecurity approach that acknowledges the distinct characteristics of AI, moving beyond traditional security models. The work was conducted by a team of experts from various NIST divisions and partner organizations, including former employee Mohammad Zarei. The document is available for free download via the provided DOI. The draft was finalized in December 2025, reflecting the ongoing efforts to establish standards and best practices in the rapidly developing field of AI cybersecurity. The inclusion of specific equipment and instruments underscores the practical, experimental nature of the research underpinning the ‘Cyber AI Profile’.
