AI Principles — Slovakia’s Approach to AI Governance

Authors: Unknown

This document outlines Slovakia’s developing approach to artificial intelligence governance, aligning with broader European efforts and prioritizing ethical considerations. The country is actively modeling its strategy on digital leaders such as Finland, France, and the UK, demonstrating a commitment to globally harmonized standards and interoperable digital governance. Slovakia is establishing a measured, EU-integrated AI governance model, placing significant emphasis on fundamental rights, digital inclusion, and institutional accountability. The core of this model is rooted in compliance with the EU AI Act, with initial supervisory responsibilities currently held by ÚOOÚ and VOP. As the regulatory landscape matures, it’s anticipated that these bodies will be supplemented by additional public institutions, creating a coordinated supervisory framework. A key element of Slovakia’s strategy is the development of a national AI Act, alongside ongoing engagement with EU-level policy developments, which will significantly shape the country’s future in AI governance. Currently in a preparatory phase, Slovakia’s approach indicates a gradual and deliberate strategy focused on ensuring AI systems are utilized safely, ethically, and in a manner that safeguards fundamental rights across various sectors. Notably, Slovakia has already introduced sector-specific ethical guidance, exemplified by the Ethical Guidelines for Teachers released in 2022, offering recommendations for responsible AI implementation within educational contexts. The document highlights that Slovakia has not yet enacted standalone AI legislation, but is implementing the EU AI Act. The overall tone suggests a proactive and cautious approach to AI adoption, prioritizing responsible innovation and alignment with international best practices. The document’s focus is on the initial steps and future intentions of Slovakia’s AI governance framework, emphasizing its integration with the EU’s regulatory efforts and its commitment to ethical AI development.
