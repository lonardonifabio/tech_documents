Australian Responsible AI Index 2025 Fifth Quadrant National Artificial Intelligence Centre Final Report

Authors: National Artificial Intelligence Centre

The Australian Responsible AI Index 2025, produced by the National Artificial Intelligence Centre and sponsored by the Australian Government Department of Industry, Science and Resources, presents a comprehensive analysis of Responsible AI (RAI) adoption within Australian organizations. The report’s primary objective is to assess the maturity of RAI practices across key dimensions, including fairness, accountability, transparency, explainability, and safety. It tracks RAI system maturity across five key dimensions: fairness; accountability; transparency; explainability; and safety. The index aims to understand the strategic alignment of AI implementation with broader societal and environmental values. Specifically, the report investigates organizational AI strategy maturity, examining the support and responsibility structures for developing RAI. It also analyzes the implementation of RAI practices, measuring performance and awareness of ethical principles, AI standards, and guidelines. A crucial component of the analysis focuses on identifying and assessing ‘high risk AI use cases’ – those presenting the greatest potential for ethical or societal harm. The research delves into the current state of AI usage within Australian organizations, seeking to understand the types of AI applications being deployed and their associated risks. Furthermore, the report explores the business outcomes linked to RAI adoption, attempting to quantify the value derived from responsible AI practices. The document provides a framework for evaluating the progress of Australia in adopting and implementing Responsible AI, highlighting areas of strength and identifying critical gaps that require attention. The investigation is designed to inform policy and strategic decision-making related to AI development and deployment in Australia, ensuring alignment with ethical considerations and societal values. The report’s findings are intended to contribute to a more robust and trustworthy AI ecosystem within the country.

Key Concepts:

  • Responsible AI (RAI) is designed and developed with a focus on ethical, safe, transparent, and accountable use of AI technology, in line with fair human, societal and environmental values.
  • The Index offers a comprehensive analysis of RAI adoption in Australian organisations, tracking RAI system maturity across five key dimensions.
  • Key Areas of Investigation include AI Strategy, Responsible AI Implementation, and AI Usage.
  • Developing responsible AI practices with growing momentum.
  • Actively embedding responsible AI with solid frameworks.
  • Pioneers of responsible AI scaling AI at pace.
  • Mature implementation with a high number of responsible AI practices.
  • Significant AI experience across organizations.
  • Alignment of AI strategy with business objectives.
  • Focus on security applications within AI usage.
