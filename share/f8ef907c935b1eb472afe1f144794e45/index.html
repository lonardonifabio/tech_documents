Cyber Resilience Act versus EU AI Act A Mapping Exercise

Authors: Sean Musch, Michael Borrelli, Charles Kerrigan, Dr. Amritha Subhayan Krishnan, Arnoud Engelfriet, Indra Joshi, Maryam Ghadrdan (PhD), Debbie Reyno

This report, published by AI & Partners, provides a mapping exercise comparing the Cyber Resilience Act (CRA) and the EU AI Act. AI & Partners, a firm dedicated to defending digital rights and promoting responsible AI development, produced this document. The organization’s core mission is to ensure AI is used safely and ethically, mitigating the risks associated with uncontrolled AI systems. The report likely delves into the key differences and potential overlaps between these two regulatory frameworks, focusing on how the CRA’s approach to cybersecurity and risk management interacts with the EU AI Act’s broader focus on AI governance and potential harms. AI & Partners advocates for a future where AI unlocks potential rather than causing harm, emphasizing the importance of trust and responsible development. The firm’s activities include direct technical support, policy engagement, global advocacy, and participation in industry groups like AI Commons. The document highlights the dangers of ‘unknown AI adoption’ and the need for organizations to build AI systems they can trust.
