Statistical Methods for Machine Learning

Authors: Larry Wasserman

This document excerpt details the course 'Statistical Methods for Machine Learning' offered by Larry Wasserman at Carnegie Mellon University. The course serves as an introduction to modern statistical machine learning techniques, focusing on both the theoretical foundations and practical applications of these methods. Key topics covered include density estimation, nonparametric regression, linear regression, sparsity, nonparametric classifiers, random forests, clustering, graphical models, directed graphical models, causal inference, minimax theory, nonparametric Bayesian inference, conformal prediction, differential privacy, optimal transport and Wasserstein distance, two sample testing, and dimension reduction. The course also incorporates boosting and support vector machines, alongside online learning methodologies. A significant portion of the curriculum is dedicated to understanding the underlying theory and mathematical principles behind these techniques. The course emphasizes a rigorous approach to statistical machine learning, requiring a solid understanding of intermediate statistical theory (pre-requisite 36-705) and regression (pre-requisite 10-707). Course materials consist primarily of lecture notes, which are made available online. Recommended supplementary materials include 'The Elements of Statistical Learning' by Hastie, Tibshirani, and Friedman, accessible online. Larry Wasserman, the instructor, holds office hours on Tuesdays from 12:00 PM to 1:00 PM in Baker Hall 132G, and can be contacted via email at larry@cmu.edu. Teaching assistants' information will be available on the course website. The course is held on Tuesdays and Thursdays from 1:30 PM to 2:50 PM in POS 152. The course aims to provide students with a comprehensive understanding of the statistical methods used in machine learning, preparing them for advanced study or practical applications in the field.

Key Concepts:

  • Introduction to Statistical Machine Learning
  • Study of modern methods and underlying theory
  • Pre-requisites: 36-705 and 10-707
  • Conformal Prediction - a method for prediction with confidence intervals
  • Replacing true loss with a surrogate loss for easier minimization.
  • Logistic regression as a result of replacing nonconvex loss with log(1 + exp(Y(X)))
  • Adaboost algorithm using exp(Y(X)) as a surrogate loss.
  • Density estimation as a technique for estimating probability distributions without strong assumptions.
