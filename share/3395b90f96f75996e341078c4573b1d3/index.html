Authors: World Economic Forum

Key Concepts:

  • The rapid commercialization of emerging technologies is reshaping workflows, business models and talent pipelines.
  • Artificial intelligence (AI) shifts from experimentation to integration, deepening uncertainty about its implications.
  • The views of business executives on the impact of AI vary globally.
  • The potential for technological advancement to exacerbate social fractures, leading to unemployment and instability.
  • The shift towards human-AI collaboration and incremental transformation across industries as a more realistic approach to AI integration.
  • The importance of early investment in training, digital infrastructure, and AI governance to mitigate risks and capitalize on emerging technologies.
  • The need for retraining due to unemployment and the rise of AI.
  • The challenge of establishing governance and safety standards for AI technologies.
  • The potential for AI to drive economic instability through outages and geopolitical restrictions.
  • Transparent and responsible AI deployment and data governance become key sources of trust, reputation and competitive differentiation.
