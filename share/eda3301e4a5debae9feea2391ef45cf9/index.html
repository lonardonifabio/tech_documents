This document, the "AI Ecosystem Brief 2025 Edition Germany," published in September 2025, is a publication by AI & Partners, focusing on the evolving landscape of artificial intelligence, particularly within the German context. The report highlights AI & Partners’ mission to defend and extend digital rights for users globally who are at risk due to AI. It emphasizes a responsible approach to AI development and deployment, advocating for trust and innovation while mitigating potential harm. The organization’s strategy involves a multi-faceted approach including direct technical support, policy engagement, global advocacy, grassroots professional services, regulatory interventions, and participation in industry groups like AI Commons. The document underscores the importance of proactive measures to ensure AI benefits humanity rather than causing detrimental consequences. AI & Partners’ core belief is that AI should unlock potential and foster innovation, rather than leading to fear and loss of control. The report is authored by Sean Musch and Michael Borrelli, and provides contact information for further inquiries. It’s a strategic overview of the organization’s activities and its commitment to responsible AI practices. The document’s purpose is to inform stakeholders about AI & Partners’ role in shaping the future of AI governance and its impact on digital rights.
