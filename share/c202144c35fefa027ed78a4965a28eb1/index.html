The Widening AI Value Gap Build for the Future 2025

Authors: Jessica Apotheker, Vinciane Beauchene, Nicolas de Bellefonds, Patrick Forth, Marc Roman Franke, Michael Grebe, Nina Kataeva, Santeri Kirvelä, Djon Kleine, Romain de Laubier, Vladimir Lukic, Amanda Luther, Mary Martin, Jeff Walters, Christoph Schweizer

This report, titled "The Widening AI Value Gap Build for the Future 2025," produced by Boston Consulting Group (BCG), examines the disparity in AI value generation among companies. The research highlights that while a small percentage of companies are successfully leveraging AI to drive revenue, cash flow improvements, and shareholder returns, the majority are not realizing significant value from their AI investments. BCG’s analysis reveals that only approximately 5% of companies are effectively generating substantial bottom-line value through AI. The report delves into the factors contributing to this "value gap," including strategic misalignment, talent shortages, and the adoption of inappropriate technologies. It identifies key strategies for accelerating AI value creation, such as pursuing multi-year strategic AI ambitions, reshaping business processes with AI, adopting an AI-first operating model, and securing the necessary talent. Furthermore, the document acknowledges that AI maturity varies significantly across different sectors and regions, emphasizing the need for tailored approaches. The report includes definitions of AI and details the survey methodology used in the research. The core argument is that a proactive and strategic approach to AI implementation is crucial for companies to avoid falling behind in this rapidly evolving landscape. The document provides a critical assessment of current AI investment outcomes and offers actionable insights for organizations seeking to maximize the return on their AI investments.

Key Concepts:

  • The widening AI value gap exists, with a small number of companies successfully generating significant AI value.
  • Companies are struggling to translate AI investments into tangible bottom-line results.
  • Strategic AI ambitions and AI-first operating models are crucial for maximizing AI value.
