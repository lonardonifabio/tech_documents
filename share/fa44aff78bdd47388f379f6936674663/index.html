Programming for Computations – Python

Authors: Svein Linge, Hans Petter Langtangen

This book, "Programming for Computations – Python," provides a gentle introduction to numerical simulations using Python. Authored by Svein Linge and Hans Petter Langtangen, it focuses on practical techniques for solving computational problems. The book is geared towards individuals with a basic understanding of programming concepts who are interested in applying Python to scientific and engineering simulations. It covers topics such as numerical methods, data structures, and algorithms relevant to computational science and engineering. The authors, Svein Linge from the University College of Southeast Norway and Hans Petter Langtangen from Simula Research Laboratory, aim to equip readers with the skills to perform efficient and accurate numerical computations. The book includes examples and exercises to reinforce learning. It's part of the "Texts in Computational Science and Engineering" series, published by Springer. The book's ISBNs are 978-3-319-32427-2 and 978-3-319-32428-9 (eBook). The mathematical subject classification codes are 26-01, 34A05, 34A30, 34A34, 39-01, 40-01, 65D15, 65D25, 65D30, 68-01, 68N01, 68N19, 68N30, 70-01, 92D25, 97-04, 97U50.
