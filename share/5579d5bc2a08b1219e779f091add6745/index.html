This document, the "AI Ecosystem Brief 2025 Edition," is a publication by AI & Partners, released in Estonia in August 2025. It outlines the organization's mission to defend and extend digital rights for users at risk globally, particularly in the context of artificial intelligence. AI & Partners employs a multi-faceted approach, combining technical support, policy engagement, global advocacy, professional services, regulatory interventions, and participation in industry groups like AI Commons. The report emphasizes the importance of responsible AI development and deployment, acknowledging the potential for harm when teams lose control of AI systems. It highlights the need to foster trust and innovation through responsible handling of AI. The document details AI & Partners' commitment to ensuring AI benefits humanity rather than causing detriment. The organization’s work is driven by a belief that AI should unlock potential, not cause harm, and they actively work to mitigate risks associated with uncontrolled AI development. The report is a publication of AI & Partners and provides contact information for Michael Charles Borrelli, the Director, at m.borrelli@ai-and-partners.com. It’s a strategic document intended to showcase the organization's role in navigating the evolving landscape of AI and its impact on human rights and digital freedoms. The document’s focus on regulatory interventions and participation in industry groups suggests a proactive approach to shaping the future of AI governance.
