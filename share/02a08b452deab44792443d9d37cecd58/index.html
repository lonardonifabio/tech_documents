The AI Tech Stack: A Primer for Tech and Cyber Policy

Authors: Kemba Walden, Devin Lynch

This report serves as a foundational primer for public policy and cybersecurity professionals seeking to comprehend the rapidly evolving and complex landscape of Artificial Intelligence (AI). Specifically, it addresses the intersection of AI with cybersecurity concerns, emphasizing the need for deliberate and layered security solutions across the entire AI technology stack. The document outlines the various components of this stack, recognizing its dynamic and decentralized nature. The core objective is to equip readers with the knowledge necessary to strategically approach security challenges within each layer of the AI tech stack, both horizontally (across layers) and vertically (within layers). The report is produced by the Paladin Global Institute, an organization dedicated to safeguarding global critical infrastructure from cyber, AI, and deep tech threats. Their mission involves research, advocacy, policy recommendations, and collaboration between public and private sectors to foster innovation and enhance online safety. The authors, Kemba Walden and Devin Lynch, bring significant expertise to the analysis, with Walden having previously served as Acting United States National Cyber Director and Lynch holding a role at the Office of the National Cyber Director (ONCD). The report acknowledges contributions from a team of reviewers including Roberta Campbell, Eric Wengrowski, Ben Colman, Fred Heiding, Chris Inglis, Ciaran Martin, and David Hoffm, highlighting the collaborative effort behind this foundational document. The document’s focus on the AI tech stack underscores the importance of a holistic security approach, considering the interconnectedness of different AI technologies and their potential vulnerabilities. It’s a critical resource for those tasked with developing and implementing policies related to AI and cybersecurity, aiming to bridge the gap between technical understanding and actionable policy decisions. The report’s emphasis on collaboration between sectors is key to effectively addressing the multifaceted challenges posed by AI’s advancement.

Key Concepts:

  • The report aims to provide a primer on the AI technology stack for public policy and cybersecurity practitioners.
  • The AI technology stack is described as dynamic, diffuse, and emerging, requiring deliberate security solutions across its layers.
  • The Paladin Global Institute’s mission focuses on protecting critical infrastructure from cyber and AI threats through research, advocacy, and collaboration.
  • The AI Triad (algorithm, compute, and data) forms the core of AI models.
  • The AI Tech Stack comprises five layers: model, infrastructure, data, application, and governance, each with distinct roles and interdependencies.
  • Governance Layer is crucial for risk management and accountability in AI deployments.
  • Building AI systems that inspire trust and increase resilience is crucial for societal advancement.
  • A structured framework (five-layer AI technology stack) is needed to systematically address security and safety vulnerabilities in AI.
  • Effective security in AI is about creating a trusted foundation for innovation, not restrictive limitations.
