European Commission : Draft Guidance Article 73 AI Act - Incident Reporting (High -Risk AI Systems)

Authors: European Commission

This document presents draft guidance related to Incident Reporting for High-Risk AI Systems, specifically referencing Article 73 of the proposed AI Act developed by the European Commission. The primary objective is to establish a framework for reporting incidents and malfunctions involving high-risk AI systems, ensuring accountability and promoting responsible AI development and deployment. The guidance outlines key definitions crucial to understanding the context of incident reporting, including definitions of the AI Act itself, ‘incident,’ ‘malfunction,’ and the concept of ‘directly or indirectly’ caused harm. It aims to clarify the responsibilities of organizations utilizing high-risk AI systems in identifying, documenting, and reporting any adverse events or unintended consequences. The document likely details the types of incidents that require reporting, the required level of detail, and the channels for submitting reports. Furthermore, it addresses the complexities of determining causality, specifically whether an incident was ‘directly or indirectly’ caused by the AI system. The draft guidance is intended to support the implementation of the AI Act, which seeks to regulate AI systems based on their risk level, with high-risk systems subject to stringent requirements regarding transparency, accountability, and human oversight. The document's release date is September 2025, indicating it's a pre-final version of the guidance. The structure of the document includes a contents section, key definitions, and sections dedicated to explaining these definitions in detail. The focus on incident reporting is central to the AI Act's approach to managing the potential risks associated with AI, emphasizing proactive monitoring and rapid response to any issues that may arise. It’s a foundational document for understanding the reporting obligations associated with high-risk AI systems under the forthcoming AI Act.
