AI Ecosystem Brief 2025 Edition Ireland

Authors: Sean Musch, Michael Borrelli, Charles Kerrigan, James Corcoran, Mark Kelly, Tony Moroney, Marie O’Riordan, Roisin Bennett, Kevin McGivern, Vivian O’Brien, Clio Louise McCormack, Hugo Grattirola, Ben O’Bright

This document, the "AI Ecosystem Brief 2025 Edition Ireland," published by AI & Partners, outlines the organization's mission and approach to safeguarding digital rights within the evolving landscape of artificial intelligence. The report, authored by a team including Sean Musch and Michael Borrelli, focuses on the critical need for responsible AI development and deployment to mitigate potential harms and foster trust. AI & Partners operates by combining technical support, policy engagement, global advocacy, and regulatory interventions to champion fundamental rights for users facing risks associated with AI. They actively participate in industry groups like AI Commons, demonstrating a collaborative approach to addressing these challenges. The core philosophy of AI & Partners centers around the belief that AI should unlock potential rather than cause harm, recognizing the negative consequences of losing control over AI systems. The organization’s work is driven by a desire to empower users with AI they can trust and support, emphasizing long-term sustainability and responsible innovation. The document highlights the importance of proactive measures to prevent misuse and ensure ethical considerations are at the forefront of AI development. It’s a strategic document designed to guide stakeholders in navigating the complexities of the AI ecosystem and promoting a future where AI benefits humanity. The report’s purpose is to provide a framework for responsible AI practices and to advocate for user rights in the age of artificial intelligence. It underscores the need for ongoing vigilance and collaboration to ensure AI’s positive impact.
