Key Concepts:

  • L’Intelligenza Artificiale represents a profound transformation vector of the 21st century, impacting decision-making processes across various systems.
  • AI systems are increasingly acting as infrastructural decision-making tools, directly influencing rights, opportunities, and power dynamics.
  • The application of AI in high-stakes sectors like healthcare, finance, and public administration carries significant implications for individuals and society.
  • The ISO 26000 standard provides a framework for ethical considerations, particularly when applied to artificial intelligence.
  • Accountability in AI systems requires a broader understanding of decision-making distribution beyond individual legal responsibility.
  • Transparency involves explaining and justifying automated decisions to those affected by them.
  • The accountability and transparency of AI systems are emergent properties of the overall organizational system.
  • Dynamic Ethics: The ethics of AI is not static but evolves due to changing data and contexts.
  • Continuous Monitoring: The need for ongoing observation of AI system outputs and effects to detect deviations and negative impacts.
  • Alignment and Governance: Maintaining alignment between stated values and actual system behavior through defined indicators and intervention procedures.
