Global Cybersecurity Outlook 2026 INSIGHT REPORT

Authors: World Economic Forum

The "Global Cybersecurity Outlook 2026" report, published by the World Economic Forum in January 2026, provides a comprehensive analysis of the evolving cybersecurity landscape. The report’s core focus is on anticipating and addressing the key challenges and trends shaping cybersecurity over the next five years. It begins with a historical overview of the previous Global Cybersecurity Outlook reports, followed by an executive summary outlining the critical findings. The document then delves into five key areas: a review of CEOs’ priorities in a dynamically changing cyber environment, an examination of the major trends reshaping cybersecurity, and a detailed exploration of specific threats and vulnerabilities. Notably, the report highlights the transformative impact of Artificial Intelligence (AI) on both offensive and defensive cybersecurity strategies, recognizing AI's accelerating role in risk assessment and response. Geopolitical factors are identified as a central and defining element of the cybersecurity landscape, influencing strategies and attack vectors. The report further investigates the evolving nature of cybercrime, specifically focusing on the increasing role of AI and fraud, alongside the global response efforts. A crucial section addresses cyber resilience, emphasizing its importance in protecting economic value, and examines the challenges of securing complex and often opaque supply chains, particularly considering the risks of concentration. The report also investigates drivers of cyber inequity, anticipating potential disparities in cybersecurity preparedness and capabilities. Finally, it addresses emerging threat vectors that are operating in relative silence, suggesting a shift in attack methodologies. The document concludes with a summary of key findings and includes a methodology appendix, contributor details, and endnotes for further research. The report’s findings are presented as a collaborative effort endorsed by the World Economic Forum, acknowledging that they may not fully represent the views of all its members or stakeholders. The publication is protected by copyright and prohibits reproduction without permission.

Key Concepts:

  • The document is a collaborative insight report on global cybersecurity trends, published by the World Economic Forum.
  • CEOs' priorities in a shifting cyber landscape are a key focus, including the impact of AI and geopolitics.
  • Cyber resilience and securing supply chains are identified as crucial elements in safeguarding economic value and mitigating risks.
  • Polarization and uneven progress in the global economy, particularly within the cybersecurity sector.
  • Increasing complexity and unpredictability driven by multiple factors, leading to a fragmented world and rising threats.
  • The critical importance of collaboration to address the widening tech divide and growing cyber resilience gap.
  • AI-related vulnerabilities are the fastest-growing cyber risk.
  • There's a shift in AI risk concerns, with adversarial capabilities decreasing in importance and data leaks associated with genAI becoming a leading concern.
  • The ‘AI arms race’ between attackers and defenders is intensifying, with a focus on unintended data exposure through generative and agentic systems.
