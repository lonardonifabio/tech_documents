OWASP AI Maturity Assessment Version V1.0

Authors: Unknown

The OWASP AI Maturity Assessment Version V1.0, released in August 11, 2025, provides a framework for organizations to evaluate and improve their approach to Artificial Intelligence (AI) development and deployment. This document outlines a comprehensive model centered around Responsible AI principles, encompassing ethical considerations, governance, data management, privacy, design, implementation, verification, and operations. The assessment model addresses the shortcomings of existing maturity models by specifically targeting the unique risks and challenges associated with AI systems. It’s structured around key areas including Responsible AI – focusing on ethical values, transparency, fairness, and bias mitigation; robust Governance practices including strategy, policy, and compliance; meticulous Data Management practices ensuring data quality, integrity, and accountability; and a strong emphasis on Privacy by Design and User Control. Furthermore, the document details considerations for secure Design, Implementation, and Verification stages, alongside operational aspects like Incident and Event Management. The assessment utilizes worksheets for each key area, offering a structured approach to evaluating an organization's AI maturity. The document is part of the broader OWASP ecosystem, providing AI-specific resources and aligning with OWASP's commitment to open-source security best practices. The maturity assessment is designed to help organizations proactively manage AI risks, build trustworthy AI systems, and comply with evolving regulations. The inclusion of a glossary and appendices further enhances the document's utility for practitioners and stakeholders involved in AI development and deployment. This version represents a significant step towards standardizing AI maturity assessments and promoting responsible AI practices within the industry.

Key Concepts:

  • AI Maturity Assessment Model – A framework for evaluating AI systems.
  • Responsible AI – Focuses on ethical considerations, transparency, fairness, and bias in AI development.
  • Governance – Establishing policies, metrics, and compliance procedures for AI systems.
  • Data Management – Ensuring data quality, integrity, and accountability within AI systems.
  • Privacy – Implementing data minimization, privacy by design, and user control measures.
  • Responsible innovation through AI practices
  • Alignment of AI systems with ethical values and societal values
  • Mitigating potential negative impacts of AI on humans and the environment
  • Building stakeholder trust by incorporating ethical considerations into AI development
  • Defining a clear AI security strategy and metrics to track progress.
