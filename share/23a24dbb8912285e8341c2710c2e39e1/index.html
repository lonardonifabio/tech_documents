Ethics Index 2025

Authors: Governance Institute of Australia

The Ethics Index 2025, published by the Governance Institute of Australia, provides insights into ethical considerations within organizations. This tenth edition focuses on equipping governance and risk management professionals with the tools needed to drive better governance. The Institute advocates for a community of governance professionals, serving members across listed, unlisted, and not-for-profit sectors. It offers chartered governance accreditation, short courses, certificates, micro-credentials, and postgraduate study through the Governance Academy. The Institute’s activities include thought leadership, research projects, and news publications, leveraging its membership of the Chartered Governance Institute to monitor global trends.
