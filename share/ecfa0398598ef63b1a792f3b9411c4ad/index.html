AI Controls Framework Working Group - Draft

Authors: Marina Bregkou, Daniele Catteddu, Luca De Candia, Ken Huang, Faisal Khan, Betina Tagle, Sam Washko, Stephen Lumpe

This document excerpt represents a draft of the AI Controls Framework developed by the Cloud Security Alliance (CSA) Working Group. It outlines the official location for the working group – https://cloudsecurityalliance.org/research/working-groups/ai-controls – and details the usage rights associated with the draft. Specifically, it permits personal, informational, noncommercial use, prohibits modification or redistribution, and requires attribution to the Cloud Security Alliance when quoting portions under Fair Use provisions of the United States Copyright Act. The document acknowledges contributions from CSA Global staff and various individuals including Marina Bregkou, Daniele Catteddu, Luca De Candia, Ken Huang, Faisal Khan, Betina Tagle, and Sam Washko. The excerpt includes a table of contents and acknowledgements section, further solidifying its role as a foundational document for the AI Controls Framework. The Cloud Security Alliance holds all copyright and trademark rights to the document, emphasizing its official status and intended use within the cybersecurity community. The document's purpose is to guide the development and implementation of controls for AI systems, aligning with CSA's broader mission of promoting security best practices in cloud computing. The draft is intended for informational purposes only and is subject to ongoing review and updates by the AI Controls Framework Working Group. The CSA’s commitment to open research and collaboration is evident through the collaborative effort of multiple contributors and the availability of the framework for download and use under specified conditions. This excerpt serves as an introduction to the framework and highlights the key terms of use and the individuals involved in its creation.

Key Concepts:

  • The AI Controls Framework Working Group's official location is provided.
  • Usage restrictions apply to the draft document, including limitations on modification, redistribution, and removal of notices.
  • Attribution is required when quoting portions of the draft under Fair Use provisions.
  • Organizations need to define control objectives for their AI security programs to enforce mandates and monitor adherence.
  • AICM (AI Control Matrix) provides a structured framework with 18 security domains and 243 controls, aligning with existing cybersecurity frameworks and emerging AI standards.
  • Unique AI challenges like model manipulation, data poisoning, and supply chain risks require specialized guidance within the AICM framework.
  • Shared Responsibility Model (AICs and CSPs)
  • Change Management for AI Systems
  • Data Lifecycle Protection through Cryptography
  • The AICM's Interoperability and Portability (IPY) domain focuses on facilitating the safe and secure exchange of AI data and models across platforms.
