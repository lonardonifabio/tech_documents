This document, the "AI Ecosystem Brief 2025 Edition Hungary," is a publication by AI & Partners, released in October 2025. It focuses on the organization's mission to defend and extend the digital rights of users globally who are at risk due to the development and deployment of artificial intelligence. AI & Partners employs a multi-faceted approach, combining direct technical support, policy engagement, global advocacy, grassroots professional services, regulatory interventions, and participation in industry groups like AI Commons, to champion fundamental rights within the evolving landscape of AI. The report highlights the organization's belief that AI should be a tool for unlocking potential rather than causing harm, emphasizing the importance of responsible AI development and deployment. AI & Partners aims to assist clients in building AI systems they can trust and stand behind, mitigating the risks associated with uncontrolled AI and fostering innovation. The document details the organization's strategy for navigating the complexities of the AI Act and promoting ethical AI practices. Contact information for Michael Charles Borrelli, Director, m.borrelli@ai-and-partners.com, is provided, along with the organization's website: https://www.ai-and-partners.com/. The brief underscores the critical role of advocacy and responsible development in shaping the future of AI. It’s a strategic overview of AI & Partners’ activities and philosophy.
