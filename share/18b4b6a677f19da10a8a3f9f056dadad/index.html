The AI Leadership Blueprint: A Practical Guide to Responsible Workforce Transformation

Authors: Jill Angerbauer, Penny Atkins, Jeb Dean, Meghan Dovick, Robin Huling, Mary Killela, JUDr. Nora Kurzova, Jimmy McDonough, Seneca Perri Moore, Christian Napier, Rebecca Peterson, Anneliese Pixton, Olivia Sanders, Carolyn Scheese, Jerome Soller, David Stringfellow, Kevin Williams

This document, "The AI Leadership Blueprint," provides a practical guide focused on responsible workforce transformation in the context of artificial intelligence. Authored by a diverse group of experts from various institutions including Harvard, the University of Utah, and the State of Utah, the blueprint addresses the strategic considerations and operational steps required for organizations to successfully integrate AI while mitigating potential risks. The authors leverage a combination of human expertise and AI-assisted tools, specifically utilizing Large Language Models (LLMs) such as OpenAI's ChatGPT, Tropics, Claude, Google's Gemini, Perplexity, and others, to generate the content. The blueprint emphasizes a holistic approach, encompassing not only technological implementation but also the necessary organizational and cultural shifts. It’s designed to equip leaders with the knowledge and frameworks to navigate the complexities of AI adoption, ensuring a sustainable and ethical transition. The document’s content is presented as the authors’ individual views and opinions, and does not represent any official policy. The guide’s scope includes considerations for workforce development, ethical AI practices, and the strategic alignment of AI initiatives with overall business goals. The authors’ combined experience across healthcare, technology, and higher education contributes to a robust and nuanced perspective on this rapidly evolving field. The document’s creation involved a deliberate use of LLM-assisted amplification tools, showcasing a forward-thinking approach to knowledge creation and dissemination. The blueprint aims to be a valuable resource for organizations seeking to harness the power of AI responsibly and effectively.
