First Key Update Capabilities and Risk Implications

Authors: Yoshua Bengio, Stephen Clare, Carina Prunkl, Maksym Andriushchenko, Ben Bucknall, Philip Fox, Tiancheng Hu, Cameron Jones, Sam Manning, Nestor Maslej, Vasilios Mavroudis, Conor McGlynn, Malcolm Murray, Shalaleh Rismani, Charlotte Stix, Lucia Velasco, Nicole Wheeler, Daniel Privitera

This document, titled "First Key Update Capabilities and Risk Implications," is the first key update related to the International AI Safety Report, scheduled for release in October 2025. It’s a report produced by the LawZero/Mila - Quebec AI Institute, with significant input from an international advisory panel. The Expert Advisory Panel, comprising representatives from 30 countries, the United Nations, the European Union, and the Organisation for Economic Co-operation and Development, provided technical feedback. Crucially, the report and its advisory panel do not endorse any specific policy or regulatory approach, focusing instead on a comprehensive assessment of AI safety. The document details key update capabilities and associated risks related to artificial intelligence. The advisory panel itself is a diverse group, reflecting global engagement in AI safety research and governance. The membership details are available at internationalaisafetyreport.org/expert-advisory-panel. The report’s creation highlights the growing international concern surrounding the development and deployment of AI, emphasizing the need for ongoing monitoring and evaluation of potential risks. The document’s purpose is to inform stakeholders about the current state of AI safety research and to contribute to the development of responsible AI practices. It represents a critical step in the ongoing effort to ensure that AI systems are developed and used in a way that benefits humanity. The report's focus on capabilities and risks underscores the importance of proactive risk management within the rapidly evolving field of artificial intelligence. The document’s release in 2025 signifies a commitment to continuous updates and refinements as AI technology advances.

Key Concepts:

  • The report is an international advisory body providing technical feedback.
  • The Expert Advisory Panel comprises representatives from various international organizations and countries.
  • The report and its panel do not endorse specific policy or regulatory approaches.
  • General-purpose AI systems are continuously improving in performance and capabilities.
  • Despite limitations and errors, AI systems are achieving significant breakthroughs in problem-solving and task completion.
  • Evaluation of AI capabilities is evolving and faces challenges, as demonstrated by the performance on 'Humanity's Last Exam'.
  • AI tools are used daily by a significant portion of professional software developers.
  • Trust in AI tools remains low despite their widespread use, with many developers expressing mistrust.
  • The impact of AI tools on developer productivity varies significantly depending on the study, experience level, and complexity of the codebase.
  • Developers are implementing safeguards due to incomplete evidence about real-world risks.
