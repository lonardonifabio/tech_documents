Key Concepts:

  • Model parameters are the weights and biases adjusted during training to capture language patterns.
  • Scale refers to the total number of parameters in an LLM, impacting performance and compute requirements.
  • Emergent abilities are capabilities that appear unexpectedly in LLMs beyond a certain scale threshold, representing a shift in model behavior.
  • Reliability of LLMs is defined by consistency, factual accuracy, and resistance to adversarial prompts.
  • A trustworthy LLM exhibits verifiable answers, signals uncertainty, and avoids biased content, often prefacing uncertain responses with qualifiers and offering source retrieval.
