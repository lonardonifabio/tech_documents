AI Ecosystem Brief 2025 Edition

Authors: Sean Musch, Michael Borrelli, Charles Kerrigan, Harald Friedl, Jeannette Gorzala, Josef Baker -Brunnbauer, Andreas Hladky, pwc

This document, the "AI Ecosystem Brief 2025 Edition," is a publication by AI & Partners, a company dedicated to defending and extending the digital rights of users impacted by artificial intelligence. The report focuses on the critical need for responsible AI development and deployment, highlighting the potential risks associated with uncontrolled AI adoption, including confusion, risk, and reputational damage. AI & Partners aims to mitigate these risks by providing tools and services to enable safe AI usage for organizations and their clients, emphasizing the importance of governance, monitoring, and control. The organization's approach combines direct technical support, policy engagement, global advocacy, regulatory interventions, and participation in industry groups like AI Commons. The core mission is to build AI systems that users can trust and stand behind, ensuring responsible innovation and safeguarding against potential harm. The report underscores the need for proactive measures to manage AI risks and promote ethical considerations within the rapidly evolving AI landscape. Contact information and a website link are provided for further information and engagement with AI & Partners.

Key Concepts:

  • AI & Partners' mission to defend digital rights in the AI age.
  • The importance of responsible AI handling to avoid harm and foster trust.
  • The need for tools to manage and govern AI adoption for safety and control.
  • Regulatory sandboxes for AI testing
  • Ethical considerations alongside technology impact assessments
  • Data fairness, accountability, autonomy, and societal impacts in AI
  • Preparing the education system for AI-induced curricular changes
  • Strategic AI deployment to strengthen key sectors (healthcare, mobility, industry, climate innovation)
  • Investment in digital infrastructure to enable AI adoption
  • Secure and scalable data environments for responsible AI innovation
