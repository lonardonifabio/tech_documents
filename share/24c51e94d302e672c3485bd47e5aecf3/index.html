Authors: Adele Jashari, David Shrier, Aldo Faisal

This document, "Sovereign AI in Defence: Strategic Autonomy and Collaborative Opportunity," explores the critical need for nations to develop and deploy Sovereign AI systems within the defense sector. It argues that increasing geopolitical instability, particularly in grey-zone conflicts, necessitates a shift towards autonomous defense capabilities. The report defines Sovereign AI within the defense domain, emphasizing strategic autonomy and the potential for collaborative development. Key strategic drivers identified include the escalating threat of conflict, accelerated technological diffusion, and the evolving nature of warfare – specifically, blended warfare across domains (land, sea, air, and cyber). The document analyzes the Russia-Ukraine conflict as a prime example of these dynamics, highlighting the implications for capability development and the importance of non-compromising interoperability. It delves into the rationale for prioritizing Sovereign AI, advocating for systems that maintain operational independence and minimize vulnerabilities. The analysis considers the expansion of the defense concept to encompass blended warfare and the need for AI systems that can operate effectively across diverse environments. The report underscores the urgency of addressing these challenges through strategic investment and collaborative research, ultimately aiming to enhance national security and resilience in an increasingly complex and contested global landscape. The document’s focus is on the strategic considerations and technological implications of developing autonomous defense systems, particularly in the context of heightened geopolitical risks and the rapid advancement of AI technologies. It’s a strategic assessment of the future of defense, centered around the concept of Sovereign AI.
