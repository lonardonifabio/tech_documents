AI Risk Repository - Frameworks Slide Deck

Authors: Critch & Russell, Meek et al.

This slide deck provides an overview of the AI Risk Repository, a database and two taxonomies documenting AI risks. The repository was created through a systematic search of existing frameworks, taxonomies, and classifications of AI risks, encompassing 74 documents. The primary goal is to synthesize knowledge on AI risks from both academic and industry sources, identifying common themes and gaps in understanding. The slide deck specifically focuses on the frameworks presented within the repository, offering a holistic view of how AI risks are currently conceptualized and categorized. It details the selection criteria used for the documents, emphasizing a focus on structured taxonomies, multi-sectoral coverage, original frameworks, and peer-reviewed status (journal papers, preprints, conference papers, or industry reports). The slide deck serves as a resource for researchers and practitioners seeking to understand the diverse approaches to classifying AI risks. It allows users to explore the variety of frameworks developed by different authors and to bookmark relevant frameworks for future reference. Specifically, it includes a detailed examination of Document 1, "TASRA: a Taxonomy and Analysis of Societal-Scale Risks from AI" by Critch & Russell (2023), and Document 9, "Managing the ethical and risk implications of rapid advances in artificial intelligence: A literature review" by Meek et al. (2016). The document also references Document 17, though the full content is not provided in the excerpt. The repository aims to facilitate a more comprehensive understanding of the evolving landscape of AI risks and promote collaboration in addressing these challenges. The slide deck is intended to be a starting point for further investigation into the broader AI Risk Repository.
