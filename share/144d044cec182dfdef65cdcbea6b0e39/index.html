REGULATION (EU) 2023/2854 of the European Parliament and of the Council on harmonised rules on fair access to and use of data

Authors: European Parliament, Council of the European Union

This document is a European Union regulation, specifically Regulation (EU) 2023/2854, which establishes harmonized rules concerning fair access to and use of data. It was adopted by the European Parliament and the Council of the European Union on December 13, 2023. The regulation aims to facilitate data sharing and reuse across various sectors, recognizing the transformative impact of data-driven technologies on the economy. It acknowledges the increasing volume and potential value of data generated by connected products and the importance of high-quality, interoperable data for fostering competitiveness, innovation, and sustainable economic growth. The regulation addresses barriers to data sharing, promoting a more open and accessible data ecosystem within the European Union. It’s a key piece of legislation designed to support the digital economy and align with the broader goals of the European Union's data strategy. The regulation's scope is broad, encompassing data from diverse domains and intended to be applied across numerous industries. The document highlights the need for data to be used and reused without compromising its quality or quantity, a critical element for driving innovation and economic development. The legislative process involved the European Commission's proposal, transmission to national parliaments, and consideration of opinions from the European Central Bank, the European Economic and Social Committee, and the Committee of the Regions, demonstrating a comprehensive approach to developing this regulatory framework. The regulation builds upon existing legislation, specifically amending Regulation (EU) 2017/2394 and Directive (EU) 2020/1828.
