The Widening AI Value Gap Build for the Future 2025

Authors: Jessica Apotheker, Vinciane Beauchene, Nicolas de Bellefonds, Patrick Forth, Marc Roman Franke, Michael Grebe, Nina Kataeva, Santeri Kirvelä, Djon Kleine, Romain de Laubier, Vladimir Lukic, Amanda Luther, Mary Martin, Jeff Walters, Christoph Schweizer

This report, titled "The Widening AI Value Gap Build for the Future 2025," produced by Boston Consulting Group (BCG), examines the disparity in AI value generation among companies. The document highlights that while a small percentage of companies are successfully leveraging AI to drive revenue, cash flow improvements, and shareholder returns, the majority are not realizing significant value from their AI investments. BCG’s research indicates that only approximately 5% of companies are effectively utilizing AI to achieve substantial bottom-line results. The report delves into the factors contributing to this "value gap," including strategic misalignment, inadequate talent acquisition, and the failure to adopt an AI-first operating model. It explores how leading companies are pursuing multi-year AI ambitions, reshaping processes with impactful AI solutions, and securing the necessary talent and technology. The document also addresses the varying levels of AI maturity across different sectors and regions, emphasizing the need for tailored strategies. The core argument is that a proactive and strategic approach to AI implementation is crucial for companies to avoid falling behind and capitalize on the transformative potential of artificial intelligence. The report provides insights into how to accelerate AI value creation, focusing on strategic alignment, talent development, and the adoption of fit-for-purpose technology and data. It serves as a critical assessment of the current state of AI adoption and offers guidance for organizations seeking to maximize their return on AI investments. The document's findings underscore the importance of a holistic strategy that considers not only technological advancements but also organizational culture, talent, and data readiness.
