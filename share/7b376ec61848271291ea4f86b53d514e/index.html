AI Risk Mitigation Database and Taxonomy Development

Authors: MIT Airisk

This document details the creation of an AI Risk Mitigation Database and a corresponding Taxonomy, undertaken by the MIT Airisk team. The project’s primary goal was to consolidate and organize existing mitigation strategies proposed by various organizations to address the risks associated with Artificial Intelligence. The team systematically identified and extracted mitigations from 13 documents, resulting in a database containing 831 distinct mitigations. These mitigations were then used to construct a draft AI Risk Mitigation Taxonomy, categorized into four core areas: Governance & Oversight Controls, Technical & Security Controls, Operational Process Controls, and Transparency & Accountability Controls. Governance & Oversight Controls focus on establishing human oversight and decision-making protocols. Technical & Security Controls emphasize safeguarding AI systems and limiting model behavior through engineering and security measures. Operational Process Controls encompass the management frameworks for AI system deployment, usage, monitoring, incident handling, and validation. Finally, Transparency & Accountability Controls involve formal disclosure practices and verification mechanisms to ensure external scrutiny of AI systems. The most prevalent type of mitigation identified was Operational Process Controls, appearing in all 13 source documents (n = 295). The draft Taxonomy further breaks down these controls into 23 subcategories, providing a structured approach to understanding and managing AI risks. The project’s output, a comprehensive AI Risk Mitigation Database and Taxonomy, represents a valuable resource for organizations seeking to proactively address the challenges and risks posed by the increasing adoption of AI technologies. The Airtable view provides access to the full database. This work highlights the need for standardized approaches to risk management within the rapidly evolving field of AI.

Key Concepts:

  • AI Risk Mitigation Database: A centralized repository of AI risk mitigation strategies.
  • AI Risk Taxonomy: A structured classification system for organizing AI risk mitigation techniques.
  • Governance & Oversight Controls: Formal mechanisms for human oversight and decision-making related to AI systems.
  • Technical & Security Controls: Safeguards designed to protect and constrain AI systems.
  • Operational Process Controls: Frameworks governing the deployment, usage, and monitoring of AI systems.
  • Problems with AI output quality and consistency in extraction and classification.
  • AI assistants hallucinating or combining mitigations inaccurately.
  • Manual quality assurance process involving detailed review and cross-checking of extracted and classified mitigations.
  • Linking risk sources to corresponding risk measures is a promising approach.
  • Increasing interoperability of AI risk knowledge through taxonomies and ontologies is crucial.
