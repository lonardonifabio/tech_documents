AI Audit Checklist

Authors: Kamran Iqbal

This document presents an AI Audit Checklist designed to guide organizations in assessing their AI governance, compliance, and risk management practices. The checklist is structured around key areas, including AI Governance Policies, Regulatory Compliance, and AI Risk Management & Auditing. It provides a framework for determining compliance status through a series of targeted audit questions, requiring responses of 'Yes/No' and supporting remarks. Specifically, it examines whether the organization has documented AI governance frameworks, if AI risk management processes align with established standards like ISO 42001, NIST AI RMF, and GDPR, and whether an AI ethics committee is in place. The checklist also delves into regulatory compliance, focusing on adherence to regulations such as GDPR, CCPA, and sector-specific rules, alongside the documentation and legal justification of AI data processing activities. Crucially, it assesses the design of AI models for transparency, explainability, and accountability, demanding review of system documentation, model explanations, and established accountability mechanisms. Furthermore, the document emphasizes the importance of a risk assessment framework specifically tailored for AI deployment, prompting a thorough evaluation of methodologies and past risk reports. The checklist's ultimate goal is to provide a practical tool for organizations to proactively identify and mitigate potential risks associated with AI implementation, ensuring alignment with relevant regulations and ethical considerations. It's intended for use by Certified Trainers and Consultants, as indicated by the document's title and source website (www.ctc-global.com), suggesting a professional training and consulting context. The checklist's modular structure allows for targeted audits based on an organization's specific AI initiatives and regulatory landscape. The inclusion of 'Remarks' fields encourages detailed documentation and justification for each audit finding, facilitating a more comprehensive and actionable audit process. The checklist's reference to ISO 42001 and NIST AI RMF highlights a focus on internationally recognized standards for AI risk management, demonstrating a commitment to best practices.

Key Concepts:

  • The document provides an AI Audit Checklist for assessing compliance and governance related to AI systems.
  • It outlines key areas of assessment including AI Governance Policies, Regulatory Compliance, and AI Risk Management & Auditing.
  • The checklist focuses on verifying alignment with standards like ISO 42001, NIST AI RMF, and GDPR, as well as assessing data processing documentation and model transparency.
  • Evaluation of human intervention mechanisms in AI systems for high-risk applications.
  • Assessment of AI models based on fairness, accountability, and transparency principles.
  • Verification of AI decision-making processes and adherence to ethical guidelines, including human oversight mechanisms and appeal processes.
