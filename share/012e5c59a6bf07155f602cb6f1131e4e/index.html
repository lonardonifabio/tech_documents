AI Action Plan for Justice

Authors: Lord Timpson, Prime Minister, Lord Chancellor

This policy paper, published on July 31, 2025, outlines the Ministry of Justice’s strategy for integrating Artificial Intelligence (AI) across the entire justice system. The document, authored by the Prime Minister, Lord Chancellor, and Lord Timpson, represents a pioneering effort to leverage AI for improved public services. It begins with a commitment to creating a more productive and agile state, emphasizing the role of AI and technology in enhancing efficiency within public services. The core of the plan focuses on three key areas: strengthening foundational elements, embedding AI throughout the justice system, and investing in the people and partners involved. Specifically, the plan aims to simplify, accelerate, and personalize interactions between the public and the justice system. The document highlights the need for a transformation in the public’s experience, driven by AI’s ability to adapt and respond to individual needs. The plan’s success hinges on a collaborative approach, involving strategic investments in human capital and partnerships. The policy paper’s publication signifies a significant step towards modernizing the justice system and aligning it with the demands of the 21st century. The document’s accessibility through the national archives website underscores the government’s commitment to transparency and public engagement. The plan’s ultimate goal is to create a justice system that is not only more efficient but also more responsive and user-friendly, ultimately improving outcomes for all citizens. The inclusion of the Open Government Licence indicates a commitment to open access and collaboration.

Key Concepts:

  • The government is committed to using AI to improve public services.
  • The AI Action Plan for Justice aims to transform the public’s experience with the justice system.
  • AI will make interactions with the justice system simpler, faster, and more tailored.
  • A pro-innovation and pro-safety roadmap for AI across government.
  • The use of AI as an umbrella term encompassing various tools.
  • The need to strengthen foundations for AI-driven innovation in public services.
  • Collaboration between specialist teams to explore AI applications for public good.
  • Dragons’ Den-style Reverse Pitch event for SME solutions.
  • Reducing the Learning Curve: Streamlining training for operational staff.
  • AI-driven digital learning platform for government services.
