AI Ecosystem Brief 2025 Edition Greece

Authors: Sean Musch, Michael Borrelli, Charles Kerrigan, Prof. Dr. Fotios Fitsilis, Christos Makris, Yannis Mastrogeorgiou, Ester Martinez, Professor Dr. Phoebe Koundouri, Giannis Tolios, Ani Filipova, Stella Tsitsoula, Yiannis (JMK) Kalogerakis, Georgios Petas

This document, the "AI Ecosystem Brief 2025 Edition Greece," published by AI & Partners in September 2025, outlines the organization’s mission to defend and extend the digital rights of users globally, particularly those at risk due to the development and deployment of artificial intelligence. The report emphasizes a proactive approach, combining technical support, policy engagement, global advocacy, grassroots professional services, regulatory interventions, and participation in industry groups like AI Commons. AI & Partners’ core belief is that AI should be a force for unlocking potential rather than causing harm, reflecting concerns about the potential negative consequences of uncontrolled AI development. The organization’s strategy involves mitigating risks through comprehensive oversight and promoting responsible AI practices. The report was prepared by Sean Musch and Michael Borrelli, and details the organization’s commitment to safeguarding user rights within the evolving landscape of artificial intelligence. AI & Partners actively engages in regulatory interventions and industry collaborations to ensure ethical and responsible AI development. The document highlights the importance of maintaining control and fostering trust in AI systems, addressing the potential for misuse and the need for robust safeguards. It’s a publication by AI & Partners and provides contact information for Michael Borrelli, the Director, at m.borrelli@ai-and-partners.com. The organization’s work is focused on navigating the complexities of the EU AI Act and promoting a future where AI benefits humanity.

Key Concepts:

  • AI should unlock potential, not cause harm.
  • Protecting digital rights of users at risk.
  • Importance of responsible AI development and deployment.
  • Implementation of the EU AI Act in Greece
  • Enforcement of prohibited AI practices
  • National adoption of AI regulations
  • Algorithmic impact assessments for public entities
  • Data protection and algorithmic impact assessments for AI systems
  • Strategic AI Deployment: Greece is leveraging AI to boost productivity and innovation across various sectors.
  • AI Democratization: Focus on making AI accessible and beneficial to all, emphasizing design, development, and implementation.
