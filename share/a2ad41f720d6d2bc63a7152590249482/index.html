AI Regulatory Sandboxes Best Practices for AI Stakeholders EU AI Act August 2025

Authors: Sean Musch, Michael Borrelli, Charles Kerrigan, Michael Godfriaux, Darius Grigaliunas

This document, published by AI & Partners, outlines best practices for AI stakeholders concerning the EU AI Act, scheduled for implementation in August 2025. The report emphasizes the importance of responsible AI development and deployment to mitigate risks associated with uncontrolled AI systems. AI & Partners’ mission is to safeguard digital rights for users globally, advocating for responsible AI practices through a multi-faceted approach including technical support, policy engagement, advocacy, and participation in industry groups like AI Commons. The organization aims to help clients and their organizations build and manage AI systems that are trustworthy and sustainable, addressing the potential for confusion, risk, and reputational damage stemming from unknown AI adoption. The core focus is on enabling safe AI usage, providing tools for identification, monitoring, and governance of AI systems. The report highlights the need for proactive measures to ensure AI benefits society without causing harm. It’s a publication by AI & Partners, a company dedicated to defending digital rights and promoting responsible AI development.
