AI & Human Behaviour

Authors: Behavioural Insights Ltd

This document, "AI & Human Behaviour," published by Behavioural Insights Ltd in 2025, explores the critical intersection of artificial intelligence and human behavior. It argues that the rapid growth and adoption of generative AI, projected to reach $644 billion in 2025, are being driven by economic and technological forces without sufficient consideration for how humans actually respond and interact with these new technologies. The report highlights the widespread use of Large Language Models (LLMs) like ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Grok, Deepseek, and Copilot, with nearly half of US adults having utilized them. The core message is that the successful implementation of AI hinges on a deep understanding of human cognition and behavior. It posits that organizations will achieve greater success by strategically aligning human and AI agents, recognizing that the potential of AI is fundamentally limited without this understanding. The document emphasizes the need to address biases within AI systems, referencing an experiment on reducing biases detailed in the "Align" section. It acknowledges contributions from numerous individuals and teams involved in the research and development, including Serene Koh, Fendi Tsim, David Halpern, Tony Curzon Price, Marina Nenadic, Robert Harris, and a wider group involved in the experiment on reducing biases. The report’s focus is on the practical implications of integrating AI into business operations, advocating for a human-centric approach to maximize the benefits of AI technology. The document’s structure is divided into four key themes: Augment, Adopt, Align, and Adapt, reflecting a holistic strategy for navigating the evolving relationship between AI and human decision-making. The document’s ultimate goal is to provide insights and guidance for organizations seeking to leverage AI effectively while mitigating potential risks and maximizing positive outcomes.

Key Concepts:

  • The rise of generative AI has triggered an explosion of attention, spending, and organisational change.
  • Organizations will reap greater rewards if they know the best way to get humans and AI agents working together.
  • The promise of AI can only be fulfilled by understanding how and why people think and act the way they do.
  • Biases are strengthened through a feedback loop between AI and user.
  • AI models can create ‘chat chambers’ reinforcing existing biases.
  • Fine-tuning AI models to align with user’s long-term well-being is a key approach to improving human-AI alignment.
  • The dual-process framework is being used to improve AI system objectives.
  • Human-level intelligence involves transitioning between fast, intuitive and slow, deliberate reasoning processes.
  • Internalizing slow-thinking capabilities through techniques like Reinforcement Learning allows for step-by-step reasoning chains.
  • Fallible Triggers: Humans make mistakes, highlighting the need for robust systems.
