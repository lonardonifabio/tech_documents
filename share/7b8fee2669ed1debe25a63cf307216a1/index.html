Guidelines on Scope of Obligations for GPAI Models

Authors: European Commission

This document outlines the European Commission's guidelines regarding the scope of obligations associated with General Purpose AI (GPAI) models. Released in August 2025, the guidelines aim to establish a framework for managing the risks and responsibilities related to the development and deployment of these models. The core focus is on 'general-purpose AI models,' defined as those capable of performing a wide range of tasks. The document meticulously details the criteria for classifying a model as general-purpose, emphasizing the importance of understanding the model's lifecycle – from development to deployment and ongoing monitoring. A key element is the identification of models posing 'systemic risk,' which necessitates specific notification procedures. The guidelines provide a classification system for determining systemic risk, and mandates notification requirements for models identified as such. The document's purpose is to promote responsible AI development and deployment within the European Union, addressing potential harms and ensuring accountability. It’s a foundational document for understanding the evolving regulatory landscape surrounding GPAI models, and is likely to be updated as the technology and its associated risks continue to develop. The document’s structure is designed to be a practical guide, offering clarity on key concepts and procedures. It’s a critical resource for developers, deployers, and regulators alike, providing a starting point for navigating the complex considerations surrounding GPAI models. The document's release in August 2025 suggests a proactive approach by the European Commission to address the potential challenges posed by rapidly advancing AI technology. The focus on systemic risk highlights a concern about the potential for widespread disruption or harm if these models are not carefully managed.
