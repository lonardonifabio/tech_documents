ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE Federal Efforts Guided by Requirements and Advisory Groups

Authors: United States Government Accountability Office

This report, issued by the United States Government Accountability Office (GAO), examines federal efforts related to Artificial Intelligence (AI) as of July 2025. The study identifies and analyzes various requirements imposed on federal agencies through legislation, executive orders, and guidance. A key finding is the identification of 94 AI-related requirements, many of which have government-wide implications. For example, the General Services Administration is mandated to establish an AI Center of Excellence, impacting the entire federal government. The report highlights the role of ten executive branch oversight and advisory groups, including the National AI Advisory Committee and the National AI Initiative Office, in overseeing and implementing AI strategies within the government. The GAO’s investigation was undertaken to assess the potential of AI to enhance government operations. The document provides a snapshot of the evolving landscape of AI adoption and governance within the U.S. federal government, emphasizing the importance of aligning agency activities with established requirements and the guidance provided by key advisory bodies. The report’s findings underscore the need for continued monitoring and adaptation as AI technology continues to advance and its applications within the government expand. The analysis focuses on the current state of AI implementation, offering insights for policymakers and agency leaders navigating this rapidly changing technological domain. The report’s conclusions contribute to a broader understanding of how the federal government is approaching the challenges and opportunities presented by AI.

Key Concepts:

  • Federal efforts to implement and oversee artificial intelligence.
  • Government-wide requirements for AI adoption across agencies.
  • Role of advisory groups in AI governance within the federal government.

  • AI is a rapidly growing transformative technology.
  • AI has applications in various technologies like video games and facial recognition.
  • AI oversight within the federal government is not solely defined by government-wide requirements.
