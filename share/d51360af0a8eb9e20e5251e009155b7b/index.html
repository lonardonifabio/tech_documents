AI in GovTech: what works, what fails, and why

Authors: Elena Sügis, Grace Smith, Bobby Chandan, Andres Raieste

This report, authored by Nortal experts, delves into the practical application of Artificial Intelligence (AI) within the government sector – specifically focusing on GovTech. It addresses the prevalent issue of the AI market being saturated with unfulfilled promises, arguing that the true solution lies in developing AI solutions tailored to the unique needs of public sector organizations. The document outlines key strategies for successful AI implementation, starting with sector-driven capability building and progressing to AI-augmented government services and AI-ready digital public infrastructure. A core focus is on evaluating AI projects effectively, identifying the critical factors that determine whether a project should proceed, pause, or be abandoned, particularly during the Proof of Concept (PoC) phase. The report highlights common reasons for PoC stagnation, including challenges with citizen adoption, and emphasizes the importance of understanding citizen behavior when deploying AI solutions. Furthermore, it tackles crucial considerations such as expectations and Return on Investment (ROI), budget constraints, data-related hurdles, and legal restrictions. The analysis draws upon insights from successful AI deployments within the public sector, providing a framework for governments to navigate the complexities of integrating AI. The report underscores the need for governments to proactively adapt to the rapid advancements in AI technology, recognizing that a strategic and needs-driven approach is essential for maximizing the benefits of AI while mitigating potential risks. It concludes with key questions to ask when considering AI projects, reinforcing the importance of aligning AI initiatives with clear objectives and realistic expectations. The document serves as a valuable guide for government agencies seeking to leverage AI to improve public services and operational efficiency.

Key Concepts:

  • The report focuses on understanding what works and what fails regarding AI implementation within government.
  • Successful AI deployment relies heavily on sector-driven capability building and aligning AI tools with specific public sector needs.
  • Many AI projects stall during the Proof of Concept (PoC) phase due to factors like citizen adoption challenges, budget constraints, and data-related restrictions.
  • AI projects often fail due to risks like hallucinations and misinformation.
  • Successful AI projects require strategic alignment and clear leadership.
  • Lack of dedicated leadership and ownership is a major obstacle to scaling AI projects beyond the PoC phase.
