Artificial intelligence is reshaping health systems: state of readiness across the WHO European Region

Authors: World Health Organization

This report provides the first comprehensive assessment of artificial intelligence (AI) integration within health systems across the entire WHO European Region. Based on data collected from the 2024-2025 survey on AI for healthcare, the study examines a wide range of critical factors impacting AI adoption. Specifically, it delves into national strategies for AI implementation, analyzing the diverse governance models employed by participating countries. The report meticulously investigates the existing legal and ethical frameworks surrounding AI in healthcare, recognizing the complexities involved in ensuring responsible innovation. Furthermore, it assesses the preparedness of the healthcare workforce to utilize and manage AI technologies, alongside critical considerations of data governance and the importance of robust stakeholder engagement. A key focus is on understanding the evolving roles of the private sector within the healthcare AI landscape. The research identifies emerging trends, highlights existing gaps in implementation, and documents best practices to guide policymakers in developing coherent, ethical, and patient-centered strategies for AI in healthcare. The analysis draws upon insights from 50 Member States of the WHO European Region, offering a nuanced understanding of the diverse approaches being taken. The report’s findings are intended to inform policy decisions and promote a strategic, responsible, and ultimately beneficial integration of AI into healthcare, maximizing its potential to improve patient outcomes and the efficiency of health systems. The document emphasizes the need for a balanced approach, acknowledging both the significant opportunities presented by AI and the potential challenges that must be addressed proactively. The research contributes to a growing body of knowledge on the application of AI in healthcare, offering valuable insights for organizations and governments seeking to leverage this transformative technology effectively. The report’s conclusions underscore the importance of ongoing monitoring, evaluation, and adaptation as AI continues to evolve and its impact on healthcare becomes more pronounced.

Key Concepts:

  • Artificial intelligence is transforming health systems, reshaping how care is planned, delivered and governed.
  • The report assesses AI integration across the WHO European Region, examining national strategies and governance models.
  • The report explores how countries are navigating opportunities and challenges related to AI in healthcare, highlighting emerging trends and gaps.
  • AI is rapidly reshaping health systems and influencing healthcare planning, delivery, and governance.
  • The report provides a region-wide assessment of Member States' policies and adoption of AI initiatives.
  • The work aligns with the WHO European Region's digital health action plan and aims to guide investments and foster collaboration in ethical AI development.
  • AI initiatives require strategic planning and operational investment to maximize potential.
  • Prioritization of AI applications varies across Member States, with patient care and health outcomes being key drivers.
  • AI tools, such as AI-assisted diagnostics and chatbots, can improve clinician workloads and patient engagement.
