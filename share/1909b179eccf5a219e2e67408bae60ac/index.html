Agentic AI Advantage: Unlocking Next-Level Value

Authors: KPMG

This KPMG whitepaper explores the rapidly evolving landscape of agentic AI, positioning it as a significant step-change beyond the current generative AI wave. It argues that AI agents – systems capable of independent goal pursuit and operation across complex workflows – represent a transformative shift impacting how work is done, decisions are made, and value is delivered. The document emphasizes the urgency of preparation, noting that this shift is occurring much faster than the evolution of cloud computing, demanding immediate action from leading organizations. KPMG’s insights are derived from direct client engagements and internal work, highlighting the need for a leadership-driven approach. Successful implementation requires close alignment between business and technology leaders, a shared vision, coordinated execution, and a strategic plan for embedding intelligence at scale. Crucially, the paper stresses the importance of preparing the workforce through targeted training to adapt to the demands of this new AI paradigm. The document underscores that this isn't merely a technological transition but a fundamental shift in leadership and organizational strategy, urging proactive investment and positioning to capitalize on the potential of AI agents. The core message is that organizations that fail to prepare will be left behind as agentic AI reshapes the future of work and value creation. The document advocates for a proactive, strategic approach to adopting and implementing agentic AI, recognizing its potential to dramatically improve efficiency, decision-making, and overall business performance.
