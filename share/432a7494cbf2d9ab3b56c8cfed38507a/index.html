Guidance for AI Adoption: Foundations

Authors: industry.gov.au/NAIC

This document, "Guidance for AI Adoption: Foundations," released in October 2025, provides six essential practices for responsible AI governance and adoption, representing an update to Australia’s Voluntary AI Safety Standard (VAISS) launched in 2024. The guidance is based on national and international ethics principles and aims to assist organizations, particularly those in the early stages of adopting AI, in establishing robust AI governance frameworks. A key change in this version is a consolidation of previously defined guardrails into these six core practices, streamlining the guidance for clarity and accessibility. The update specifically expands the intended audience to include developers alongside deployers, recognizing the increasingly important role of software engineers in AI development. The document acknowledges the significant opportunities presented by AI for Australian businesses, noting its transformative impact across various sectors. It highlights the growing adoption of AI within Australian organizations, particularly among software engineers utilizing AI for coding assistance. Furthermore, the document distinguishes between two versions of the guidance: "Foundations," which outlines the essential practices, and "Implementation practices," which offers detailed, step-by-step instructions for implementation. This tiered approach allows organizations to begin with a foundational understanding and then progress to more granular guidance as their AI initiatives mature. The document’s purpose is to promote responsible AI development and deployment within the Australian context, aligning with broader international ethical considerations. The update reflects a commitment to ongoing refinement of AI safety standards and best practices.

Key Concepts:

  • The Guidance for AI Adoption sets out 6 essential practices for responsible AI governance and adoption.
  • Australia’s Voluntary AI Safety Standard (VAISS) was launched in 2024 and updated in October 2025.
  • AI brings enormous opportunity and potential to Australian business and is reshaping how organisations operate.
  • Clearly communicating AI use to stakeholders is standard practice.
  • Transparency across the AI supply chain is crucial for understanding system components and limitations.
  • Testing and monitoring AI systems is necessary due to potential changes in behavior and predictability.
