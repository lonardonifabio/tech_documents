2025 Responsible AI Transparency Report

Authors: Unknown

This 2025 Responsible AI Transparency Report details the company's approach to developing and deploying generative AI systems responsibly. Released in May 2024, the report represents the second annual publication and reflects feedback received from stakeholders globally. The core focus is on building trust in AI technologies through a multi-faceted strategy encompassing governance, risk assessment, mitigation, and customer support. Key areas covered include establishing policies, people, and processes for responsible AI development (Govern), identifying and mapping potential risks (Map), measuring and assessing those risks alongside mitigation strategies (Measure), and actively managing identified AI risks (Manage). Furthermore, the report outlines the company’s approach to decision-making regarding the release of generative AI systems, specifically addressing deployment safety and sensitive uses. A significant component is the ‘Sensitive Uses and Emerging Technologies’ program, demonstrating a proactive stance on potential ethical concerns. The document emphasizes the company’s commitment to supporting customers in building AI responsibly, offering AI Customer Commitments and tooling designed to facilitate responsible deployment. Transparency is presented as a cornerstone, aiming to empower customers to develop and utilize AI effectively. The report also highlights investments in AI research and a dedication to advancing AI innovation through good governance. Crucially, it incorporates multistakeholder input, indicating a collaborative approach to responsible AI development. The document acknowledges the rapid AI adoption observed by organizations in the past year, suggesting a dynamic and evolving landscape that the company is actively monitoring and adapting to. The report’s release in 2025 indicates a forward-looking perspective, anticipating future challenges and opportunities within the field of responsible AI. The document’s structure and content clearly demonstrate a comprehensive strategy for navigating the complexities of AI development and deployment, prioritizing ethical considerations and stakeholder engagement.
