Talent for AI in Science Mutual Learning Exercise on National Policies on AI in Science

Authors: Benat BILBAO OSORIO, Stéphane VANKALCK, Annamaria ZONNO, Laura ROMAN, Daniela PETKOV

This document represents the third thematic report of a Mutual Learning Exercise focused on National Policies on AI in Science. It’s produced by the European Commission’s Directorate-General for Research and Innovation, specifically the ERA & Innovation Unit A.1, and is coordinated by the Horizon Europe PSF coordination team. The report details an ongoing effort to analyze and develop national strategies surrounding artificial intelligence within scientific contexts. The core of the exercise involves a collaborative learning approach, aiming to foster mutual understanding and alignment among participating nations. The document highlights the importance of strategic planning and policy development to effectively leverage AI's potential within scientific research. It’s a key output of the European Commission’s commitment to supporting innovation and scientific advancement through informed policy decisions. The report’s creation reflects a significant investment in research and development, aligning with broader European initiatives to promote technological leadership and address critical challenges. The document’s production involves multiple individuals within the European Commission, demonstrating a coordinated effort to gather intelligence and provide strategic insights. The inclusion of the Horizon Europe PSF coordination team underscores the document’s connection to the European Commission’s framework for research and innovation funding. The report’s finalization in April 2025 indicates its relevance to current policy discussions and future strategic planning. The European Commission’s reuse policy, outlined in Commission Decision 2011/833/EU, governs the accessibility and utilization of this publication, promoting transparency and wider dissemination of knowledge. The document serves as a valuable resource for policymakers, researchers, and stakeholders involved in shaping the future of AI in science, contributing to a more coordinated and effective approach to harnessing its transformative potential.
