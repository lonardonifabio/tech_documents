Interoperability in AI Safety Governance: Ethics, Regulations, and Standards

Authors: Yik Chan Chin, David A Raho, Hag-Min Kim, Chunli Bi, James Ong, Jingbo Huang, Serge Stinckwich

This policy report, produced by the United Nations University Institute in Macau, examines the critical issue of interoperability within AI safety governance frameworks. The report focuses on the intersection of ethics, regulations, and standards across several key nations – the United Kingdom, South Korea, China, and Singapore – highlighting the complexities of ensuring responsible AI development and deployment globally. It investigates how different regulatory approaches and ethical considerations can be harmonized to promote consistent safety standards and mitigate potential risks associated with artificial intelligence. The research team, comprised of experts from various institutions including Sheffield Hallam University, China Academy of Information and Communications Technology, Kyung Hee University, and Artificial Intelligence International Institute, explores the challenges and opportunities presented by varying national contexts. Specifically, the report delves into the need for standardized methodologies for risk assessment, transparency requirements, and accountability mechanisms within AI systems. It addresses the technical aspects of interoperability, considering how different AI systems and governance structures can effectively communicate and collaborate to achieve shared safety goals. Furthermore, the document analyzes the role of international cooperation and the potential for establishing global norms and best practices. The research considers the implications of differing legal frameworks and cultural values on AI governance, advocating for a flexible and adaptable approach that can evolve alongside technological advancements. The report’s findings contribute to the ongoing debate surrounding AI safety and offer valuable insights for policymakers, regulators, and industry stakeholders seeking to navigate the rapidly changing landscape of artificial intelligence. The project benefited from the contributions of several researchers and received funding from SenseTime, demonstrating the significant investment and attention being directed towards this crucial area. The document acknowledges the support of numerous individuals who provided valuable feedback and comments, further enriching the analysis and recommendations presented.
