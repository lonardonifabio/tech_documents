This document, titled ‘International Partnerships Evaluation Handbook,’ is a publication released in July 2024 by the Publications Office of the European Union. The handbook was developed by a task force comprised of staff from DG INTPA’s Unit D4 and its former Evaluation Support Service (ESS), operating under the direction of a management board from DG INTPA and FPI. It’s a crucial resource for those involved in designing, implementing, and evaluating these partnerships, ensuring accountability and maximizing their positive outcomes.
