Natural Language Processing (NLP)

Authors: Harsh Choudhary

This document provides a foundational overview of Natural Language Processing (NLP), a crucial branch of Artificial Intelligence focused on enabling machines to comprehend and manipulate human languages. NLP encompasses both Natural Language Understanding (NLU) and Natural Language Generation (NLG). NLU concentrates on interpreting the meaning embedded within text, while NLG focuses on creating human-like text based on processed data. The process of NLP typically involves a series of interconnected phases designed to analyze and interpret language, considering its structural and semantic elements. Several key libraries support NLP development, including NLTK (Natural Language Toolkit), spaCy, TextBlob, Transformers (by Hugging Face), and Gensim. NLTK, in particular, is highlighted as a widely adopted library, offering a comprehensive suite of linguistic resources and pre-trained models. These resources are valuable for both academic research endeavors and practical applications. The document underscores the significance of NLP in bridging the gap between human communication and machine understanding, paving the way for innovative applications across diverse domains. The core concepts presented establish a basic understanding of NLP's components and the tools available for its implementation. Further exploration would delve into the specific techniques and algorithms employed within each phase of NLP processing.

Key Concepts:

  • Natural Language Processing is a branch of AI focused on understanding and processing human languages.
  • NLP is categorized into Natural Language Understanding and Natural Language Generation.
  • NLP involves a series of phases to process and interpret language.
  • NLTK is a widely used Python library for NLP, providing linguistic resources and pre-trained models.
