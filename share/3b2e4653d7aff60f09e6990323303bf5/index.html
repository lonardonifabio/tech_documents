Partnering with Generative AI in the Finance Function

Authors: MIT Technology Review

This document explores the emerging role of generative AI within the finance function, specifically focusing on how CFOs are leveraging these technologies to optimize their operations and strategic focus. The core argument centers around CFOs experimenting with AI use cases to alleviate the burden of routine, time-consuming tasks, thereby freeing up capacity for high-value strategic work such as proactively advising the business on financial strategy. Large language models (LLMs) and generative AI tools are being utilized to support daily activities including generating quarterly reports, communicating with investors, and creating strategic summaries. The document highlights the potential for generative AI to transform the finance function by automating repetitive tasks, ultimately boosting productivity and process optimization. Despite challenges related to technology maturity and concerns around cybersecurity and 'hallucination' (potential inaccuracies in AI outputs), financial organizations are increasing their investment in generative AI. A key future trend identified is 'capability automation,' where complex tasks are broken down and combined across multiple steps, representing a more sophisticated application of generative AI within finance. The document implicitly acknowledges the ongoing geopolitical and financial uncertainty impacting businesses, further emphasizing the need for CFOs to adapt and leverage technology to maintain strategic advantage. The core message is that generative AI is not simply a tool for efficiency, but a catalyst for a fundamental shift in how finance functions operate.

Key Concepts:

  • CFOs experimenting with AI use cases to free up capacity.
  • Generative AI transforming the finance function and supporting strategic work.
  • Capability automation – combining tasks across multiple steps for increased efficiency.
  • Under-promise and over-deliver
  • AI adoption by CFOs
  • Unstructured data analysis using AI
  • Enterprise generative AI tools for financial analysis
  • LLM-based AI chatbots for financial data queries
  • Trust in AI accuracy for financial calculations
