Beyond ESG: From Checklists to Capabilities

Authors: Sven Smit, Kevin Russell, Michael Chui, Nick Leung, Michael Birshan, Clarisse Magnin, Francisco Garrido, Francisco Galtieri

This document, titled "Beyond ESG: From Checklists to Capabilities," is a publication produced by the McKinsey Global Institute, anticipated for release in September 2025. It outlines the institute's mission, which is to provide a robust, fact-based foundation for decision-making regarding critical economic and business challenges faced by global companies and policy leaders. Established in 1990, the McKinsey Global Institute leverages the extensive knowledge and expertise of the broader McKinsey organization, drawing upon regional, sectoral, and functional insights. The institute’s research is currently structured around five key themes: Productivity and prosperity, focusing on efficient asset utilization; Resources of the world, dedicated to sustainable building, powering, and feeding; Human potential, concentrating on maximizing human talent; Global connections, examining the impact of flows across various sectors; and Technologies and markets of the future, exploring emerging value and competitive landscapes. A core principle of the institute’s work is maintaining independence and relying on factual research, explicitly stating that no research is commissioned or funded by any business entity. The document emphasizes a shift from traditional "checklists" to a more capabilities-based approach, suggesting a move beyond simple compliance to a deeper understanding and application of relevant strategies. The publication’s intent is to provide a comprehensive resource for navigating complex global issues, underpinned by rigorous research and a commitment to objective analysis. The institute’s structure, with directors and partners solely responsible for editorial decisions, ensures a focused and strategic research agenda.
