AI Ecosystem Brief 2025 Edition Italy

Authors: Sean Musch, Michael Borrelli, Charles Kerrigan, Arvin Obnasca, Valeria Lazzaroli, Andrea Frosinini, Cecilia Lascialfari, Alessio Lidozzi, Fabio Lonardoni, Fabio Moioli, Mirko Minenza, Bruno Calabretta, Gregor Maric, Romy Liram, Najoua Belayane

This document, the "AI Ecosystem Brief 2025 Edition Italy," published by AI & Partners, provides an overview of the evolving AI landscape, particularly within Italy. The report’s core mission is to defend and extend the digital rights of users globally who are potentially at risk due to the development and deployment of artificial intelligence. AI & Partners operates through a multi-faceted approach, combining direct technical support, robust policy engagement, global advocacy, grassroots professional services, regulatory interventions, and participation in industry groups like AI Commons. The organization’s philosophy centers around the belief that AI should foster potential rather than causing harm, recognizing the risks associated with uncontrolled AI development. They aim to guide organizations in building AI systems that are trustworthy and ethically sound. The report highlights the importance of responsible AI practices and proactive engagement with regulatory frameworks. AI & Partners’ activities are driven by a commitment to safeguarding user rights and promoting a future where AI benefits humanity. The document emphasizes the need for a balanced approach, acknowledging both the potential dangers and the opportunities presented by AI. It’s a publication designed to inform and guide stakeholders navigating the complexities of the AI ecosystem, particularly within the Italian context. The organization’s approach is deeply rooted in ethical considerations and a dedication to responsible innovation. Contact information and a website URL are provided for further engagement.

Key Concepts:

  • AI & Partners defends and extends the digital rights of users at risk around the world.
  • AI should unlock potential — not cause harm.
  • Building AI you can trust
  • Alignment with European standards through the EU AI Act definition.
  • Incorporation of ethical principles (transparency, proportionality, fairness, gender equality, sustainability) into AI governance.
  • Multi-layered governance framework involving existing laws and sector-specific guidelines.
  • Neuro-symbolic AI: Combining symbolic reasoning with neural networks.
  • Public-private partnership: Collaboration between public and private sectors to foster AI innovation.
  • AI-driven entrepreneurship: Utilizing AI to drive business growth and innovation.
  • Dual appointments: Combining academic research with industry experience to develop AI talent.
